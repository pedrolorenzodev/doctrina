# «Pasajes» page of a tradition

Proposal (2026-10-29, waiting for Pedro's pick): https://claude.ai/artifact/LWsmP5mVDVgECnc1z1u4YF, archived in `docs/research/proposals/passages/index.html` (second round, 2026-10-29; the first round — a list per passage, an index and detail, a translation bar — was discarded by Pedro as «un estilo de UI/UX viejo, estático y aburrido»). Now: A Foco (one big passage, a vertical ruler, the tradition's reading in the margin with its quoted words lighting up in the verse, changed words flash when the translation changes), B Mesa de lectura (columns of translations or parallels, shared words, show differences), C Documento (all passages continuous, margin readings, translation strip, right-edge ruler). Pedro liked the orientation of the old B «Índice y pasaje» a little.

Intent: the page behind the tree's «Pasajes» row (under each tradition) and the button «Ver los N pasajes» of 03's «La Escritura que usa». Priority (Pedro, 2026-10-29): the first proposal Artifact, ahead of «Fuentes».

**Pedro, verbatim:** «Si yo soy un usuario que no conoce porque la iglesia catolica piensa que la biblia SI enseña en la regeneracion bautismal, principalmente lo que me interesa es ver todos los versiculos que la tradicion CITA y USA a favor, y como los entiende (cuando se tenga el dato) e interpreta. Una pagina dedicada solo a esto, de manera grande e informativa, ES SUMAMENTE UTIL.» «Mi objetivo es que en esa pagina yo pueda ver, POR CADA VERSICULO/S, EL VERSICULO EXACTAMENTE DE MANERA COMPLETO, TODOS LOS VERSICULOS O VERSICULOS PARALELOS (que son los mismos pero en distintos evangelios en casos asi), Y PUDIENDO CAMBIAR AHI MISMO EN EL MOMENTO LA TRADUCCION A TODAS LAS DISPONIBLES, Y PUDIENDO LEER COMO LOS LEE E INTERPRETA LA TRADICION EN CUESTION. Y QUE PUEDA IR A LEER EL VERSICULO A LA PAGINA DEL LECTOR DE LA BIBLIA, QUE ME LLEVE A ESE VERSICULO ESPECIFICO PARA LEER TODO EL CONTEXTO Y DEMAS.»

**What the page must do (acceptance for the proposal):**
- One entry per cited passage or group of passages, with the whole text of every verse (never an excerpt) and its parallels in other books when there are any.
- A translation switch on the spot, over every edition available (RV1909, BSB, KJV, WEB, Douay-Rheims; the Catholic deuterocanon only where the edition has it).
- How the tradition reads and interprets each passage, in its own words and with its edition; where that data does not exist yet the entry says nothing about it (no placeholders, no «pending»).
- A link per entry to the reader at that verse, to read the whole context.
- Clean and intuitive within the design system; mobile as well.

**Out of scope:** building it before a proposal Artifact is picked; the interpretation data (comes with the dossiers, which hold «La Escritura que usa»).
**Verify:** proposal measured against `DESIGN.md`, then `docs/playbooks/build-approved-design.md`.

## Decision of 2026-10-29 (later): variant A «Foco» is the base, plus the apologists layer

Pedro chose A (B and C discarded) and wants every passage to carry: (1) «Lo que comentan/enseñan los documentos oficiales de [tradición]» (today's reading, new copy); (2) under it, «La defensa apologista [tradición]», clearly marked as popular apologists and not official documents; (3) below, an exhaustive commentary in the apologists' own words, how the tradition reads the passage and defends that reading. His words and the agent's framing: DECISIONS 2026-10-29 «Pasajes: variant A…». Data that exists: `docs/research/doctrines/apologetic-answers.md` (Baptism DO-5: Acts 2:38, 1 Pet 3:21, Mark 16:16, with persons, videos, minutes and quotes `[verified: captions]`); everything else is research to do, only when Pedro says so. A layer with no data does not show.

**Fourth round (2026-10-29):** Pedro liked C's width (the verse and the full argument each take a very wide block, easier to read) but not that, once expanded, the full apologetic argument ends far from the verse, behind the two mini-blocks. Added D «El argumento sube» (a bar right under the verse opens the argument in place and the two columns drop below it) and E «Una capa a ancho completo» (a selector under the verse; the chosen layer takes the whole width, the apologists layer shows the intro and the argument already open). A, B and C stay for comparison; Pedro has not chosen a variant.

**Fifth round (2026-10-29):** Pedro chose E as the structure (room for each block, «respiración») but found its finish «AI-slop»: no wall texture, fonts and spacing not convincing. Rebuilt as F «Edición» (one 720 px column, Newsreader verse with hanging numerals, quote with its source below) and G «Pliego» (reader-face verse, quote and source in two columns); E stays beside them. What was wrong in E, from the repo's own rules: no `texture-page`; metadata joined with «·» and arrows glued to links (both in DESIGN «Don'ts»); boxed controls (now the flat tradition-switcher tabs and title tabs with a sliding line); type outside the scale (now `defn`, `quote`, `punch`, `prose`); loose spacing (now 36 / 64 / 72 / 52 px); quotes in the site's «cita + fuente» block with the date mark and the external-link mark; navigation with the «Anterior / Siguiente» boxes. Reference for measure and leading: Butterick, *Practical Typography*, summary of key rules (45–90 characters, 120–145 %). A–D discarded.

- The 19 passages are `page.scripture` of `src/content/pages/baptism/catholic.es.ts` (`refs` + `text`, with `{c:…}` citations and `{v:…}` verses); the verse texts come from the Bible corpus through `get-bible` for every edition; the readings stay as written. - **Existing apologist data** (all `[verified: captions]` in `docs/research/doctrines/apologetic-answers.md`, Baptism DO-5, unreviewed by Pedro): Mark 16:16 inside the «Mateo 28:19–20 · Marcos 16:15–16» passage (Heschmeyer, Otálora, Horn), Acts 2:38 (Broussard ×2, Heschmeyer), 1 Peter 3:21 (Heschmeyer, Horn): 8 quotes; the proposal's `source/apol.py` has them structured with the Spanish rendering of the English quotes (labelled AI-assisted). - **Parallels** are not in the data: the proposal shows Matthew 3:13–17 ↔ Mark 1:9–11, Luke 3:21–22 and Mark 10:14 ↔ Matthew 19:14, Luke 18:16 (the usual synoptic parallels); they must be sourced before launch.
- **Rules that bind the data:** DECISIONS 2026-10-29 «Pasajes: variant A…» (criteria: at least 3 independent apologists, not contested within the tradition, applying what it teaches officially; brief quotes with link for modern works; the agent locates and quotes, never writes the defence; every tradition that turns on needs the same layer). ### Research patterns that worked (for the next agent)

- **Design research:** read `docs/DESIGN.md` (type scale, patterns, «Don'ts»), `docs/DECISIONS.md` (AI-slop list) and `docs/research/analogs.md` first; then WebSearch in standard mode for comparators and only cite what the results confirm. Found: Logos *Text Comparison* (base text, «Show Differences», stacks when narrow), STEPBible display modes (columns, interleaved, word-level differences only inside one language), SwordSearcher's commentary panel (tabs, coloured when a commentary has an entry for the verse), Logos *Parallel Gospel Reader*, Throckmorton's *Gospel Parallels*. Not confirmed: Blue Letter Bible, Bible Hub, any tool with commentaries of several traditions side by side. Measure and leading: Butterick, *Practical Typography*, summary of key rules (45–90 characters, 120–145 %).
- **Content research:** reuse `docs/research/doctrines/apologetic-answers.md` and `docs/playbooks/debate-objections.md` (criteria, tags, minute and speaker per quote); quote the speaker's exact words from captions, keep the original language visible, and exclude any figure the research left `[unverified]`.
