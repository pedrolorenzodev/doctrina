Pedro, verbatim (2026-10-28 morning): «Al llegar a los últimos párrafos del capítulo, calcular si el usuario estuvo [n] cantidad de segundos sobre los últimos párrafos, si lo estuvo, que se muestre un pequeño botón ya sea flotando sobre el texto, o debajo del capítulo como ahora, al pie (no sé si centrado o no, no sé qué estilo), para que el usuario marque el capítulo como leído. Algo del estilo me imagino. Creá un artifact que UTILICE NUESTRO DESIGN SYSTEM ACTUAL, para proponer estilos y posiciones de este botón, y animación de entrada y salida smooth y sutil.»
«En cuanto al botón para marcar capítulo como leído, una idea que podemos probar como variante sea que sea simplemente como un botón circular o similar, pequeño con un check o algo así, que al hacer hover pasa con una transición smooth a decir algo como LEÍDO o algo así.»
«PERO, no implementes esto ahora» (the eleventh session saved it; the twelfth does the proposal).

How the eleventh session understood it, to confirm with Pedro in one line before building:
- **Nothing is ever marked automatically.** The only way a chapter becomes «leído» is the button (or the existing manual ways, none today). The tree mark and `doctrina-read-chapters` stay as they are.
- **Trigger:** the reader has the last paragraphs of the chapter on screen (the same «verse fully visible» test as `read-marker.tsx`, 90 % in view below the strip) and has been over them for `[n]` seconds with real activity (tab visible and focused, a scroll/wheel/touch/pointer/key event in the last 5 s: the rule that exists in `src/features/reader/read-marker.tsx`, `src/lib/reading-pace.ts`). Pedro did not give `[n]`; propose a default (about 5 to 8 s) and show it in the demo with a speed switch like the previous artifact.
- **Leaving without pressing:** when the reader scrolls on into the next chapter (or back up away from the last paragraphs) the button fades out smoothly (200 ms `ease-standard`, the system's fade-out; instant under reduced motion) and **does not come back until the conditions are met again** (a new dwell, in that chapter or another).
- **Variant to test:** a small round button with a check; on hover it transitions smoothly into a pill that reads «Leído» (or similar); pressed state, focus ring, 44 px target only under `pointer-coarse`.
- **Positions to show (2 to 4 options, with the one that exists today beside them):** (a) at the foot of the chapter above the separator hairline (today's «offer», left aligned in the 96 px gap, no layout shift), (b) the same centred, (c) floating over the text near the bottom edge of the reading column (like the undo bar, which sits centred at the bottom), (d) floating at the right of the column beside the last paragraph. Entrance and exit animations for each (fade + 4–6 px rise, 200 ms; the circle growing into the pill on hover).
- **After pressing:** the tree mark appears and the existing undo bar says «Capítulo marcado como leído.» with «Deshacer» (kit 180 built this; keep it).
- **Agent's objection to record, not to repeat:** a dwell over the last paragraphs alone can be reached by jumping to the end and waiting (option A of the previous artifact). Because nothing is marked without a tap, the cost of that is only an offer that appears; so the objection is weak and Pedro's design is fine. Fathers' works have no «leído» yet; the same button would serve them (ask).
