## What the site does today (read from the code)
One recipe, many files: `bg-accent-weak` + `shadow-current` (inset 2 px accent at the left) for rows (`row-current` in `globals.css`, `sidebar.tsx`, `translation-rows.tsx`, `panel-picker.tsx`, `book-picker.tsx`); `bg-accent-weak` + `shadow-current-underline` (inset 2 px accent at the bottom) for tabs (`sliding-tabs.tsx` flat, `tradition-switcher.tsx`); `bg-accent-weak` sliding pad for segments (`segmented-filter.tsx`, `scope-control.tsx`, `text-options.tsx`, `parallel-menu.tsx`, `text-settings.tsx`); pressed chips with accent border and fill; chapter grid circle (`chapter-grid.tsx`); heading tabs with a 2 px accent underline (`sliding-tabs.tsx` heading). Colour dots use a ring (`--shadow-swatch-current`).

## Research (2026-10-30)
- Anti-pattern catalogs of AI UI tells name the one-sided thick coloured border (the «side-stripe» / left-border accent) as the most recognizable tell [search: Impeccable «The visible tells of AI design», Hallmark anti-patterns]; none of them names a tinted selected background, so that half of the complaint is not confirmed.
- Atlassian AUI separates «current» (the page on screen; a non-link with an accessible label) from «selected»; one active item at a time in a whole sidebar; `aria-current="page"` [search].
- Segmented-control guidance in third-party systems: three to five options, one always selected, selected state visible without colour [search]. Apple HIG was not reached.
- Six documentation sidebars captured today in a normal Chrome (`docs/research/selectors-refs/`): Stripe marks the current page with accent-coloured medium-weight text and no fill; Vercel docs and Claude docs with a neutral grey filled row; MDN with a tinted fill plus a left bar (the recipe Doctrina uses); Wikipedia's table of contents has no selected mark.
