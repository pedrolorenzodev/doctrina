## Module (tanda 1)

Folder `src/content/bible-reference/` (pure TypeScript, no UI, no route):

- `names.ts`: book names and abbreviations per language (`es`, `en`, or `both`), key = letters and digits only, accents removed, roman and Spanish ordinals turned into digits.
- `parse.ts`: `parseReferences(text)`: split by `;`, then by `,` (an item with letters starts a new passage, an item of digits continues the last one); one part is `book [locator]`.
- `resolve.ts`: `resolveReferences(parts, versesPerChapter)`: checks chapters and verses against the translation's table; whole book = chapter 1 (kind `book`); duplicates flagged, overlaps kept.
- `language.ts`: `detectQueryLanguage(parts, fallback?)` and `translationForLanguage(language)` (ids from `src/content/bible/translation-defaults.ts`, also used by `default-translation.ts`).
- `route.ts`: `routeFor(passages, language)`, `passageToken`, `parseRouteTokens`.
- `versification.ts`: generated by `tools/build-bible-versification.mjs` from `data/corpus/bibles` (verses per chapter per book, and verse numbers absent below the chapter's highest, `gaps`).
- Tools: `tools/check-bible-reference.mjs` (in `npm run lint`; 75 parse, 15 language, 13 route, 10 token round-trip, 3 must-fail fixtures; checks the table against the corpus when the corpus exists), `npm run bible-ref -- "juan 6:53; gal 1"` (dev output: parsed, resolved, route).

## Grammar (what the parser accepts)

Part = `book [locator]`. Book: any name or abbreviation in the table, Spanish or English, with or without accents and spaces (`1Cor`, `1 Co`, `I Cor`, `1 Corintios`, `Primera de Corintios`, `Jn`, `Jo` = Job). Locator (after separators `:`, `.`, spaces; `–` = `-`):

| Form | Example | Result |
|---|---|---|
| nothing | `Juan` | whole book, chapter 1 (`book`) |
| chapter | `Juan 6` | chapter (`chapters`) |
| chapter range | `Juan 3-5` | chapters 3, 4, 5 |
| verse | `Juan 6:53` | verse |
| verse range | `Juan 6:26-53` | verses |
| verse list | `Juan 6:53,55`, `Romanos 8:28,31-39` | several spans in one passage |
| cross-chapter | `Gen 1:1-2:3`, `Gen 1:1-Gen 2:3`, `Juan 6:26-7:2` | one span over two chapters |
| open end | `Juan 6:53-` | verse 53 to the end of the chapter |
| dot form | `jn.6.53`, `john6:53` | the same as the colon form |

Between parts: `;` (BibleGateway) and `,` before a new book (`Juan 6:53, Gal 1`, also BibleGateway). Empty parts and empty list items are ignored (BibleGateway ignores them too). Errors per part, never for the whole text: `unknown-book`, `bad-locator` (`Juan 6:`, `Juan 6:53:55`, `Juan 6:53-55-57`), `unsupported-item`, `cross-book` (`Juan 6:53-Gal 1:1`), then from the resolver `chapter-out-of-range`, `verse-out-of-range`, `reversed-range` (also `Juan 6:53-7`, which is read as verses 53 to 7 of chapter 6), `book-not-in-translation` (e.g. Tobit in RV1909).

Single-chapter books (Jude, Philemon, 2 and 3 John, Obadiah): a bare number `n` is chapter 1 when `n` = 1, and verse `n` of chapter 1 otherwise (`Jude 3` = `Jude 1:3`, `Jude 25` = `Jude 1:25`, `Jude 26` fails). This is what BibleGateway shows.

## Language and translation rule

- Each part's book name belongs to Spanish (`es`), English (`en`) or both. Parts with an unambiguous name vote; the majority decides (`detectQueryLanguage`). A tie or no vote uses the fallback (the site's current language) or `unknown`.
- Examples in the check: `juan 6:53` = es; `john 6:53` = en; `Job 1` alone = unknown; `Judas 3; Jude 3` = unknown; `Juan 6:53; Gal 1` = es (`Gal` is both: `Gál` is a Spanish abbreviation).
- Translation: es = `rv1909`, en = `bsb`, deuterocanon = `dra` (the `dra` default stays in the reader for deuterocanon books). BSB is not verified on BibleGateway (see above); the choice is the site's own.

## Routes (decision for the app)

- One verse, a range in one chapter, or a whole chapter: the reader URL of that chapter (`/es/biblia/juan/6`), with `#v-N` for a verse or the first verse of a range. `#v-N` is the existing convention (`src/features/reader/verse-arrival.tsx`, `arrivalHash`). The reader marks one verse, so a range marks its first verse: a gap for tanda 2 to decide.
- A whole book: chapter 1, flagged `wholeBook` (Pedro's rule; BibleGateway opens a keyword search instead).
- Several passages, a verse list, a chapter range or a cross-chapter range: the stacked view `/es/biblia/leer?p=…` (en: `/en/bible/read?p=…`). Tokens are OSIS-like with the repo's book ids in lower case: `john.6.53`, `gal.1`, `mal.1.11`, `john.3-5`, `john.6.26-7.2`, `john.6.53,55`. The repo's ids are the OSIS abbreviations (`Gen`, `1Cor`, `Ps`), so `jn` in the brief becomes `john`.
- The translation does not travel in the URL yet. Proposal: a `translation` parameter, decided with the stacked view.
- Route name `leer`/`read` is a proposal. It must not collide with a book slug (no Spanish or English book is named so).

## Versification

Source of truth: `src/content/bible-reference/versification.ts` (generated, committed). Counts from `data/corpus/bibles` (git-ignored). Checked against the corpus whenever the corpus exists; the table is used by the resolver in CI without the corpus.

- Totals: rv1909 31084 and kjv 31102 and dra 35811 match `data/sources/bibles.json`. BSB corpus has 31086 verses in text (manifest 31086); the table counts 31102 slots, because the corpus lacks 16 KJV verses (`gaps`: Matt 17:21, 18:11, 23:14; Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26, 15:28; Luke 17:36, 23:17; John 5:4; Acts 8:37, 15:34, 24:7, 28:29; Rom 16:24). web-c corpus has 35379 verses (manifest 35379); the table counts 35420 slots: 41 gaps, mostly Sirach and Esther 4:6, 9:5, 9:30; not checked whether they are real omissions or empty-text verses dropped by `build_bibles.py`.
- Psalms: DRA (Vulgate) differs from Psalm 2 on (DRA 2 = 13 verses vs 12; DRA 3 = 9 vs 8; DRA 22 has the 6 verses of Hebrew 23).
- Daniel: rv1909, kjv, bsb have 12 chapters (Dan 3 = 30 verses). DRA has 14 (3 = 100, 13 = 65, 14 = 42); web-c 3 = 97, 13 = 64, 14 = 42.
- Esther: rv1909, kjv, bsb 10 chapters (167 verses); DRA 16 chapters (the additions as 11-16); web-c 10 chapters with the additions inside (chapter 4 = 47, chapter 10 = 14).
- Job: rv1909 differs from kjv in chapters 35 (15 vs 16), 38 (38 vs 41) and 40 (19 vs 24); DRA differs in several. The mechanism is not checked against the printed RV1909: to confirm before any multi-translation view.
- Malachi, Joel, Song, Romans, 3 John, Jude: same chapter counts in every translation (Malachi 3 = 18, 4 = 6: the English numbering of Hebrew 3:19-24). BibleGateway accepts `Mal 3:19-4:6` (Hebrew numbering) and shows it as Malachi 4:1-6; we reject `Mal 3:19` (out of range). Deferred: Hebrew-numbering aliases (decision for Pedro).
- Deuterocanon (Tob, Jdt, Wis, Sir, Bar, 1-2 Macc): dra and web-c only.

Deferred: the resolver does not use `gaps` yet (a verse absent in the translation resolves but has no text); a verse-list view across translations; mixed translations in one query.

## Decisions taken by the agent (to confirm)

1. **Parser errors per part.** BibleGateway drops invalid parts silently when others are valid; we report them per part (the brief asks for it). The app can show them.
2. **Whole book = chapter 1**, flagged (Pedro's rule). BibleGateway shows keyword search for a bare book name (`John`, `Jude`).
3. **Version in the query is not parsed** (`John 6:53 KJV` gives no results on BibleGateway). Same here: the translation comes from the language, not from the text.
4. **Ordinals:** Arabic and Roman numerals (`1`, `I`, `II`), and Spanish ordinal words (`Primera de Corintios`). English ordinal words (`First John`) are rejected, as BibleGateway does.
5. **Comma:** between books it separates passages; before a number it is a verse list (BibleGateway does the same).
6. **Duplicates:** an exact duplicate is kept in the typed order and flagged `duplicate` (not routed). Overlaps are kept (BibleGateway shows them twice).
7. **Single-chapter books:** `n` = chapter 1 when 1, else verse `n` (BibleGateway).
8. **Open end** `Juan 6:53-` = to the end of the chapter (BibleGateway).
9. **Routes:** one verse marks it with `#v-N`; several passages go to the stacked view; a verse list goes to the stacked view too (the reader marks only one verse).
10. **Tokens:** repo ids in lower case (`john`, not `jn`); the verse list inside one passage uses commas.
11. **Language:** majority over the unambiguous book names; ties use the fallback.
12. **Translation defaults in one file** (`translation-defaults.ts`), used by `default-translation.ts` (no behaviour change).
13. **Spanish abbreviations** in `names.ts` come from memory (not checked against an external source): `Gn`, `Lv`, `Nm`, `Dt`, `Jos`, `Jue`, `Rt`, `1 S`, `Esd`, `Cnt`, `Lm`, `Jr`, `Os`, `Jl`, `Mt`, `Mr`, `Lc`, `Hch`, `Gl`, `Ef`, `Flp`, `Stg`, `Ap`, and others. The site's own names are the reliable part.
