Legend: CONFIRMED / WRONG / UNCONFIRMABLE. Section refs are to `baptist.md` (B) and `pentecostal.md` (P). Method: pages and PDFs fetched with curl (browser User-Agent), converted to text and grepped (not summarised by a model), so quoted strings are exact matches. archive.org full texts (McGlothlin 1911, Mullins 1908) are OCR; whitespace-normalised before matching. ag.org, foursquare.org (no `www`) and baptistpress.com HTML are Cloudflare-blocked to curl; where used, the Wayback copy or a `www.` variant is named.

### B-log 14 (B §10 open question, §11 row 14) NH "1833, Art. 14" locator in the pilot data
CONFIRMED, and sharper than the log states. `data/site/sources.extra.json` id `new-hampshire-1833` points to https://www.gracegems.org/new_hampshire_baptist_confes.htm. That page's own title is "The New Hampshire Baptist Confession of **1853**" (18 articles; "14. Of Baptism and the Lord's Supper"; "spelling has been updated to modern English"). Recommendation: relabel the edition as 1853 (Brown), keep "art. 14".
