| Ignatius, *Smyrnaeans*: where the sentence on the Eucharist stands | in the ANF printing the sentence «They abstain from the Eucharist and from prayer, because they confess not the Eucharist to be the flesh of our Saviour Jesus Christ, which suffered for our sins, and which the Father, of His goodness, raised up again» is in **chapter VII**, headed «Let us stand aloof from such heretics»; the text of chapter VI that precedes it contains, in its middle (the chapter opens «Let no man deceive himself»), the exhortation that no [high] place puff a man up and «consider those who are of a different opinion with respect to the grace of Christ» (the two recensions are printed in parallel in the OCR, so the words are interleaved) | *The Ante-Nicene Fathers* vol. I, Buffalo: The Christian Literature Publishing Company, 1885, archive.org `antenicenefather01buff`, *Epistle of Ignatius to the Smyrnaeans*, ch. VI–VII | `[verified: OCR of the scan]`. So this edition places the flesh-of-our-Saviour sentence in chapter VII, which agrees with the locator 7.1 the data file uses (`ew-flesh`, *Smyrn.* 7.1). The «6.2» that E12 asks about is the Lake/Lightfoot number of the «no regard for love … the widow, the orphan» sentence, which ANF prints in chapter VI (`queue-4-5-7-8.apply.md` item 8, not re-read here), so the 6.2-versus-7.1 question is settled for both sentences; the ANF does not print the modern section numbers inside a chapter. Which of the two printed recensions carries the flesh-of-our-Saviour sentence was not determined from the OCR (the column continues «…the Gospel, in which the passion [of Christ] has been revealed to us»): check a page image before the site says «shorter» or «longer». |
