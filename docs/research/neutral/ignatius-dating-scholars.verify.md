## Title format (section "Consistent Spanish dating titles")

Verdict: consistent in grammar and neutral in tone, but not ready. Neutral: it never ranks datings, names the dated object, and keeps Eusebius (an ancient witness) out of the scholar cards; no Church Father is attached to a modern tradition. Problems, in order of weight:

1. Wrong scholar-level attribution (Lake): "Lake · epoca de Trajano (98-117) - martirio" presents a survey sentence as Lake's own dating. Fix or drop (see Lake).
2. Pervo's object is unverified: the secondary source (Wikipedia, citing Pervo 2010, pp. 134-135) says "Pervo dated Ignatius' death to 135-140". The target's title says "cartas atribuidas a Ignacio" and its record says "Ignatius/the corpus". Use the object that the primary text states; until someone opens pp. 134-135, a neutral "Ignacio (muerte)" per the secondary source or leave Pervo marked [not found].
3. Vinzent's second line: the claim is "after 170" plus "by c. 177 at the latest", not an approximate closed interval. The table row "Approximate bounded interval: c. 170-177" erases the difference between the two bounds. Either write "despues de 170, a mas tardar c. 177" or add the row "Terminus post and ante".
4. Conflict with the decision on date words (DECISIONS 2026-10-06 "Death dates: words, and dagger where space is tight"): the rule uses "n. 1959 - vive", but Pedro rejected the abbreviation "m." because he did not read it as "murio". "n." has the same problem. Use "Nacio 1959" (en: "Born") and "Murio 1889" or the dagger form; and for unknown births the decided format "Nacimiento: desconocido" (the target says "product data should not invent a life span"; with Lechner's 1962 found the case may be moot, but Schoedel's birth year remains a gap). "vive" asserts alive: the same file defines "living" as "no death record found", so say nothing or "sin fecha de muerte registrada".
5. "decada de 140" is not natural Spanish; "decada del 140" or "anos 140" reads better. Pedro to choose; not a fact error.
6. Publication year is required only for changed views (Harnack), but the final recommendation says an equal timeline must "retain the dated object and publication year". Make one rule: show the work's year on every card (Zahn 1873, Lightfoot 1889, Lake 1912, Schoedel 1985, Joly 1979, Lechner 1999, Hubner 1997, Pervo 2010, Barnes 2008, Decrept 2008, Vinzent 2024).
7. Object column check: Decrept "persecucion en Antioquia" [verified, article abstract: "persecution of the Christians of Antioch in July 116"]; Zahn "viaje, cartas y muerte" [verified]; Lightfoot "martirio y cartas": only the martyrdom is dated on p. 30; Barnes "cartas" [verified by abstract]; Hubner/Joly "corpus/cartas" fine; Harnack both entries fine ("cartas" in 1878, martyrdom and letters in 1897).
