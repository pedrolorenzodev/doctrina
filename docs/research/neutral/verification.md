## 1. Method and legend

This pass follows `docs/playbooks/verification-pass.md`: exact catalogue and licence text was read at the linked page or file; scan identities were checked against title pages or Internet Archive metadata; modern reception statements were checked on the issuing bodies' sites. Search-result snippets and Wikipedia were used only to locate evidence and never upgrade a claim to primary-source status.

- **CONFIRMED** — the stated claim appears in the opened source. A qualification in the evidence column remains part of the verdict.
- **WRONG** — the checked file needs a factual or sourcing correction.
- **UNCONFIRMABLE** — the available source did not establish the whole claim, the source was unavailable, or only tertiary evidence was found.
