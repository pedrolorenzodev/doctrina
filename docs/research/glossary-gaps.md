_Dónde:_ Justificación (QE-9, §6). Uso divergente: no.

- es: «Una de las finalidades de la presente Declaración conjunta es demostrar que a partir de este diálogo, las iglesias luterana y católica romana se encuentran en posición de articular una interpretación común de nuestra justificación por la gracia de Dios mediante la fe en Cristo. - en: "The present Joint Declaration has this intention: namely, to show that on the basis of their dialogue the subscribing Lutheran churches and the Roman Catholic Church are now able to articulate a common understanding of our justification by God's grace through faith in Christ. It does not cover all that either church teaches about justification; it does encompass a consensus on basic truths of the doctrine of justification and shows that the remaining differences in its explication are no longer the occasion for doctrinal condemnations." — DJ 5, https://lutheranworld.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/original_jddj_english.pdf `[verified]`
