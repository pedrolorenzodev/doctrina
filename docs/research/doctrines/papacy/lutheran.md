## Qué enseña

### Definition: ministry entrusted through the gospel

> “Evangelium tribuit his, qui praesunt ecclesiis, mandatum docendi evangelii, remittendi peccata, administrandi sacramenta”.

— Tr 60, Latin original, Triglot p. 520 [verified: scan]; opening excerpt.

> «El evangelio atribuye a quienes presiden las iglesias el mandato de enseñar el evangelio, perdonar los pecados y administrar los sacramentos».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The paragraph continues with jurisdiction, excommunication and absolving the repentant; its full practical scope is given below.

### QE-1 · The common ministry of pastors and bishops

> “hanc potestatem iure divino communem esse omnibus, qui praesunt ecclesiis, sive vocentur pastores sive presbyteri sive episcopi”.

— Tr 61, Latin, p. 520 [verified: scan]; excerpt from the sentence asserting this common power.

> «que esta potestad es común por derecho divino a todos los que presiden las iglesias, ya se los llame pastores, presbíteros u obispos».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### QE-2 · The gospel office and the keys

AC XXVIII.5 calls the power of keys or bishops according to the gospel God's mandate to preach, remit/retain sins and administer sacraments (Latin and German originals, Triglot p. 84) [verified: scan]. Its own positive account precedes the papal rejection.

### QE-3 · Keys belonging to the church

> “Tribuit igitur principaliter claves ecclesiae et immediate, sicut et ob eam causam ecclesia principaliter habet ius vocationis.”

— Tr 24, Latin original, p. 510 [verified: scan].

> «Por tanto, atribuye las llaves a la iglesia de manera principal e inmediata, así como por esa misma razón la iglesia tiene de manera principal el derecho de llamar».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The reference is to calling ministers in the Treatise's argument, not every civil or personal kind of invitation.

### QE-4 · Rejection of universal papal superiority by divine right

Tr 7 introduces its claim that the Roman bishop is not superior by divine right to other bishops and pastors (p. 504) [verified: scan]. SA II.4.1 denies that the pope is the whole church's head by divine right and assigns that headship to Jesus Christ; it describes the pope instead as bishop/pastor of Rome and those associated with him (German original, p. 470) [verified: scan]. These are the named confessions' claims, not the site's conclusion.

### QE-5 · Historical Antichrist designation

> “daß der Papst der rechte Endechrist oder Widerchrist sei, der sich über und wider Christum gesetzt und erhöht hat, weil er will die Christen nicht lassen selig werden ohne seine Gewalt, welche doch nichts ist, von Gott nicht geordnet noch geboten.”

— SA II.4.10, German original, p. 474 [verified: scan]; designation and its complete qualifying grounds, beginning after the introductory assertion. Corrected 2026-10-30, C-L1; prior shortened excerpt preserved in `lutheran.apply.md`.

> «que el papa es el verdadero Anticristo o adversario de Cristo, que se ha puesto y elevado por encima y en contra de Cristo, porque pretende no dejar que los cristianos se salven sin su poder, que sin embargo nada es, ni está ordenado ni mandado por Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

**Status:** this is a historical SA formulation inside the Book of Concord. LCMS *Handbook* 2023, Constitution II p. 11, names the Smalcald Articles within its acceptance of symbolical books without reservation [verified: scan; L-06; official district-hosted PDF linked in Fuentes]. This establishes that named institutional subscription, not a new paragraph-specific interpretation or every body's 2026 position. The map's ELCA 2024 2.05–2.06 graded-language comparison remains inherited and [unverified] here. The clause is never presented as every present Lutheran individual's description of another church.

### QE-6 · Human arrangements distinguished from divine necessity

SA II.4.7–9 considers a head chosen by human authority for peace and unity as a hypothetical, with the limitations and criticism it states (German pp. 472–474) [verified: scan]. Melanchthon's individual subscription gives its own qualified human-law formulation, separately identified below. Neither is silently substituted for the whole collection's teaching.

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · Apostles sent equally

> “Christus pariter mittit apostolos sine discrimine, quum ait: Sicut misit me Pater, ita et ego mitto vos.”

— Tr 9, Latin original, Triglot p. 504 [verified: scan]; excerpt following the printed reference John 20:21.

> «Cristo envía por igual a los apóstoles, sin distinción, cuando dice: Como me envió el Padre, así también yo os envío».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### PQ-2 · The common ministry rather than domination

Tr 8 reads Luke 22:25 within the apostles' dispute about government and interprets Christ as sending them as equals to the common ministry of the gospel (Latin original, p. 504) [verified: scan]. Tr 11 reads 1 Corinthians 3:6 as equalizing ministers and placing the church above its ministers (Latin original, p. 506) [verified: scan]. These are the Treatise's interpretations, not independent exegesis supplied by the dossier.

### PQ-3 · Peter speaking for the apostolic company

> “In omnibus illis dictis Petrus sustinet personam communem totius coetus apostolorum”.

— Tr 23, Latin original, p. 510 [verified: scan]; opening excerpt.

> «En todos esos dichos Pedro representa a toda la asamblea de los apóstoles».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The paragraph itself juxtaposes singular address to Peter with Matthew 18:18 and John 20:23's plural address.

### PQ-4 · The rock in the Treatise's own wording

> “certe ecclesia non est aedificata super auctoritatem hominis, sed super ministerium illius professionis, quam Petrus fecerat, in qua praedicat Iesum esse Christum, Filium Dei”.

— Tr 25, Latin original, p. 510 [verified: scan]; excerpt from its interpretation of Matthew 16.

> «ciertamente la iglesia no está edificada sobre la autoridad de un hombre, sino sobre el ministerio de aquella confesión que Pedro había hecho, en la cual proclama que Jesús es el Cristo, el Hijo de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The Treatise's subsequent citations of Fathers remain its quoted witnesses, not modern Lutheran voices.

### PQ-5 · Its historical argument

Tr 12–14 invokes Nicaea and local episcopal elections to distinguish synodical/human arrangement from superiority by divine right (Latin original, p. 506) [verified: scan]. These are the Treatise's historical claims. This draft has not independently verified the canons, patristic quotations or its account of early councils, and does not present them as new neutral historical findings [unverified] for the underlying history.

### PQ-6 · Human-law accommodation is qualified

Melanchthon's individual subscription states:

> “Vom Papst aber halte ich, so er das Evangelium wollte zulassen, daß ihm um Friedens und gemeiner Einigkeit willen derjenigen Christen, so auch unter ihm sind und künftig sein möchten, seine Superiorität über die Bischöfe, die er sonst hat, iure humano auch von uns zugelassen sei.”

— German subscription following SA, Triglot p. 500 [verified: scan].

> «En cuanto al papa, sostengo que, si quisiera permitir el evangelio, por la paz y la unidad común de los cristianos que están bajo él y podrían estarlo en adelante, también nosotros podríamos admitir, por derecho humano, la superioridad que de otro modo tiene sobre los obispos».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. This is a named signer's condition, not a universal Lutheran acceptance of papal primacy or an erasure of SA II.4 and Tr.

## Cómo se vive

### Teaching and sacramental ministry

AC XXVIII.8 places the exercise of ecclesial power in teaching/preaching the Word and administering sacraments according to vocation (original Latin and German, Triglot p. 84) [verified: scan]. In its immediate continuation AC XXVIII.8–9 names eternal righteousness, the Holy Spirit and eternal life as the gifts conferred through Word and sacraments (same p. 84) [verified: scan; L-05]. It distinguishes that ministry from civil administration.

### Discipline and restoration

Tr 60 continues the definition quoted at the opening:

> “praeterea iurisdictionem, videlicet mandatum excommunicandi eos, quorum nota sunt crimina, et resipiscentes rursum absolvendi.”

— Latin original, Triglot p. 520 [verified: scan].

> «además, jurisdicción, es decir, el mandato de excomulgar a quienes tienen delitos conocidos y de absolver nuevamente a quienes se arrepienten».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. This is the Treatise's own disciplinary prescription, not an account of its frequency today.

### Government and equality of office

Tr 61 attributes the power to those presiding over churches, whether called pastors, presbyters or bishops (QE-1). SA II.4.9 proposes living under Christ as head, with bishops holding together in common doctrine, faith, sacraments, prayer and love (German p. 472) [verified: scan]. Those are the sources' stated prescriptions, not an invented administrative constitution.

### Ecclesial judgment

Tr 56 calls synodical judgments judgments of the church rather than the pontiffs and speaks of judging and deciding from God's Word (Latin original, p. 520) [verified: scan]. The text's historical treatment of magistrates is not rewritten as a present civil policy.

No present Lutheran ordination procedure, episcopal succession requirement, parish governance statistic or pontifical ceremonial is inferred from these excerpts. Those matters are outside the practiced prescriptions documented here; no universal silence is claimed.
