## A. Neutrality: the site's own voice

### 1 · Four Scripture rows and the Cómo se vive intro state what Christ did and what communion is, in the site's voice — **must-fix**

**Where:** §3 table, rows Jn 20:21, Jn 21:15-17, Mt 18:18 / 28:16-20, Lc 22:26-27 / Mt 20:25; §5 intro, first paragraph.
**Problem:** The justification critic's 1, same section. Jn 20:21: «Cristo edificó la Iglesia enviando a los apóstoles, y quiso que sus sucesores, los obispos, fueran pastores hasta el fin de los siglos (Lumen gentium 18)» is a historical and theological claim in the site's voice with the citation at the end; a Baptist reader denies the "sucesores" half. Jn 21: «después de la resurrección, Cristo confía a Pedro todo el rebaño (Vaticano I, cap. 1)»: the 1848 Encyclical reads the same verses as «a simple restoration to his Apostleship» `[verified in D]`, so "confía a Pedro todo el rebaño" is one side's reading stated as the event. Mt 18:18: «El poder de "atar y desatar" se dio también al colegio… a Pedro solo se le confiaron explícitamente las llaves»: the Treatise ¶23 says the keys were given «pariter omnibus apostolis» `[verified in D]`; the row decides it. Lc 22:26-27: «el primado no es poder… sino servicio; por eso el papa es "siervo de los siervos de Dios"»: "el primado no es poder" is the site's sentence (UUS 88 says the opposite of what a reader may take from it: that power must not be separated from ministry, `[verified: uus_es.txt, §88]`). §5 intro: «Estar en plena comunión con la Iglesia católica es estar unido a Cristo dentro de su estructura visible…» defines full communion as union with Christ in the site's voice; CIC 205 is the Church's definition and must wear its owner. Also «Para un católico, el papa no es una figura lejana»: affective framing in the site's voice, not a fact of any document. Rows that quote and attribute (Mt 16, Jn 1:42, Lc 22:31-32, Hch 2, 1 Co 15:5, Mt 16:23, Ef 2:20, Jn 17:21, 2 Co 11:28, Mt 10:2) are fine.
**Fix:** Jn 20:21: «Para Lumen gentium 18, Cristo edificó la Iglesia enviando a los apóstoles y quiso que sus sucesores, los obispos, fueran pastores "hasta el fin de los siglos".» Jn 21: «El Vaticano I lee aquí que Cristo, después de la resurrección, confió a Pedro todo el rebaño (cap. 1); Juan Pablo II subraya…». Mt 18:18: «Para Lumen gentium 22, el poder de "atar y desatar" se dio también al colegio, pero "unido a su Cabeza"; el Catecismo añade que Pedro es "el único a quien Él confió explícitamente las llaves" (553).» Lc 22: «Juan Pablo II cita "Yo estoy en medio de vosotros como el que sirve" para decir que la potestad del primado no puede separarse del servicio; por eso se llama "siervo de los siervos de Dios" (Ut unum sint 88 y 94).» §5 intro: «Para el Código, están en plena comunión los bautizados unidos a Cristo "por los vínculos de la profesión de fe, de los sacramentos y del régimen eclesiástico" (can. 205)»; drop «no es una figura lejana» («Para un católico, el papa se nombra en cada Misa: …»).

### 2 · QE-1 opens «Según el Evangelio» in the site's voice — **must-fix (one phrase)**

**Where:** §1 QE-1 "Texto para el sitio".
**Problem:** «Según el Evangelio, Cristo prometió y dio directamente a Pedro, y solo a él, el primado de jurisdicción…». The indicative inside a QE draft is accepted under the heading «Lo que la Iglesia católica enseña» (justification critic 6), but "Según el Evangelio" appeals to the authority the evangelical reader also holds and says the Gospel teaches the primacy of jurisdiction. Vatican I's own words are «juxta Evangelii testimonia» `[verified in D]`; the site must show that the claim about the Gospel is the council's.
**Fix:** «"Según los testimonios del Evangelio" (Vaticano I), Cristo prometió y dio…» or «Para la Iglesia, el Evangelio atestigua que Cristo…».

### 3 · «reconoce» in three answers presupposes the fact or frames the Church as conceding — **must-fix (one word each)**

**Where:** §4 OB-2 answer, second paragraph; OB-3 answer, third paragraph; OB-4 answer, first paragraph.
**Problem:** Justification critic 4 ("reconoce" presupposes). OB-2: «La Iglesia reconoce también lo que dicen Mateo 18 y Juan 20»: "reconoce" grants that the verses "say" what the Treatise says they say, then the answer denies the conclusion. ### 4 · «El canon del Vaticano I que responde a esta objeción» decides whom the canon hits — **must-fix (wording)**

**Where:** §4 OB-1, OB-3, OB-4, last paragraph of each answer; the §4 Method line "Answer endings".
**Problem:** The justification critic's 10, unchanged. OB-4 is the clearest case: the ch. 2 canon anathematizes whoever says «Romanum Pontificem non esse beati Petri in eodem primatu successorem» `[verified in D]`; the 1848 Encyclical §11 does not say that. **Fix:** «El canon del Vaticano I sobre este punto dice:» in all three, as decided for the justification dossier; in OB-4 the dossier note should say which clause of the 1848 text, if any, the canon reaches (none directly; the relevant Catholic claim is the ch. 2 chapter sentence that Peter lives on «in suis successoribus, episcopis sanctae Romanae Sedis, ab ipso fundatae, eiusque consecratae sanguine» `[verified: pa_la.txt, read today]`, and it is not a canon).

## Verdict

The Catholic doctrine is reported in the Church's words, the grades show their basis, the Fathers stay inside the Catechism and Vatican I, and the objections are built from the official texts of the traditions that raise them, with the harsh words on both sides quoted as written. The breaches in the site's voice are phrases: four Scripture rows and the Cómo se vive intro that state what Christ did and what communion is, «Según el Evangelio» in QE-1, three «reconoce», «el canon que responde a esta objeción», and two evaluative asides. The fairness problems are larger and run in both directions. Against the objectors: joint syntheses give every tradition the clauses of one (OB-1, OB-5, OB-7); the Spanish of the Orthodox texts is a fragment of the English quoted while the Catholic answers are in full official Spanish (OB-5 above all); Florence is offered to the Orthodox without the fact that they repudiated it; the status rule D announces is applied to two objections of eight, and the status D says is "not searched" for the LCMS is in the Lutheran map and reaffirms the identification in 1932. Against the Church: her answer to "Scripture alone judges" is cut before CCC 85, so she reads as conceding what she does not; and in OB-2 she is credited with recognizing "what John 20 says" and quoted on nothing of the kind, when the Catechism has the text. The Treatise's history (¶35–36) is summarized while the CDF's counter-history is quoted (OB-8). Fix the phrases, mark the single-tradition clauses, translate the objectors' passages whole, put the LCMS 1932 line and the IPCH 1903 text in the status lines, add CCC 85, 1441 and 1444 and Tr ¶35–36, keep the martyrdom out of the "Dogma" sentence; then a confessional Lutheran, a Presbyterian and an Orthodox reader would each recognize their objection on the page, a Catholic theologian would find his Church neither softened nor sharpened, and a secular reader would find the labels no stronger than the canons behind them.
