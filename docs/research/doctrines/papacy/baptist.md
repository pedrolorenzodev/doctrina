## Qué enseña

### Positive church account: NH XIII

> “a visible Church of Christ is a congregation of baptized believers, associated by covenant in the faith and fellowship of the Gospel”

> «una Iglesia visible de Cristo es una congregación de creyentes bautizados, asociados por pacto en la fe y la comunión del Evangelio».

— NH XIII, McGlothlin 1911 p. 305 [verified: scan; B-02]. Spanish [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from English, unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. Excerpt starts after the introductory confession formula; no variant footnote modifies it.

### Current convention statement: BF&M VI

> “Lordship of Christ”

— BF&M VI, current official print p. 7 [verified: scan; B-03]; [official article](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/#vi). «señorío de Cristo» [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from English, unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

BF&M VI describes an autonomous local congregation of baptized believers, covenant fellowship, Christ's ordinances and laws, gospel mission and democratic processes under Christ. Each member is accountable to Christ; its two scriptural offices are pastor/elder/overseer and deacon. Its final paragraph also names the Body of Christ including the redeemed of every age and people. These are the article's positive ecclesial claims [verified: scan; B-03], not an inferred papal refutation.

**Sparse for a specifically papal practice.** The inspected NH XIII/BF&M VI passages prescribe church fellowship, ordinances, Christ's laws and ministry; BF&M VI describes mission, congregational processes and accountability. Neither prescribes a specifically papal observance in these passages [verified: bounded scan check; B-04]. ## Grado de autoridad

BF&M's preamble describes confessions as:

> “Confessions are only guides in interpretation”

— current print p. 21 [verified: scan; B-03]; [official preamble](https://bfm.sbc.net/preamble/).

The same preamble places sole authority for faith/practice in Scripture, denies confessional finality or infallibility and combines liberty with doctrinal accountability. These are its own categories, not the site's judgment. NH is a historical convention-line confession; current BF&M a versioned convention statement. Contemporary adoption of a particular version by every Spanish-speaking body is not established here [unverified: inherited map boundaries].
