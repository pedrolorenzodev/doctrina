**Verifier correction before application:** the initial suspicion that omitted square brackets were an exactness error is rejected. The final dossier will show the brackets diplomatically to make the evidence easier to inspect; this is a presentation change, not a second WRONG item.

### B-06 — Authority and dates: CONFIRMED within named witnesses

McGlothlin pp. 300–301 visually checked: the editor dates Brown's eighteen-article revision to 1853 and identifies his base/variant method. Title and copyright pages visually confirm Philadelphia, American Baptist Publication Society, publication March 1911. His historical introduction is editorial evidence, not Baptist dogma. Current BF&M PDF printed p. 21 / PDF p. 23: its five principles give consensus for guidance, deny finality/infallibility, reserve revision and group confessions, and make Scripture the sole authority, with confessions interpretive guides rather than authority over conscience. The same page also calls confessions instruments of doctrinal accountability; that positive half must accompany the liberty language. The draft's current convention-statement category is supported; institutional/Free Will categories are outside the selected column.

### B-08 — Sparse practice/rationale and negative searches: CONFIRMED as bounded findings

Read the complete NH XIV on printed p. 306, BF&M 1925 XIII p. 73, 1963 VII p. 276 left and current VII p. 8. They give remembrance, and the latter two anticipation; NH adds solemn self-examination. They name elements and baptism prerequisite but supply no numerical schedule, detailed ministerial rubric, reservation rule or theory of spiritual nourishment like 2LCF 30. Do not fill those absences with another family's confession. The selected articles do not establish actual congregation-level practice. This is a bounded search, not a statement that Baptists never teach those matters elsewhere.
