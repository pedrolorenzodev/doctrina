## Conventions and source boundary

- **Represented tradition:** Eastern Orthodox churches in communion as delimited in `traditions/orthodox.md` §1; Oriental Orthodox churches are outside this column. This is not a confession attributed identically to every jurisdiction.
- **Column documents:** the seven received ecumenical councils and their received canonical corpus, including Trullo; the later councils listed in the map, with their distinct reception; the synodally approved seventeenth-century confessions, with approval and Orthodox criticism; Philaret's synodally published catechism. This follows DECISIONS 2026-10-15, “Which texts each column quotes”.
- **Confessional witnesses:** Jerusalem 1672, Dositheus XV–XVII; Mogila's Greek confession approved in 1643, I.98–99,106–107; Jeremiah II's Answers where the map supplies them. Gennadius and Kritopoulos are not promoted from historical/private witnesses to binding confessions. Lucaris is not an adopted Orthodox strand.
- **Greek and Slavic/Russian usages:** retained separately, in the map's order. The Greek Dositheus text and Philaret's Russian catechetical witness are individually attributed. Neither replaces the other. Russian revisions of Dositheus are not silently substituted for the Greek text.
- **Boundary of this dossier:** the foregoing mapped column universe is used only within this Eucharist research pass. Liturgical books document received practice with recension labels; they do not acquire a new pan-Orthodox approval by being quoted. The 1848/1895 encyclicals retain their patriarchal-synodal category.
- **Crete 2016:** its list identifies later councils and its reception caveat accompanies that map; its own teaching is not quoted as a doctrine-page voice. The ROC 2020 catechetical project is mentioned only. Local statutes and contemporary catechisms cannot silently fill a confessional gap.
- **Original witness:** Greek Dositheus in Philip Schaff, *The Creeds of Christendom*, II, pp. 427–431, [digitized print](https://archive.org/details/creedschristendo02scha/page/427/mode/1up). The inspected title page reads New York: Harper & Brothers, **1896**; its copyright page reads **1877**, the year also retained by the catalogue. The 1896 imprint governs this copy. [verified: scan; verification-orthodox.md O-18]
- **English controls:** J. N. W. B. Robertson, *The Acts and Decrees of the Synod of Jerusalem* (London: Thomas Baker, 1899); Mogila in J. J. Overbeck's 1898 edition; Philaret in Schaff II, Blackmore's English translation. English translations are not the source of the Spanish renderings below.
- **Spanish:** the mapped search found no reusable public-domain Spanish confession/catechism edition (`traditions/orthodox.md` §1.4; `sources/orthodox.md`). Spanish quotations here are translated directly from the Greek page, labelled **[traducción propia asistida por IA]**, unreviewed by Pedro. - **Tags:** `[verified: scan]` refers to the specified source-language page image checked in `verification-orthodox.md`, O-01–20/O-23–24. - **Copyright:** the historical witnesses are mapped as public domain; no new jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction rights audit has been done [unverified]. ## Qué enseña

### Definition: the mystery handed on by Christ

**Dositheus XVII**, opening, Greek, Schaff II p. 427:

> Πιστεύομεν τὸ πανάγιον μυστήριον τῆς ἱερᾶς εὐχαριστίας, ὅπερ ἀνωτέρω κατὰ τάξιν τέταρτον ἐξεθέμεθα, ἐκεῖνο εἶναι, ὅπερ ὁ κύριος παρέδωκε τῇ νυκτί, ᾗ παρέδιδου ἑαυτὸν ὑπὲρ τῆς τοῦ κόσμου ζωῆς. [verified: scan]

> «El santísimo misterio de la sagrada Eucaristía, que antes colocamos cuarto en el orden, es aquel que el Señor entregó la noche en que se entregaba a sí mismo por la vida del mundo». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The introductory verb of confession is omitted in this Spanish excerpt; the positive predicate is retained. Authority: confession of the Jerusalem synod, not a new universal creed.

### QE-1 · The very body and blood

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 428, the positive clause after its historical contrasts:

> ἀλλ' ἀληθῶς καὶ πραγματικῶς, ὥστε μετὰ τὸν ἁγιασμὸν τοῦ ἄρτου καὶ τοῦ οἴνου μεταβάλλεσθαι, μετουσιοῦσθαι, μεταποιεῖσθαι, μεταρρυθμίζεσθαι τὸν μὲν ἄρτον εἰς αὐτὸ τὸ ἀληθὲς τοῦ κυρίου σῶμα […] [verified: scan]

> «Sino verdadera y realmente, de modo que después de la consagración del pan y del vino el pan se cambia, se transustancia, se transforma, se convierte en el mismo cuerpo verdadero del Señor […]». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The same paragraph identifies that body through Christ's birth, baptism, passion, burial, resurrection and ascension; its wine clause identifies his blood shed on the cross (p. 428). These identifications belong to the confession, not to an argument supplied by this dossier.

### QE-2 · Substance and appearances

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 428:

> Ἔτι μετὰ τὸν ἁγιασμὸν τοῦ ἄρτου καὶ τοῦ οἴνου οὐκ ἔτι μένειν τὴν οὐσίαν τοῦ ἄρτου καὶ τοῦ οἴνου, ἀλλ' αὐτὸ τὸ σῶμα καὶ τὸ αἷμα τοῦ κυρίου ἐν τῷ τοῦ ἄρτου καὶ τοῦ οἴνου εἴδει καὶ τύπῳ, ταὐτὸν εἰπεῖν, ὑπὸ τοῖς τοῦ ἄρτου συμβεβηκόσιν. [verified: scan]

> «Después de la consagración del pan y del vino ya no permanece la sustancia del pan y del vino, sino el cuerpo mismo y la sangre misma del Señor bajo la apariencia y figura del pan y del vino; es decir, bajo los accidentes del pan». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

**Textual boundary:** this is the Greek formulation printed by Schaff. The map records qualifications and omissions in the Russian 1838 Dositheus; that recension has not been collated here. Do not label the Greek clause a verbatim Russian formula.

### QE-3 · The whole Christ in each portion

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 429:

> ἀλλ' ὅλον ὁλικῶς τὸν δεσπότην Χριστὸν κατ' οὐσίαν μετὰ ψυχῆς δηλονότι καὶ θεότητος, ἤτοι τέλειον Θεὸν καὶ τέλειον ἄνθρωπον. [verified: scan]

> «Sino al Señor Cristo entero, totalmente, según la sustancia, con alma y divinidad, es decir, perfecto Dios y perfecto hombre». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The sentence concerns every smallest part of the changed bread and wine; the paragraph proceeds to the same Christ in celebrations in many places. Its subject is not a multiplication of Christs (XVII, p. 429).

### QE-4 · Reception and its fruit

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 428:

> Πλὴν τοῖς μὲν εὐσεβέσι καὶ ἀξίοις ἄφεσιν ἁμαρτιῶν καὶ ζωὴν αἰώνιον προξενεῖν· τοῖς δὲ ἀσεβέσι καὶ ἀναξίοις κατάκρισιν καὶ κόλασιν αἰώνιον παραχωρεῖν. [verified: scan]

> «A los piadosos y dignos les procura remisión de los pecados y vida eterna; a los impíos e indignos, condenación y castigo eterno». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The preceding sentence names bodily reception by both groups. This historical confessional warning is not a claim about named present-day persons or congregations.

### QE-5 · Sacrifice for living and departed

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 430:

> Ἔτι εἶναι θυσίαν ἀληθῆ καὶ ἱλαστικὴν προσφερομένην ὑπὲρ πάντων τῶν εὐσεβῶν ζώντων καὶ τεθνεώτων καὶ ὑπὲρ ὠφελείας πάντων […] [verified: scan]

> «Es un sacrificio verdadero y propiciatorio, ofrecido por todos los piadosos vivos y difuntos y para beneficio de todos […]». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The continuation attributes this to the prayers of the mystery handed on by the apostles according to the Lord's command. It does not supply a separate modern theory of sacrifice.

### QE-6 · The term does not explain the manner

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 431:

> Ἔτι τῇ μετουσίωσις λέξει οὐ τὸν τρόπον πιστεύομεν δηλοῦσθαι, καθ' ὃν ὁ ἄρτος καὶ ὁ οἶνος μεταποιοῦνται εἰς τὸ σῶμα καὶ τὸ αἷμα τοῦ κυρίου […] [verified: scan]

> «Con la palabra transustanciación no se significa el modo por el que el pan y el vino se transforman en el cuerpo y la sangre del Señor […]». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

**Philaret Q.339–340**, Schaff II pp. 497–498, Blackmore's English witness:

> Because at the moment of this act the bread and wine are changed, or transubstantiated, into the very Body of Christ, and into the very Blood of Christ. [verified: scan]

Q.340 continues that the term does not define the manner, known to God, and quotes John Damascene. This is Philaret's reception of a patristic witness; John is not assigned to a modern tradition. The English is retained without a Spanish translation from English.

### QE-7 · Mogila's distinct witness

Mogila I.106–107 is the second seventeenth-century confessional locus, not a paraphrase of Dositheus. Overbeck 1898, pp. 79–80, locates presence under the visible species, lawful priesthood, the altar/cloth, leavened bread, wine and water, and the invocation of the Holy Spirit. [verified: scan, English witness; O-23]

No verbatim Spanish rendering is supplied from Overbeck's English. The received Greek revision, rather than the first Latin draft alone, is the column's source boundary. Kimmel 1850 p. 178, I.106, was also visually read in Greek; I.107 p. 179 has gutter loss and its following page-image requests timed out. Full Greek diplomatic collation remains incomplete, so this part stays sparse, not silent. [O-23]

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · Christ's institution

**Dositheus XVII**, Greek, p. 427:

> Λαβὼν γὰρ ἄρτον καὶ εὐλογήσας ἔδωκε τοῖς ἁγίοις αὐτοῦ μαθηταῖς καὶ ἀποστόλοις εἰπών· λάβετε, φάγετε· τοῦτό ἐστι τὸ σῶμά μου. [verified: scan]

> «Pues tomó pan, lo bendijo y lo dio a sus santos discípulos y apóstoles diciendo: tomen, coman; esto es mi cuerpo». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

The cup words follow on the same page. This is the confession's own scriptural wording; the dossier does not construct another argument around it.

### PQ-2 · The consecration and invocation

**Philaret Q.338**, Schaff II p. 497, English print:

> And after this the invocation of the Holy Ghost, and the blessing the gifts, that is, the bread and wine which have been offered. [verified: scan]

The preceding sentence names the institution words and prints Matthew 26:26–28. Q.339 supplies the catechism's own “Because” answer, quoted above. No exclusive “institution versus epiclesis” theory is inferred.

Mogila I.107, Overbeck pp. 79–80, supplies a parallel invocation locus and John 19:34; the English page images have now been checked. [verified: scan, English witness; O-23] Its full Greek I.107 collation remains incomplete; no Spanish is made from the English.

### PQ-3 · Union and life

**Philaret Q.342**, Schaff II p. 498, English print:

> He is in the closest manner united to Jesus Christ himself, and, in him, is made partaker of everlasting life. [verified: scan]

Q.342 immediately prints John 6:56 and 6:54. The catechism supplies both the answer and the passages; these are not independent proof texts selected by the drafter.

### PQ-4 · One body shared

**Philaret Q.329**, Schaff II p. 496, English print:

> It signifies, as the Apostle explains, that we, being many, are one bread and one body; for we are all partakers of that one bread. [verified: scan]

The printed reference is 1 Corinthians 10:17. This belongs to the question about the one loaf; it is not transferred to a claim about present jurisdictional arrangements.

### PQ-5 · The source's stated limit

Dositheus XVII, p. 430, calls the mystery apprehensible by faith rather than the sophistries of human wisdom. Its own explicit explanation-limit is quoted under QE-6. Nothing about physical mechanism is supplied by this dossier.

**Estado del lenguaje histórico:** se atribuye al decreto del sínodo de Jerusalén de 1672, no a la voz de Doctrina. La suscripción histórica está en Schaff p. 444; este dossier no establece una reafirmación o derogación actual por cada jurisdicción. La crítica teológica señalada en Grado de autoridad se presenta por separado, sin convertirla en un acto sinodal. [verified: scan for historical subscription; current jurisdictional status not established; O-24]

## Cómo se vive

### Preparation of elements and faithful

**Philaret Q.323**, Schaff II p. 496:

> This: that first the elements are prepared for the Sacrament; secondly, the faithful are prepared for the Sacrament; lastly, the Sacrament itself is consecrated. [verified: scan]

**Philaret Q.328**, same page:

> that is, leavened, pure, wheaten bread. [verified: scan]

Q.327 describes wine mixed with water, commemoration of the Church, saints, living and departed. Q.332, p. 497, names the blood and water from Christ's side. These are catechetical directions, not measured contemporary prevalence.

### Examination, penitence, fasting and prayer

**Philaret Q.341**, p. 498:

> To examine his conscience before God, and to cleanse it from sin by penitence; for doing which he has helps in fasting and prayer. [verified: scan]

The passage then quotes 1 Corinthians 11:28–29. No new number of fasting hours is added.

### Frequency: the historical catechism's wording

**Philaret Q.343**, p. 498, excerpt:

> Our Mother the Church calls on all, who would live religiously, to confess before their ghostly Father, and communicate in the Body and Blood of Christ, four times yearly, or even every month, but requires all, without exception, to receive it at the least once in the year. [verified: scan; verification-orthodox.md O-16]

This is a historical Russian catechetical prescription; it is not labelled a universal present Orthodox minimum. The question's historical assertion about primitive Christians belongs to Philaret and has not been independently investigated.

### Participation without communion

**Philaret Q.344**, p. 499:

> They may and should take part in the Liturgy by prayer and faith, and especially by a continual remembrance of our Lord Jesus Christ […] [verified: scan]

The full answer refers to Luke 22:19. Q.333–337 distinguishes catechumens and faithful; no inferred contemporary enforcement policy is attached to those historical rubrics.

### Honour and ordained celebration

**Dositheus XVII**, p. 430:

> Μία γὰρ ἡ προσκύνησις τῆς ἁγίας τριάδος καὶ τοῦ σώματος καὶ αἵματος τοῦ κυρίου. [verified: scan]

> «Pues una es la adoración de la santa Trinidad y del cuerpo y la sangre del Señor». [traducción propia asistida por IA; checked against Greek in S1; unreviewed by Pedro]

XVII, p. 431, restricts celebration to a pious priest who received priesthood from a pious and lawful bishop. The dossier records that direction without judging another tradition's orders.

### Recensions and canonical directions

The map identifies Trullo 32, 52 and 101; Constantinople 1691; Greek and Slavic anaphoras; and the Third-Hour troparion in both the Greek-based Robertson and Slavic Hapgood witnesses. Those page images were not acquired here. This practice sublayer is sparse through incomplete source extraction, not Orthodox silence.

No claim is made about today's most common reception method, frequency, pastoral exceptions or local fasting rule. The map's whole normative universe has not been searched for those specific questions; no silence verdict is warranted.

## Grado de autoridad

### Received councils and canonical corpus

The numbered tiers in `traditions/orthodox.md` §§2–4 organize the research; they are not a universally promulgated Orthodox numbered scale. OCA's published catechesis describes the seven ecumenical councils as received by the whole Church and their definitions/canons as doctrinal sources. [verified: official publisher page; verification-orthodox.md O-22] Trullo and the later councils retain their mapped reception qualifications; their individual Greek canonical texts are not newly collated here. [unconfirmed as independent Greek collation; O-25]

### Approval and critique of the confessions

Jerusalem 1672's decrees belong to that synod's confession; the Greek subscription at Schaff p. 444 confirms its historical date and attribution [verified: scan; O-24]. Mogila's received Greek revision has the patriarchal approval of 11 March 1643, whose Greek-only qualification and date were visually checked in Overbeck p. 10 [verified: scan; O-19]. The competing dates for the Jassy meeting are left as the map records them, rather than silently resolved [Jassy date unresolved; no new date selected].

The Orthodox critique is retained alongside approval: Archbishop Basil Krivoshein's discussion (the library identifies a 2011 collected-works reprint; original publication date unconfirmed in `verification-orthodox.md` O-21) treats these confessions as historical monuments and criticizes their Latinizing formulations; the map records his refusal to treat Dositheus as an obligatory symbolic text of timeless significance (`traditions/orthodox.md` §9.3; `docs/research/traditions/verification-orthodox.md` C6). This is an attributed Orthodox critique, not a synodal repeal or this dossier's verdict [author-text paraphrase checked; original print/date unconfirmed in verification-orthodox.md O-21].

### Philaret and the range of reception

Schaff's printed title reproduces the Russian Holy Synod approval, school/Orthodox-Christian use and Moscow Synodical Press 1839 identity. The adjacent editorial headnote identifies Blackmore's 1845 English translation and says the question numbering is Schaff's. [verified: scan; O-20] Schaff's own ranking is not a synodal grading act. Its English language and qualifications remain visible beside the Greek confession; the Russian original is not independently collated in this pass.

The mapped 1838 Russian Dositheus and Philaret Q.340 are distinct witnesses; the 1838 recension warning remains unconfirmed as an independent print collation (O-25). Neither a Western editor's ranking nor a theologian's critique is made a pan-Orthodox approval act.
