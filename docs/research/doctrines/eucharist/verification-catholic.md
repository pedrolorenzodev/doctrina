Totals: 111 CONFIRMED, 8 WRONG, 10 UNCONFIRMABLE (verdict-level counts; see the two lists at the end). WebSearch not used; direct fetches only.
Legend: CONFIRMED / WRONG / UNCONFIRMABLE. Section refs are to `catholic.md` (D) by the dossier's own labels (QE-n, OB-n, PQ-n, CV-n) and line numbers. Method: pages fetched with curl, converted to text and searched with exact-string matching after normalizing case, accents, long s and punctuation (so OCR-noise differences are tolerated, never wording differences). Printed page numbers of the scans were derived from the per-page OCR (`_djvu.xml`: leaf index plus running-head numbers, majority vote over neighbouring leaves). No unlicensed scan or copy was used.
