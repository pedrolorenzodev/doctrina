## Conventions and source boundary

- **Column boundary:** the four families in the Baptist map, not a confession binding every Baptist. A = Particular/Reformed; B = General/Free Will; C = Southern Baptist/convention; D = bodies organized around principles. The owner's work order requires these strands to remain visible; it does not settle which the eventual screen presents.
- **A documents:** First London 1644, with the separately printed 1646 revision; Somerset 1656; Second London 1677/1689 (2LCF); Philadelphia 1742, whose additions are printed by McGlothlin; Baptist Catechism c. 1693/1695.
- **B documents:** Standard Confession 1660; Orthodox Creed 1678/9 (OC); northern Freewill Treatise 1834 with explicitly distinguished 1869 variants; NAFWB Treatise 2026. The northern historical text is not substituted for the present NAFWB text.
- **C documents:** New Hampshire in Brown's **1853** eighteen-article form (NH); Abstract of Principles 1858 (AP), an institutional standard; BF&M 1925, 1963 and 2000, the last with the 2023 amendment to VI. McGlothlin's NH presentation combines a base text and variant notes: quotations below explicitly reconstruct 1853 from those notes. It is not a scan of an 1833 original.
- **D documents:** BUGB Declaration of Principle, current official web text; BWA Beliefs Statement, supplementary federation testimony, whose member-body subscription status the map does not establish. Neither is silently treated as an SBC confession.
- **Regional controls:** the mapped UEBE Confession and Principles, CEBA 1987, CNBM doctrines/principles, CNBV distinctive doctrines, Paraguay's BF&M text, and the other mapped subscription/principles evidence are kept distinct from their English-language relatives. Similarity does not prove adoption. Coverage records acquisition limits rather than declaring a body silent from its relative's text.
- **Edition:** historical English quotations use W. J. McGlothlin, *Baptist Confessions of Faith* (Philadelphia: American Baptist Publication Society, 1911), with page and article. His historical introductions are editorial evidence, not confessional teaching. Page images: [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/baptistconfessio00mcgl).
- **Spanish:** the map identifies no public-domain Spanish edition for these historical confessions. UEBE and circulating 1689 translations have no established reusable public-domain licence. Spanish renderings below are made from the original English and labelled **[traducción propia asistida por IA]**, unreviewed by Pedro. Official modern Spanish excerpts, when used, are labelled separately.
- **Tags:** `[verified: scan]` means that particular English wording was read on the printed page during drafting; it does not certify the dossier. `[unverified]` marks web transcriptions, OCR-only material, edition/licence details not independently checked, and translations. There is no verification, critic or apply pass.
- **Copyright:** historical English print is treated as public-domain material under the mapped edition boundary; current BF&M, NAFWB and contemporary declarations receive only short excerpts and official links. The 1963 US nonrenewal claim is not permission for worldwide full-text reuse.
- **Scripture:** only references supplied by the Baptist documents are listed. Adjacent references in a combined article are not automatically assigned to one clause. No independent biblical argument is added.

## Qué enseña

### A — 2LCF: remembrance, nourishment and communion

> “for the perpetual remembrance, and shewing forth the sacrifice in his death, confirmation of the faith of believers in all the benefits thereof, their spiritual nourishment, and growth in him”
>
> «para el recuerdo perpetuo y la proclamación del sacrificio en su muerte, la confirmación de la fe de los creyentes en todos sus beneficios, su alimento espiritual y su crecimiento en él». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 30.1, McGlothlin p. 270. English [verified: scan]. The paragraph begins with Christ's institution on the night of his betrayal and continues with obedience and communion. Its positive account, rather than a denial addressed to another communion, opens this dossier.

> “do then also inwardly by faith, really and indeed, yet not carnally, and corporally, but spiritually receive, and feed upon Christ crucified & all the benefits of his death”
>
> «entonces también reciben interiormente por la fe, real y verdaderamente, aunque no carnal ni corporalmente sino espiritualmente, a Cristo crucificado y todos los beneficios de su muerte, y se alimentan de él». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 30.7, p. 272. English [verified: scan]. Its subjects are the paragraph's worthy receivers, not every person who receives the elements.

> “spiritually present to the faith of Believers, in that Ordinance, as the Elements themselves are to their outward senses”
>
> «espiritualmente presentes a la fe de los creyentes en esa ordenanza, como los elementos mismos lo están a sus sentidos externos». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 30.7, p. 272. English [verified: scan]. The grammatical subject in the full sentence is Christ's body and blood. This sentence is not reduced to BF&M's memorial terminology.

### B — Orthodox Creed: covenant, nourishment and sacrament

> “for the confirmation of the faithful believers in all the benefits of his death and resurrection, and spiritual nourishment and growth in him; sealing unto them their continuance in the covenant of grace”
>
> «para confirmar a los fieles creyentes en todos los beneficios de su muerte y resurrección, y para su alimento espiritual y crecimiento en él; sellándoles su permanencia en el pacto de gracia». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXIII, McGlothlin p. 148. English [verified: scan]. The article also uses “holy sacrament”; the vocabulary is this General Baptist document's own, not an imported classification.

### C — New Hampshire: common commemoration

> “by the sacred use of bread and wine, are to commemorate together the dying love of Christ; preceded always by solemn self-examination”
>
> «mediante el uso sagrado del pan y del vino, han de conmemorar juntos el amor de Cristo en su muerte; precedido siempre de un solemne examen de sí mismos». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

NH 1853 XIV, McGlothlin p. 306. English [verified: scan], retaining the bracketed 1853 addition “sacred”. The subjects in the full sentence are members of the church; baptism is its preceding requirement.

### C — Abstract: confirmation and renewed fellowship

> “to commemorate His death, to confirm the faith and other graces of Christians, and to be a bond, pledge and renewal of their communion with Him, and of their church fellowship”
>
> «conmemorar su muerte, confirmar la fe y las demás gracias de los cristianos, y ser vínculo, prenda y renovación de su comunión con él y de su comunión eclesial». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

AP XVI, 1858, [SBTS official transcription](https://www.sbts.edu/about/abstract/). English [unverified]. This institutional text is not a substitute for the convention's later statement.

### B — Free Will: commemoration, communion and entitlement

> “to commemorate the sufferings of Christ, and to represent, in the use of bread and wine, the communion which saints have with him, and with each other”
>
> «conmemorar los padecimientos de Cristo y representar, mediante el uso del pan y del vino, la comunión que los santos tienen con él y entre sí». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

Northern Freewill Treatise **1834**, church-ordinances §2, McGlothlin p. 326. English [verified: scan]. The text continues with the believer's right and duty to participate; the 1869 formulation is separately printed at p. 323 note, XVII.2.

>
> «unión espiritual con Cristo». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

NAFWB *Treatise* **2026**, II.XVIII.2, print p. 15 / PDF p. 23, [official PDF](https://nafwb.org/site/wp-content/uploads/NAFWB-Treatise-2026.pdf). English [unverified]. These words identify the beneficiaries of its stated privilege and duty; NH's admission rule is not imposed on this document.

### A — Philadelphia and catechetical witnesses

> “laying on of hands (with prayer) upon baptized believers, as such, is an ordinance of Christ, and ought to be submitted to by all such persons that are admitted to partake of the Lord's Supper”
>
> «la imposición de manos, con oración, sobre los creyentes bautizados, como tales, es una ordenanza de Cristo, a la que deben someterse todas las personas admitidas a participar de la Cena del Señor». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

The 1695-labelled Baptist Catechism transcription treats the Supper at Q102–104, not the later “Keach” transcription's Q107–108. Its answer about spiritual participation is retained as an unverified locator, rather than counted as another scan check of 2LCF. [unverified]

## Por qué

### Institution and duration

> “appointed by the Lord Jesus the only Law-giver, to be continued in his Church to the end of the world”
>
> «establecidas por el Señor Jesús, único legislador, para continuar en su Iglesia hasta el fin del mundo». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 28.1, McGlothlin p. 269. English [verified: scan]. The sentence refers to baptism and the Supper. Its margin cites Matthew 28:19–20 and 1 Corinthians 11:26.

### The once-for-all offering and the memorial

> “In this ordinance Christ is not offered up to his Father, nor any real sacrifice made at all, for remission of sin of the quick or dead; but only a memorial of that one offering up of himself, by himself, upon the crosse, once for all”
>
> «En esta ordenanza Cristo no es ofrecido a su Padre, ni se realiza sacrificio real alguno para remisión del pecado de vivos o muertos; sino sólo un memorial de aquella única ofrenda de sí mismo, hecha por él mismo sobre la cruz de una vez para siempre». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 30.2, pp. 270–271. English [verified: scan]. The paragraph's own rationale cites Hebrews 9:25–26,28. The sentence is retained as a confessional formulation, without a debate layer.

### The elements and their names

> “albeit in substance, and nature, they still remain truly, and only Bread, and Wine, as they were before”
>
> «aunque en sustancia y naturaleza siguen siendo verdaderamente, y sólo, pan y vino, como antes». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 30.5, p. 271. English [verified: scan]. Its preceding clause gives a figurative relation between the names of the elements and what they represent. The document's own account is quoted; no philosophical explanation is supplied by the drafter.

### Continuance in doctrine and fellowship

> “to continue stedfastly in Christs and the Apostles Doctrine, and assembling together, in fellowship, in breaking of Bread, and Prayer”
>
> «perseverar en la doctrina de Cristo y de los apóstoles, reuniéndose en comunión, en el partimiento del pan y en la oración». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

Standard Confession XIII, McGlothlin p. 116. English [verified: scan]. Its own reference is Acts 2:42. Somerset XXV.2 lists breaking bread with 1 Corinthians 11:23–24 (p. 209); its fuller fellowship passage is XLIII, pp. 213–214. These short historic formulations are not padded into a theory of presence.

### Bounded limits

First London 1644/1646, Somerset and Standard do not provide a separate detailed account comparable in length to 2LCF 30 in the bounded passages searched. BUGB's three-clause declaration has no Supper exposition. AP, NH, BF&M, OC and the Free Will texts supply the purposes quoted above; no further rationale is fabricated for a strand.
