Legend: CONFIRMED / WRONG / UNCONFIRMABLE. Section refs are the dossier's own labels (QE-n, PQ-n, OB-n, CV-n, ST-xx). Line numbers `tg`/`tl` = NPH e-text of the Triglot; `scan` = line of `lut/scan/trig_djvu.txt` (print-scan OCR).

**Method.** Every quote in «…» (German, Latin, Spanish of Trent/Westminster) and in "…" (English) was extracted from the dossier by script and aligned word by word (accents, case, ſ/s, ß, hyphenation and punctuation normalised; nothing else) against: the e-text, the scan OCR, the cached bookofconcord.org pages (`lut/boc/`), Ayala/Waterworth/WCF/WCF-1880/OPC/Robertson files. Every difference was then read by hand on the source line. The scan is treated as the edition; where the e-text and the scan differ the scan wins. Paragraph numbers were checked against the `N]` markers of the e-text at every cited line. Status lines were checked against the primary PDFs (LCMS Handbook 2023, ELCA CBCR (Wayback Oct 2024), LWF constitution, Leuenberg PDFs, *A Formula of Agreement* PDF) fetched today. RV1909 against `lut/rv/spaRV1909_vpl.txt`. Scripts and outputs: scratchpad `ver-lut/`.
