## Dated print witness

[verified] The comparison witness is *La Santa Biblia que contiene los Sagrados Libros del Antiguo y Nuevo Testamento: antigua versión de Cipriano de Valera cotejada con diversas traducciones y revisada con arreglo a los originales hebreo y griego, con referencias* (Madrid: Depósito Central de la Sociedad Bíblica B. y E., 1914), Internet Archive [`lasantabibliaque00unse`](https://archive.org/details/lasantabibliaque00unse/page/n6/mode/1up). The publisher and date were read on the title-page image. Image leaves used below are [Isaiah 53, leaf 498 / printed p. 488](https://archive.org/details/lasantabibliaque00unse/page/n498/mode/1up), [Malachi 1, leaf 630 / printed p. 620](https://archive.org/details/lasantabibliaque00unse/page/n630/mode/1up), [Hebrews 7, leaf 808 / printed p. 174](https://archive.org/details/lasantabibliaque00unse/page/n808/mode/1up), and [Hebrews 9–10, leaf 809 / printed p. 175](https://archive.org/details/lasantabibliaque00unse/page/n809/mode/1up).

The title page does not print the label “Reina-Valera 1909”; it identifies the Valera revision lineage and the Bible Society edition. Its text matches the eBible RV1909 in every requested verse. This makes it a dated early print witness for the text, not proof by itself of the naming or publication chronology of the revision. That distinction matters because later bibliographic accounts disagree over whether the revision called “1909” was already issued in that year or reached its definitive publication in 1923.

## Result

All 15 requested VPL verses agree word-for-word with the dated 1914 print images. The only observed representation difference is the VPL's use of square brackets where the print uses italics for supplied Spanish words in Hebrews 7:27, 10:10, and 10:12. The application should preserve the VPL spelling and punctuation exactly and decide separately whether its renderer should retain brackets or restore the print convention of italics.
