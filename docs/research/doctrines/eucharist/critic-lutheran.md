## B. Caricature: would each objector recognize the objection?

### 4 · OB-6 answers "one propitiatory sacrifice" to a Trent that is not shown saying "one and the same victim" — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-6, "Their own texts (four)" (lines 479–482); synthesis (line 474).
**Problem:** The synthesis honestly says «No es otro sacrificio que el de la cruz, ni la deroga», but none of the four texts shown says it: cann. 3, 1, 8 and Dositheus. The Catholic position's strongest honest form is Sess. XXII ch. 2: «la hostia es una misma, uno el mismo que ahora ofrece por el ministerio de los sacerdotes, que el que entonces se ofreció á sí mismo en la cruz, con sola la diferencia del modo de ofrecerse… tan lejos está que esta derogue de modo alguno á aquella» (Ayala pp. 317–318) `[verified in C, line 145; C's own OB-1 answer rests on it, line 378]`. Without it the Lutheran answer's second paragraph («el único sacrificio propiciatorio es la cruz», Ap XXIV.22) reads as refuting a Trent that teaches a second sacrifice, which Trent denies in words. A Catholic theologian will say the objector was shown at his weakest while the answer runs five paragraphs. Can. 8 (private Masses) is the weakest of the four and belongs with CV-2, not here.
**Fix:** Replace can. 8 with Sess. XXII ch. 2 (Latin, Ayala Spanish and Waterworth English, all already in `catholic.md` QE-4/OB-1 `[verified in C]`) as the second «ver más» text; keep can. 3 shown. The synthesis already fits. The Lutheran answer does not need to grow: Ap XXIV.19's distinction is its reply to "same victim, different manner" and is already there.

### 5 · OB-8: the FC's portrait of the "subtle Sacramentarians" is presented as a description of Westminster's position — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-8, answer, first paragraph (line 556): «La Fórmula de Concordia describe esa postura: algunos "dicen creer también en una verdadera presencia…", y sin embargo para ellos el cuerpo de Cristo "no está presente de ningún modo, sino solo arriba en el cielo más alto"».
**Problem:** «esa postura» is Westminster's (just quoted: "as really, but spiritually, present to the faith"). The FC describes its 1570s opponents (Ep VII.1 "the Zwinglian teachers" `[verified in D]`); the site page turns a 1577 polemical portrait into a description of a 1647 confession, and the portrait says the opponents hold the body «no está presente de ningún modo», which WCF 29.7 does not say. D's own note (line 566) sees the problem for the condemnations but not for this paragraph, where it bites most. Second, the quote is clipped to soften it: Ep VII.5 says they "speak very speciously in our own words, and pretend that they also believe a true presence" `[verified: lut/boc/epitome_the-lords-supper.txt l.8]`; D renders «dicen creer». DECISIONS 2026-10-20: harsh wording whole, never softened, status beside it.
**Fix:** «La Fórmula de Concordia (1577) describe así a los "sacramentarios sutiles" de su tiempo: "hablan con gran apariencia usando nuestras propias palabras y fingen creer también en una verdadera presencia…" (Epítome VII.4–5)» with ST-FC beside it, then the «Los sacramentarios dicen que no; nosotros decimos que sí» line. Never «esa postura». Same marker on the closing antithesis (Ep VII.26, 36), as D's note already asks.

### 6 · OB-10 synthesis puts a sentence in the Orthodox Church's mouth that Dositheus does not say — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-10 synthesis, last sentence (line 604): «Así, para la Iglesia ortodoxa, la presencia luterana junto al pan no explica las palabras "Esto es mi cuerpo".»
**Problem:** Decree XVII says impanation is what «the followers of Luther most ignorantly and wretchedly suppose» and affirms transubstantiation `[verified in D]`; it says nothing about "explaining the words". The sentence is the agent's inference, labelled «para la Iglesia ortodoxa». The Orthodox are the thinnest objector on the page (one text, no argument); adding an argument for them in the site's voice is the opposite breach of leaving them silent.
**Fix:** Cut the sentence; end the synthesis on Dositheus's own words («sino verdadera y realmente, de modo que… el pan es transmutado, transubstanciado, convertido y transformado en el verdadero Cuerpo mismo del Señor»).
