## A. Neutrality: the site's own voice

### 1 · The "Por qué" closing line and the Jn 6 table row conclude in the site's voice — **must-fix**

**Where:** §2 "Cierre" (line 232); §3 table, row Jn 6 (line 245).
**Problem:** «En Juan 6, cuando muchos discípulos se escandalizan y se van, Jesús no suaviza sus palabras: "Mi carne es verdadera comida…". Por eso, dice Juan Pablo II, "no se trata de un alimento metafórico".» The narrative clause ("Jesús no suaviza sus palabras") is the site telling the reader what Jesus did, and "Por eso" draws the conclusion before handing it to John Paul II. The table row repeats it: «Jesús insiste… y por eso no es "un alimento metafórico"». An evangelical reader hears the site endorsing the Catholic reading of John 6, on the page where the site promised each tradition speaks in its own words. The expert graded this "minor" (his 13c); for an outsider it is the single most visible breach in D, because ROADMAP makes this line the section's "punch".
**Fix:** Keep the punch, give it an owner: «Para la Iglesia católica, Juan 6 no admite una lectura metafórica: ante el escándalo de los oyentes, Jesús "recalca la verdad objetiva de sus palabras" y dice "mi carne es verdadera comida" (Juan 6:55). Juan Pablo II: "no se trata de un alimento metafórico" (Ecclesia de Eucharistia 16).» Same in the table row («…y por eso, según Ecclesia de Eucharistia 16, no es "un alimento metafórico"»). Add to the row the honest note D already has but drops from the draft: Trent XXI ch. 1 reads John 6 "according to the various interpretations of holy Fathers and Doctors" `[verified in D]`, so the Church's own documents leave the exegesis of John 6 more open than the punch line suggests.

### 2 · "Lo que la Iglesia siempre creyó" as a heading — **must-fix**

**Where:** §2 PQ-4 heading (line 217): «Lo que la Iglesia siempre creyó: dos Padres citados por el Catecismo».
**Problem:** The heading asserts, in the site's voice, that the early Church believed what the Catholic Church teaches, and places two Fathers under it. That is exactly "early Church = today's Catholic Church", the thing the Fathers rule (DECISIONS 2026-10-15) exists to avoid. Trent's own clause («ha creido por lo mismo perpetuamente la Iglesia de Dios», XIII ch. 4 `[verified in D]`) is a claim the Church makes; as a heading it becomes a claim the site makes. The neutral pointer under the quotes does not undo a heading.
**Fix:** «Dos Padres, tal como los cita el Catecismo» or «"Lo que la Iglesia ha creído siempre", según Trento: dos Padres citados por el Catecismo». In the body, make the Catechism the quoting voice visibly («El Catecismo cita a san Ambrosio: …»).

### 3 · Two answer openings in the site's voice — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-3 answer, first sentence (line 361); OB-5 answer, first sentence (line 410).
**Problem:** (a) OB-3 opens «Lo que se adora es Cristo, no el pan.» — a flat assertion before any attribution; on screen 04 it reads as the site settling the objection. (b) OB-5 opens «Trento desea lo mismo que piden los objetores» — the site speaks for the objectors and finds them in agreement with Trent. They do not ask that all attendants communicate; AC XXIV says private Masses were dropped because of lucre and *ex opere operato* (XXIV.13, 22–23 `[verified in D]`), and the Lutheran point is that the Supper is a gift received by faith, not a work offered. Claiming common ground on the objector's behalf is a rhetorical move, not a report.
**Fix:** (a) «Para la Iglesia católica, lo que se adora es Cristo, no el pan: Trento enseña…». (b) «Trento también quiere que los fieles comulguen en cada Misa: "Quisiera por cierto…"» and drop "lo mismo que piden los objetores".

### 4 · The "Cómo se vive" intro asserts the real presence and the state of grace as facts — **must-fix**

**Where:** §5 intro, second paragraph (line 479).
**Problem:** «Para comulgar hay que estar en gracia de Dios» and «Como Cristo sigue presente mientras duran las especies, las hostias consagradas se guardan en el sagrario». This paragraph is prose in the site's voice describing practice; the causal "Como Cristo sigue presente" slips from describing what Catholics do to affirming why it is true. "Estar en gracia de Dios" is insider language presented as a condition of fact.
**Fix:** «La Iglesia pide comulgar en gracia de Dios: quien tiene conciencia de pecado grave se confiesa antes (can. 916). Como, para la Iglesia, Cristo sigue presente mientras duran las especies, las hostias consagradas se guardan…».

### 6 · OB-2 puts a Westminster argument in Lutheran mouths — **must-fix** (extends expert 8 and 13a)

**Where:** §4 OB-2 title, synthesis, "Quién la plantea" (lines 315–319).
**Problem:** The expert caught the Acts 3:21 clause. Title per the objectors' words («…contradice la Escritura, el sentido común y la razón» if Reformed-led). The Catholic answer's "¿Y el cielo?" paragraph then answers Westminster and should say so.
