## Findings

### 1 · OB-5: the "ex opere operato" objection is never answered with the Church's own definition of the term — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4, OB-5, "Síntesis" and "Respuesta católica".
**Problem:** The synthesis frames the objection exactly as AC XXIV does ("una obra que valdría por el solo hecho de celebrarse, sin la fe de quien la recibe"; Latin *ex opere operato*, quoted in D). The answer never says what the Church means by *ex opere operato*; it only offers Trent XXII ch. 2 "si con recta fe". A Catholic reader sees the answer concede the caricature by silence. The Church's own definition is in the universe and is the direct reply: the sacrament's efficacy comes from Christ's work, not from the merit of minister or recipient, **and** its fruits depend on the recipient's dispositions.
**Fix:** Add CCC 1128 as the first paragraph of the answer, then keep Trent XXII ch. 2 and ch. 6. CCC 1128 (texto oficial, https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p2s1c1a2_sp.html) `[verified]`: «los sacramentos obran *ex opere operato* (según las palabras mismas del Concilio: "por el hecho mismo de que la acción es realizada"), es decir, en virtud de la obra salvífica de Cristo, realizada de una vez por todas. De ahí se sigue que "el sacramento no actúa en virtud de la justicia del hombre que lo da o que lo recibe, sino por el poder de Dios" […] siempre que un sacramento es celebrado conforme a la intención de la Iglesia, el poder de Cristo y de su Espíritu actúa en él y por él, independientemente de la santidad personal del ministro. Sin embargo, los frutos de los sacramentos dependen también de las disposiciones del que los recibe.» (The last sentence is cut in my extract at "Sin embarg"; the verifier should confirm the closing wording.) On stipends, the current law is stronger than the 1562 reform decree alone: CIC can. 947 (en, https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann879-958_en.html) `[verified]`: "Any appearance of trafficking or trading is to be excluded entirely from the offering for Masses" (the page's OCR-like text reads "tracking"; the Latin is *negotiationis vel mercaturae*; use the Spanish page); can. 945 §2 `[verified]`: priests are earnestly recommended to celebrate "for the intention of the Christian faithful, especially the needy, even if they have not received an offering." Add both to the third paragraph.

### 2 · OB-6: the answer avoids the Church's actual teaching on the form of the sacrament — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4, OB-6, "Respuesta católica" and the "Honest note".
**Problem:** The Orthodox §X objects to the Latin position that consecration occurs with the Lord's words. D answers only with the epiclesis paragraphs (CCC 1353, 1105–1106, 1412), which is true but reads as evasion: it never states what the Church holds, and D's own note admits it ("does not say at what instant the change happens"). The Church does hold a position, inside the universe: (a) Trent XIII ch. 3, "ex vi verborum" `[verified in D]`; (b) CCC 1353's second sentence, which D quotes but buries: «En el relato de la institución, la fuerza de las palabras y de la acción de Cristo y el poder del Espíritu Santo hacen sacramentalmente presentes bajo las especies de pan y de vino su Cuerpo y su Sangre» (https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p2s2c1a3_sp.html) `[verified]`; (c) Florence, *Exsultate Deo* (22 Nov 1439, map 4.B T2, DH 1321): "Forma huius sacramenti sunt verba Salvatoris, quibus hoc confecit sacramentum" `[memory — wording from memory; the decree is in Mansi 31, the volume D already used for *Laetentur*; read and verify before use]`; (d) CCC 1375 (Chrysostom: «Esta palabra transforma las cosas ofrecidas») `[verified in D]`.
**Fix:** Restructure the answer: first the Church's teaching (words of Christ as form: Florence, Trent "ex vi verborum", CCC 1353 second sentence), then "no separa palabra y Espíritu" (CCC 1353 first sentence, 1105–1106, 1412). Keep the honest note that no Catholic document read answers the historical claim about Rome and Gaul. **Proposed map addition** (official status checked): PCPCU, *Guidelines for admission to the Eucharist between the Chaldean Church and the Assyrian Church of the East*, Rome, 20 Jul 2001, "in agreement with the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith and the Congregation for the Oriental Churches"; "the Congregation for the Doctrine of Faith on January 17th, 2001 concluded that this Anaphora can be considered valid"; "Pope John Paul II subsequently approved this decision"; the words of institution are present "in a dispersed euchological way, that is, integrated in prayers of thanksgiving, praise and intercession" (https://www.christianunity.va/content/unitacristiani/en/dialoghi/sezione-orientale/chiesa-assira-dell-oriente/altri-documenti/testo-in-inglese.html) `[verified; the old vatican.va URL returns 404]`. Grade: curial document approved by the Pope (map rule 4.D.2 A); it qualifies the "instant of the words" reading and belongs in OB-6 as the Church's own nuance, not in the site draft until Pedro approves the map change.

### 3 · QE-3 site draft reduces the sacrifice to propitiation — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1, QE-3, "Texto para el sitio".
**Problem:** The draft says the Mass is a true sacrifice, the same as Calvary, and "por eso […] propiciatorio". Correct, but a Catholic reads it as a Reformation-era polemic summary: the Church's own account of the sacrifice begins with thanksgiving and praise (the name "Eucharist") and memorial, then propitiation. Also "sin sangre" is a literal gloss; the Church's word is "incruento".
**Fix:** Add one sentence from CCC 1359–1361 before the propitiation clause, e.g. «Es, ante todo, sacrificio de alabanza y acción de gracias al Padre; es el memorial de la Pascua de Cristo; y es propiciatorio…». CCC 1360 (es) `[verified]`: «La Eucaristía es un sacrificio de acción de gracias al Padre […] "Eucaristía" significa, ante todo, acción de gracias.» CCC 1361 `[verified]`: «La Eucaristía es también el sacrificio de alabanza por medio del cual la Iglesia canta la gloria de Dios en nombre de toda la creación.» CCC 1330 lists the names ("Santo Sacrificio… sacrificio de alabanza… sacrificio puro") `[verified]`. Replace "sin sangre" with "de modo incruento" (Trent XXII ch. 2, "incruentam", `[verified in D]`).

### 4 · The grading method: "canon with anathema" is not the Church's criterion, and the CDF 1998 list is explicitly non-exhaustive — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 (QE-2, QE-4, QE-5, QE-6, QE-7, QE-8 "Basis of the grade"), §6 "Grade of each text quoted".
**Problem:** Two systematic hedges are mis-stated. (a) D repeatedly writes "the CDF 1998 list does not name this point separately `[inference]`", which invites the reader (and the site) to think only presence and sacrifice are dogma. The commentary says the opposite of exhaustiveness: it opens §11's examples «Sin ninguna intención de ser exhaustivos» (https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_1998_professio-fidei_sp.html) `[verified]`; the map already records this ("without any intention of completeness"). (b) D's working rule "canon with anathema in a defining decree = dogma" is a theologians' rule of thumb, not the Church's criterion, and it has a counter-example inside the same sessions: Trent XXII can. 9 anathematizes whoever says "the mass ought to be celebrated in the vulgar tongue only; or that water ought not to be mixed with the wine" (Waterworth 1848, archive.org `cu31924029369760`) `[verified: OCR]` — matters of rite, not dogma. The Church's own criterion is the one the commentary gives: truths "definidas como verdades divinamente reveladas por medio de un juicio solemne" of pope or council, or "propuestas infaliblemente por el Magisterio ordinario y universal" (CDF 1998 §5) `[verified]`, with CIC 749 §3 ("no doctrine is understood as defined infallibly unless this is manifestly evident", map §2 `[verified in map]`).
**Fix:** In §1's preamble state both rules once: the 1998 list is illustrative; a Trent canon is graded dogma when it defines a doctrine of faith with a solemn judgment (QE-1 to QE-6, QE-8's can. 5 all qualify, as does XXI can. 1 and 3), and D should say that the Church has not classified these propositions individually, which is different from "the list does not name it". Rename the hedge accordingly: "definición solemne de Trento (canon n); no clasificada por separado por la Iglesia". The grades themselves (dogma for QE-1–QE-6, QE-7's doctrinal half, QE-8's can. 5; disciplina for the rest) are what a Catholic theologian would give; none is too strong or too weak.

### 5 · QE-8 and CV-3: Trent XIII can. 11 is missing, and the site draft omits the exception — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1, QE-8 ("Grado", "Sources", "Texto para el sitio"); §5, CV-3.
**Problem:** D grounds prior confession only in ch. 7's "ecclesiastical usage" and grades it disciplina. The strongest text is can. 11, which D never cites: "this holy Synod ordains and declares that sacramental confession, when a confessor may be had, is of necessity to be made beforehand, by those whose conscience is burthened with mortal sin, how contrite even soever they may think themselves. But if anyone shall presume to teach, preach, or obstinately to assert, or even in public disputation to defend the contrary, he shall be thereupon excommunicated" (Waterworth 1848, Session XIII can. XI) `[verified: OCR]`. CCC 1457 cites exactly these (DS 1647, 1661) and restates the rule with its exception `[verified]`: «Quien tenga conciencia de hallarse en pecado grave que no comulgue el Cuerpo del Señor sin acudir antes a la confesión sacramental (cf DS 1647, 1661), a no ser que concurra un motivo grave y no haya posibilidad de confesarse; y, en este caso, tenga presente que está obligado a hacer un acto de contrición perfecta, que incluye el propósito de confesarse cuanto antes» (https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p2s2c2a4_sp.html). The QE-8 draft's flat «debe confesarse antes de comulgar» is catechetically incomplete without the exception (CV-3 has it; QE-8 does not).
**Fix:** Add can. 11 and CCC 1457 to QE-8's sources; add the exception clause to the QE-8 draft. Grade: "disciplina" is defensible (Trent grounds it in "ecclesiastica consuetudo"; the Church has not classified it), but D should say the council "ordains and declares" it with a censure, not merely "presents it as usage".

### 6 · QE-9: the ecclesial dimension is missing and the intercommunion principle rests on no quoted text — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1, QE-9 ("Texto para el sitio", "Sources").
**Problem:** (a) The draft asserts «compartirla supone la unidad plena en la fe», but UR 15/22 as quoted do not state that principle; the Church states it in *Ecclesia de Eucharistia* 35, 38, 44–46, which D downloaded and does not use. (b) What a Catholic theologian considers essential and absent from all nine statements is "la Eucaristía hace la Iglesia" (CCC 1396; EdE 21, 26): the Eucharist's ecclesial dimension, of which intercommunion is only a consequence. (c) «no admite la intercomunión» is right as the Church's word (CCC 1400), but the Church immediately distinguishes it from administering the sacrament in grave spiritual need (CCC 1401; EdE 45); the draft reads as a flat ban.
**Fix:** Reorder QE-9 as: fuente y cumbre (LG 11) → la Eucaristía hace la Iglesia (CCC 1396 `[verified]`: «La unidad del Cuerpo místico: La Eucaristía hace la Iglesia. Los que reciben la Eucaristía se unen más estrechamente a Cristo. Por ello mismo, Cristo los une a todos los fieles en un solo cuerpo: la Iglesia») → the principle in the Church's words, EdE 35 `[verified]`: «La celebración de la Eucaristía, no obstante, no puede ser el punto de partida de la comunión, que la presupone previamente, para consolidarla y llevarla a perfección» (https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/es/encyclicals/documents/hf_jp-ii_enc_20030417_eccl-de-euch.html) → Orthodox (UR 15) and Reformation communities (UR 22) → EdE 45 `[verified]`: «el objetivo es satisfacer una grave necesidad espiritual para la salvación eterna de los fieles, singularmente considerados, pero no realizar una intercomunión, que no es posible mientras no se hayan restablecido del todo los vínculos visibles de la comunión eclesial.» Also EdE 38 `[verified]`: «No se puede dar la comunión a una persona no bautizada o que rechace la verdad íntegra de fe sobre el Misterio eucarístico […] el Sacramento de su cuerpo y su sangre no permite ficciones.» Grade of EdE: magisterio auténtico (as D already labels it). CV-9's summary of CIC 844 is correct and can stay.

### 7 · OB-1: the Church's deepest answer to "once for all" (CCC 1085) is missing — **should-fix**

**Problem:** The answer is correct and well sourced (Trent XXII chs. 1–2, can. 4; EdE 12; CCC 1364–1367). **Fix:** Insert before CCC 1364: CCC 1085 (es, https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p2s1c1a1_sp.html) `[verified]`: «todos los demás acontecimientos suceden una vez, y luego pasan y son absorbidos por el pasado. ## Verdict

A Catholic theologian would sign §1–§3 and OB-1–OB-4, OB-7 as the Church's position, with the wording fixes above. He would not sign OB-5 and OB-6 as they stand: the first never defines *ex opere operato* (CCC 1128), the second withholds the Church's teaching on the form of the sacrament and so reads as evasion. The grades are right; the way they are justified needs the two corrections in finding 4 so the site never implies that only presence and sacrifice are dogma. The main omission is the ecclesial dimension ("la Eucaristía hace la Iglesia") and the Church's own statement of why intercommunion presupposes unity (EdE 35, 44–46). No neutrality breach: the site never concludes, the Fathers stay inside Catholic documents, and the objections are quoted from the objectors' official texts.
