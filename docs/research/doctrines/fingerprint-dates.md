## Convention

Use the year of **formal promulgation, signature, adoption, or conciliar approval** for institutional documents. Use the year of **composition or first publication** for an authored work. When scholarship gives a range, display `c.` and place the tick at the first defensible conventional year stated below; retain the full range in accessible text. A combined label that represents two separately dated documents must be split rather than assigned a fictitious shared year.

Dates already documented in the Catholic dossiers were checked against the source named there. The dossier path is included in the note; the URL column gives the external source. Access date: 2026-10-20.
