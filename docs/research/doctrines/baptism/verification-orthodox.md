### O-02 — Dositheus XVI Greek definition, water, effects, infants and minister: CONFIRMED

Visually read Schaff II pp. 423–427 (`orthodox-drafting/printed-423.jpg` through `printed-426.jpg`, `schaff-p426.jpg` for actual p. 427). [p. 423](https://archive.org/details/creedschristendo02scha/page/423/mode/1up); [p. 425](https://archive.org/details/creedschristendo02scha/page/425/mode/1up). Matched all six active Greek excerpts: “τὸ ἅγιον βάπτισμα, τὸ διαταχθὲν μὲν παρὰ τοῦ κυρίου”; pure-water sentence “Γίνεται δὲ τὸ βάπτισμα δι’ ὕλης”; effects “Ἀποτελέσματα δὲ τοῦ βαπτίσματος, συνελόντι φάναι”; scriptural wording “ὅστις οὐ μὴ γεννηθῇ ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ πνεύματος”; infant inference “Εἰ δὲ τὰ νήπια ἄνθρωποι”; complete minister/necessity sentence from “Ἀποτελεῖται δὲ διὰ μόνου τοῦ ἱερέως”. Draft spellings/locators agree; straight/curly apostrophes and line breaks normalized. Each Spanish rendering checked directly against Greek, not Latin or Robertson. The effects paraphrase names release from eternal punishment, immortality and God's temples from the same p. 425; it is Dositheus's claim. Pp. 426–427 also teach an indelible character and once-only baptism; add that omitted positive relation for completeness, as a source-attributed paraphrase, not a new doctrinal inference.

### O-03 — Dositheus XV reception and Scripture index: CONFIRMED with bounded limits

Same p. 423 Greek contrasts deficient faith with baptism's integrity, receives those renouncing heresy and joining the Church, and denies rebaptism after full faith. Draft paraphrase is accurate and does not classify modern churches. P. 424 names Acts/household baptisms without verse locators; its saying attributed to Matthew is preserved as the source's attribution, not independently corrected. P. 426 names Paul and one/many with no verse locator. No unprinted reference is imported; all ten numbered index rows involving Philaret are checked under O-04.

### O-06 — Historical authority and approval: CONFIRMED within named witnesses

Schaff p. 444 Greek subscription (`wave1/schaff-457.jpg`) prints 1672, March 16, Jerusalem; this supports the named synodal confession without inventing a current universal reapproval. Philaret p. 445 (`schaff-458.jpg`) title identifies Synod examination/approval and Moscow Synodical Press 1839; editorial note identifies Blackmore's Aberdeen 1845 English translation, originally Slavono-Russian, with Q-numbering supplied by Schaff. The editor's “most authoritative” judgment is not the Church's authority grade. Overbeck printed p. 10 (`overbeck-17.jpg`), [image](https://archive.org/download/cu31924029363094/page/n17.jpg), prints approval 11 March 1643 and restricts it to the Greek-language text, explicitly excluding unread Latin. It is an English printed witness to the approval act, not collation of the original act. Keep that distinction in source grades; no new jurisdictional adoption audit.
