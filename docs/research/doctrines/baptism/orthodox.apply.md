## Conventions and source boundary

- **Column:** Eastern Orthodoxy as delimited in `traditions/orthodox.md` §1, with Greek and Slavic/Russian usages separately attributed. The boundary fixed in `eucharist/orthodox.md` applies here: received councils/canons, the later councils of the map, approved seventeenth-century confessions with approval and Orthodox critique, and Philaret. No Oriental Orthodox position is folded into it.
- **Baptism witnesses:** Constantinople I's creed and canon 7; Nicaea I canon 19; Trullo 95 and the received baptismal canons; Jerusalem 1672, Dositheus XV–XVI; Mogila I.102–103; Philaret Q.287–306. Reception witnesses in the map include Constantinople 1484, the Oros 1755 and Moscow 1667, with different local reception. The presence of an act in the map does not make it universally applied.
- **Source grades:** councils and received canons retain their own grade; Dositheus is the confession of a named synod; Mogila is the approved Greek revision; Philaret is a Russian synodal catechism. Jeremiah's Answers are not replaced by private Gennadius/Kritopoulos confessions. Lucaris is not an adopted Orthodox alternative.
- **Excluded substitutions:** Crete's own teaching is not a doctrine-page voice (DECISIONS 2026-10-15); its reception informs the map. ROC 2020 is mentioned only. A modern parish booklet, an OCA catechetical page or a theologian cannot fill a gap in the normative documents.
- **Greek print:** Dositheus XV–XVI, Schaff II pp. 423–427, [scan](https://archive.org/details/creedschristendo02scha/page/423/mode/1up). Catalogue year 1877 conflicts with the Archive copyright note's 1896; the copy's title-page date is not collated [unverified]. Printed running heads govern page references.
- **English controls:** Robertson 1899 for Dositheus; Overbeck 1898 for Mogila; Blackmore's English Philaret in Schaff II pp. 491–493. English is retained as English, never used as the source of Spanish.

## Qué enseña

### Definition: birth to spiritual and holy life

**Philaret Q.288**, Schaff II p. 491, Blackmore English:

> Baptism is a Sacrament, in which a man who believes, having his body thrice plunged in water in the name of God the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, dies to the carnal life of sin, and is born again of the Holy Ghost to a life spiritual and holy. [verified: scan]

The catechism then prints John 3:5. This is its positive definition, not a rejection of another tradition's baptism.

### QE-1 · The Lord's institution and the Trinity

**Dositheus XVI**, opening, Greek, Schaff II p. 423:

> τὸ ἅγιον βάπτισμα, τὸ διαταχθὲν μὲν παρὰ τοῦ κυρίου, γινόμενον δὲ ἐν ὀνόματι τῆς ἁγίας τριάδος [verified: scan]

> «El santo bautismo, instituido por el Señor y realizado en el nombre de la santa Trinidad». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

The opening continues that baptism is most necessary and cites birth of water and Spirit. The excerpt does not turn its Trinitarian clause into a judgment about particular present-day communities.

### QE-2 · Water and administration

**Dositheus XVI**, Greek, p. 425:

> Γίνεται δὲ τὸ βάπτισμα δι' ὕλης μὲν ὕδατος καθαροῦ καὶ οὐδενὸς ἑτέρου ὑγροῦ. [verified: scan]

> «El bautismo se realiza con agua pura como materia y con ningún otro líquido». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

**Philaret Q.290**, p. 491:

> Trine immersion in water, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. [verified: scan]

The two sources retain their own wording; the shorter Dositheus excerpt is not offered as a complete description of every rite.

### QE-3 · Remission

**Dositheus XVI**, Greek, p. 425:

> Ἀποτελέσματα δὲ τοῦ βαπτίσματος, συνελόντι φάναι, πρῶτον, ἡ ἄφεσις τοῦ προπατορικοῦ πλημμελήματος καὶ ὅσων ἄλλων ἁμαρτιῶν πεπραχὼς ἦν ὁ βαπτισθείς. [verified: scan]

> «Los efectos del bautismo, en resumen, son, primero, la remisión de la falta ancestral y de cuantos otros pecados hubiera cometido el bautizado». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

The paragraph continues with release from eternal punishment, immortality and restoration as temples of God. These remain Dositheus's stated effects (XVI, p. 425), not the drafter's inference from water symbolism.

### QE-4 · Children and sponsors

**Philaret Q.292**, p. 492:

> For the faith of their parents and sponsors, who are also bound to teach them the faith so soon as they are of sufficient age to learn. [verified: scan]

The question asks why children are baptized. Dositheus XVI, pp. 423–424, also treats infants explicitly; its rationale is kept under Por qué rather than recasting baptism as only an adult profession.

### QE-5 · One baptism, repentance afterward

**Philaret Q.303**, p. 493:

> In this sense: that Baptism can not be repeated. [verified: scan]

**Philaret Q.306**, same page, on pardon after baptism:

> There is a way, which is penitence. [verified: scan]

Dositheus XVI, p. 427, likewise distinguishes baptism once rightly administered and the recovery of adoption through repentance. That original page was read during the Eucharist extraction and is reused here without a new source search.

### QE-6 · Reception of converts remains a distinct question

Dositheus XV, p. 423, speaks of reception of people who had received baptism despite deficient faith and says they are not baptized again after acquiring full faith [verified: scan, passage read; not a new general classification of modern churches].

The map also records the 1755 Oros's baptism requirement and the different reception witnesses of 1484 and Moscow 1667 (`traditions/orthodox.md` §§4,9.6; verification I7). Those originals have not been collated here [unverified]. Neither the Oros nor a Slavic reception rule is silently made universal.

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · Christ's command

**Philaret Q.289**, Schaff II p. 491, after the account of John and Christ's baptism:

> Lastly, after his resurrection, he gave the Apostles this solemn commandment: Go ye and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. [verified: scan]

The source prints Matthew 28:19. It distinguishes John's baptism of repentance (Acts 19:4) from the command after the resurrection; the dossier does not merge the two accounts.

### PQ-2 · Water and Spirit

**Dositheus XVI**, p. 423, the source's own scriptural wording:

> ὅστις οὐ μὴ γεννηθῇ ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ πνεύματος, οὐ μὴ εἰσέλθῃ εἰς τὴν βασιλείαν τῶν οὐρανῶν. [verified: scan]

> «Quien no nazca de agua y Espíritu no entrará en el reino de los cielos». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

Philaret Q.288 prints John 3:5 with its own wording. These are quotations from the confessional witnesses; the wording is not silently replaced by a separate Bible translation.

### PQ-3 · Baptism of infants

**Dositheus XVI**, Greek, p. 424:

> Εἰ δὲ τὰ νήπια ἄνθρωποι, εἴπερ καὶ αὐτὰ δεῖται σωτηρίας· δεῖται σωτηρίας, δεῖται καὶ τοῦ βαπτίσματος. [verified: scan]

> «Si los niños son seres humanos, también ellos necesitan salvación; si necesitan salvación, necesitan también el bautismo». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

This is Dositheus's argument, not reasoning supplied by the site. Its wider paragraph also treats ancestral sin and the condition of unbaptized infants; this excerpt does not reproduce that entire historical paragraph or claim a fresh universal-current adoption finding.

**Philaret Q.293**, p. 492:

> In the time of the Old Testament, infants were circumcised when eight days old; but Baptism in the New Testament takes the place of circumcision; consequently infants should also be baptized. [verified: scan]

Q.294 then quotes Colossians 2:11–12. The age in the historical circumcision statement is not a prescription of the day on which Christian baptism must occur.

### PQ-4 · Once, as birth

**Philaret Q.304**, p. 493:

> Baptism is spiritual birth: a man is born but once; therefore he is also baptized but once. [verified: scan]

This answer belongs to Q.303's “one Baptism” in the creed. It is not converted into a universal answer to every contested reception case.

### PQ-5 · Mogila's locus

The bounded search of Overbeck's English confession located I.102–103, under the mysteries; the map's verification F2 confirms these numbers. The received Greek baptism pages were not read in this draft [unverified]. No additional rationale is supplied from the agent's knowledge or from another catechism.

## Cómo se vive

### The profession before baptism

**Philaret Q.291**, p. 492:

> Repentance and faith; for which cause, also, before Baptism they recite the Creed. [verified: scan]

The following verses are Acts 2:38 and Mark 16:16. Q.292 supplies the distinct answer concerning infants, quoted above.

### Sponsors afterward

**Philaret Q.295**, p. 492:

> In order that they may stand sureties before the Church for the faith of the baptized, and after Baptism may take him in charge, to confirm him in the faith. [verified: scan]

The catechism cites Dionysius on the ecclesiastical hierarchy. This is a witness received inside the document, not assignment of an ancient author to the modern Orthodox tradition.

### The appointed minister and necessity

**Dositheus XVI**, Greek, p. 425:

> Ἀποτελεῖται δὲ διὰ μόνου τοῦ ἱερέως, καὶ κατ' ἀνάγκην ἀπρόφασιστον ἔχει γίνεσθαι καὶ δι' ἑτέρου ἀνθρώπου, πλὴν ὀρθοδόξου καὶ σκοπὸν ἔχοντος τὸν ἁρμόδιον τῷ θείῳ βαπτίσματι. [verified: scan]

> «Lo realiza solamente el sacerdote; ante una necesidad ineludible puede realizarlo también otra persona, siempre que sea ortodoxa y tenga la intención apropiada al divino bautismo». [traducción propia asistida por IA] [unverified]

No unstated emergency exception or list of valid baptizers is added to this sentence.

### Exorcism and visible signs

**Philaret Q.297**, p. 492, on the force of exorcism:

> In the name of Jesus Christ, invoked with prayer and faith. [verified: scan]

**Philaret Q.300**, p. 493, on the white garment:

> The purity of the soul, and of the Christian life. [verified: scan]

**Philaret Q.302**, same page, on the procession around the font with a light:

> Spiritual joy, joined with spiritual illumination. [verified: scan]

Q.298–299 treats the sign of the cross; Q.301 the cross placed on the baptized. These are the historical catechism's explanations, not an inventory of every present service book.

### The reception problem is not flattened

The map's Greek/Slavic distinction matters here: Constantinople 1484, the Oros 1755 and Moscow 1667 cannot all be collapsed into a single unlabelled direction. Verification I7 does not confirm the map's earlier claim about Greece in 1932; this dossier does not repeat it as fact.

Acquisition/extraction of those originals and the Euchologion baptism rite remains incomplete in this draft. No claim of silence is made, no modern ritual is supplied from memory, and no observed prevalence is inferred.

## Grado de autoridad

### Councils and confession

The creed and received canons belong to the map's conciliar tier; the confession belongs to Jerusalem 1672. Mogila's approved Greek revision has the patriarchal act of 11 March 1643. Approval of that Greek text is not automatically approval of every later translation (`verification-orthodox.md` F1–F2) [unverified: approval acts not re-read].

### The Orthodox critique remains alongside approval

Archbishop Basil Krivoshein, “Символические тексты в Православной Церкви”, 1961, §VI, is a mapped Orthodox critique of seventeenth-century symbolic texts; it does not function as their repeal. His [Russian text](https://azbyka.ru/otechnik/Vasilij_Krivoshein/simvolicheskie-teksty-v-pravoslavnoj-tserkvi/) was fetched once for these four dossiers. The site's editorial annotation is distinct from his article. No modern hostile annotation is put in his mouth [unverified: web witness].

### Philaret and local acts

Philaret's 1839 catechism is the Russian synodal catechetical witness, with Schaff's editorial Q-numbers. Its doctrinal description is not itself a survey of all autocephalous churches (`verification-orthodox.md` F3) [unverified: approval not re-read].

The reception acts remain patriarchal-synodal/local witnesses with disputed application. No new 2026 adoption audit was authorized or conducted.
