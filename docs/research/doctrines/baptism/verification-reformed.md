### R-03 — English WCF 28 and relation to Spanish: CONFIRMED

Visually read Edinburgh 1894 pp. 91–92 (`confessionoffaith00chur-94.jpg`, `-95.jpg`), [print](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffaith00chur/page/n94/mode/1up). English supports all practice and explanation rows: sign and seal, visible church, water/minister/triune name, pouring or sprinkling, professing adults and infants of one or both believing parents, grace not tied to administration's moment and once-only administration. Spanish is a named historical translation, not newly generated original wording. English “really exhibited and conferred” is represented by that edition's “demostrada y dada”; preserve the historical rendering, not a silent modernization.

### R-04 — WLC 166–167 originals and Spanish: CONFIRMED

Visually read Edinburgh pp. 201–203 (`confessionoffaith00chur-204.jpg` through `-206.jpg`); [p. 202](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffaith00chur/page/n205/mode/1up), [p. 203](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffaith00chur/page/n206/mode/1up). ### R-05 — Scripture apparatus: WRONG (two ranges in one index correction)

All 22 rows visually compared with Edinburgh pp. 91–92, 201–203 and Monterrey pp. 139–145, 204–205. The row “Romans 6:3–5 | WCF 28.1; WLC 167” overstates WCF 28.1's actual apparatus: its English note g cites 6:3–4, and note d cites 6:5 separately. WLC 167 does print 6:3–5 (p. 203 note t); distinguish the locators rather than imply one continuous WCF proof. The Acts 8:37–38 row also needs its witness made explicit: Edinburgh p. 91 prints both; Monterrey p. 142 prints only 37. Remaining locators/ranges checked as selections, not an exhaustive proof inventory; no mismatch found. Hebrew ranges intentionally differ (Edinburgh 9:10,19–22; Monterrey 9:10,19–21). Spanish editorial immersion/Pentecost notes pp. 141–142 remain outside confessional prose.
