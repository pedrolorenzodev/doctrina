## Conventions and source boundary

- **Column boundary:** the churches of the Augsburg Confession, represented through the Book of Concord, with each document identified. The same boundary and subscription distinctions as `../eucharist/lutheran.md` apply; no single present body is silently selected as every Lutheran church's voice.
- **Map categories:** Tier 1 is the closed Book of Concord: preface, three ecumenical creeds, unaltered Augsburg Confession (AC), Apology (Ap), Smalcald Articles (SA), Treatise (Tr), Small Catechism (SC), Large Catechism (LC), Formula of Concord (FC: Ep/SD). The Catalog of Testimonies is an appendix, not the confession proper.
- **Other tiers:** Luther outside the Book of Concord is explanatory, not the column's norm; body-level statements and ecumenical agreements bind their named participants, not all Lutheran churches. Liturgical witnesses are not silently made norms. No later “Office of the Keys” questions are added to SC as if Luther wrote them.
- **Subscription strands:** LCMS accepts the whole confessional collection without reservation; ELCA distinguishes AC as a true witness from other writings as further valid interpretations; LWF's basis especially names AC and SC; CELC uses its own “because” subscription. These are mapped distinctions, not fresh adoption audits [unverified].
- **Original witness:** *Concordia Triglotta*, ed. F. Bente, English F. Bente/W. H. T. Dau, St. Louis: Concordia Publishing House, 1921, archive item `ConcordiaTriglotta`. The scanned copy is a later Northwestern Publishing House second reprint preserving the 1921 title/text; its physical reprint date is not established (L-08). German originals for SA, SC, LC and FC; Latin original for Ap and Tr; AC's German and Latin are both official and are not collapsed into one text.
- **Spanish:** the map's edition inventory identifies no usable public-domain Spanish witness. Each rendering below is [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from the original German or Latin printed in the 1921 witness, unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. Modern Meléndez/CPH and unclear-licence volunteer versions are not copied.
- **Numbers:** Triglot paragraph numbers; Ap IX.51–53 corresponds to Kolb–Wengert IX.1–3 (C-L3; map verification). Ap IX.51–53 are not silently cited as another edition's IX.1–3. SC questions may also be identified by their printed “Zum ersten/andern/dritten/vierten” blocks.
- **Tags:** [verified: scan] identifies the page-image checks in `verification-lutheran.md` L-02–08. Own Spanish remains AI-labelled/unreviewed; body subscription claims are not freshly audited. Long s is transcribed as s and divided words joined.
- **Scratch:** `~/doctrina-scratch/third-order/triglot.txt`, `triglot-pages.xml`, `triglot-metadata.json`, and `triglot-original-<printed-page>.jpg`. The earlier `triglot-p*` image names are offset locator attempts, not reliable printed-page names.
- **Practice:** catechetical prescriptions, not observed current habits. No debate videos or independent arguments; research S1–S3 complete, no import in this run.

## Qué enseña

### Definition: forgiveness, deliverance and salvation

> “Sie wirkt Vergebung der Sünden, erlöst vom Tod und Teufel und gibt die ewige Seligkeit allen, die es glauben, wie die Worte und Verheißungen Gottes lauten.”

— SC IV, “Zum andern”, answer (Triglot IV.6), German original, p. 550 [verified: scan].

> «Obra el perdón de los pecados, libra de la muerte y del diablo y da la salvación eterna a todos los que lo creen, como dicen las palabras y promesas de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### QE-1 · Water joined to God's command and Word

> “Die Taufe ist nicht allein schlecht Wasser, sondern sie ist das Wasser in Gottes Gebot gefaßt und mit Gottes Wort verbunden.”

— SC IV, “Zum ersten”, answer (IV.2), p. 550 [verified: scan].

> «El bautismo no es solamente agua común, sino el agua comprendida en el mandato de Dios y unida a la palabra de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. “Schlecht” here is rendered “común”, not a moral judgment on water.

### QE-2 · Grace offered and children baptized: both AC originals

> “De baptismo docent, quod sit necessarius ad salutem, quodque per baptismum offeratur gratia Dei, et quod pueri sint baptizandi, qui per baptismum oblati Deo recipiantur in gratiam Dei.”

— AC IX.1–2, Latin original, p. 46 [verified: scan].

> «Sobre el bautismo enseñan que es necesario para la salvación, que por el bautismo se ofrece la gracia de Dios y que los niños deben ser bautizados, quienes, ofrecidos a Dios por el bautismo, son recibidos en la gracia de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

> “Von der Taufe wird gelehrt, daß sie nötig sei, und daß dadurch Gnade angeboten werde, daß man auch die Kinder taufen soll, welche durch solche Taufe Gott überantwortet und gefällig werden.”

— AC IX, German original, p. 46 [verified: scan].

> «Sobre el bautismo se enseña que es necesario y que por él se ofrece gracia; que también se debe bautizar a los niños, quienes mediante ese bautismo son entregados a Dios y le son agradables».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The German's “gefällig” and the Latin's “recipiantur in gratiam Dei” are kept in their own formulations.

### QE-3 · The Word and the faith that trusts it

> “Wasser tut's freilich nicht, sondern das Wort Gottes, so mit und bei dem Wasser ist, und der Glaube, so solchem Worte Gottes im Wasser traut.”

— SC IV, “Zum dritten”, answer (IV.10), German, p. 550 [verified: scan].

> «Ciertamente no lo hace el agua, sino la palabra de Dios que está con el agua y junto a ella, y la fe que confía en esa palabra de Dios en el agua».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

The same answer continues:

> “Denn ohne Gottes Wort ist das Wasser schlecht Wasser und keine Taufe; aber mit dem Wort Gottes ist’s eine Taufe, das ist, ein gnadenreich Wasser des Lebens und ein Bad der neuen Geburt im Heiligen Geist”
>
> «Pues sin la palabra de Dios el agua es agua común y no es bautismo; pero con la palabra de Dios es bautismo, es decir, un agua de vida llena de gracia y un baño del nuevo nacimiento en el Espíritu Santo».

— SC IV.10, original German p. 550 [verified: scan], L-02. Spanish **[traducción propia asistida por IA]**, directly from German, unreviewed by Pedro. Apostrophe normalized, long s and divided words joined. Added 2026-10-30 (C-L1) to retain the source's positive new-birth account beside the preceding excerpt.

### QE-4 · God's work, with faith receiving

> “die Taufe aber ist nicht unser, sondern Gottes Werk”.

— LC IV.35, German, p. 740 [verified: scan]; excerpt.

> «pero el bautismo no es obra nuestra, sino de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

The same paragraph says God's works “fordern den Glauben” — «requieren la fe» — German original [verified: scan], Spanish [traducción propia asistida por IA], unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The passage keeps God's action and faith together rather than reducing baptism to either a human work or faithless benefit.

### QE-5 · Infant baptism and the promise

> “Certissimum est enim, quod promissio salutis pertinet etiam ad parvulos.”

— Ap IX.52, Latin original, p. 244 [verified: scan].

> «Pues es completamente cierto que la promesa de salvación pertenece también a los niños pequeños».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The Apology's argument and command follow in Por qué.

### QE-6 · Daily death and rising

> “Es bedeutet, daß der alte Adam in uns durch tägliche Reue und Buße soll ersäuft werden und sterben mit allen Sünden und bösen Lüsten, und wiederum täglich herauskommen und auferstehen ein neuer Mensch, der in Gerechtigkeit und Reinigkeit vor Gott ewiglich lebe.”

— SC IV, “Zum vierten”, answer (IV.12), German, p. 550 [verified: scan].

> «Significa que el viejo Adán en nosotros debe ser ahogado y morir, mediante contrición y arrepentimiento diarios, con todos los pecados y malos deseos; y que, a su vez, salga y resucite cada día un hombre nuevo que viva eternamente ante Dios en justicia y pureza».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · Institution and command

> “Die Taufe ist nichts anderes denn Gottes Wort im Wasser, durch seine Einsetzung befohlen”.

— SA III.5.1, German original, Triglot p. 490 [verified: scan]; opening excerpt.

> «El bautismo no es otra cosa que la palabra de Dios en el agua, mandada por su institución».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The same sentence cites Ephesians 5 and then quotes Augustine; Augustine is a witness quoted by SA, not assigned to a modern Lutheran tradition.

### PQ-2 · The promise reaches infants

Ap IX.52 states its own inference:

> “Igitur necesse est baptizare parvulos, ut applicetur eis promissio salutis iuxta mandatum Christi, Matth. 28, 19: Baptizate omnes gentes.”

— Latin original, Triglot p. 244 [verified: scan].

> «Por tanto, es necesario bautizar a los niños pequeños, para que se les aplique la promesa de salvación conforme al mandato de Cristo, Mateo 28:19: Bautizad a todas las naciones».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. This is the Apology's own “Igitur”, not an inference composed by the dossier.

The paragraph continues that salvation and baptism are offered to men, women, children and infants, and concludes that infants should be baptized. Ap IX.53 then adduces the gift of the Spirit to people baptized as infants as its own further ground (same printed page) [verified: scan]. Its historical claim is attributed to the Apology, not independently adopted as a demographic finding.

### PQ-3 · God himself acts

> “Denn ‘in Gottes Namen getauft werden’ ist nicht von Menschen, sondern von Gott selbst getauft werden.”

— LC IV.10, German original, Triglot p. 734 [verified: scan]; quotation marks normalized.

> «Pues ser bautizado en el nombre de Dios es ser bautizado por Dios mismo, no por seres humanos».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The following sentence explicitly recognizes the human hand while calling baptism God's own work.

### PQ-4 · Faith rests on Word and command

LC IV.56 says:

> “sondern darauf baue ich, daß es dein Wort und Befehl ist”.

— German original, Triglot p. 746 [verified: scan]; excerpt from the confession of the person coming to baptism.

> «sino que me apoyo en que es tu palabra y mandato».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

LC IV.57 says children are brought in hope and prayer that God give them faith; its stated ground for baptizing remains God's command (p. 746) [verified: scan]. The document's distinction is retained alongside SC's requirement of faith.

## Cómo se vive

### Teaching in the household

SC IV's own heading assigns simple teaching to the householder:

> “wie dasselbe ein Hausvater seinem Gesinde soll einfältiglich vorhalten.”

— German original, Triglot p. 550 [verified: scan].

> «como el padre de familia debe enseñarlo con sencillez a los de su casa».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. This is a teaching heading, not permission for every householder to administer every sacrament.

### Administration and recipients

| Aspect | Prescription | Locus |
|---|---|---|
| Element | Water joined to God's command and Word | SC IV.2, p. 550 [verified: scan] |
| Name | Father, Son and Holy Spirit | SC IV.4, p. 550 [verified: scan] |
| Children | Baptize them; grace and promise are not withheld by age | AC IX, p. 46; Ap IX.52, p. 244 [verified: scan] |
| Faith and prayer | Bring children in hope and ask God to give faith | LC IV.57, p. 746 [verified: scan] |
| Continuing life | Daily contrition, repentance and new life | SC IV.12, p. 550 [verified: scan] |
| Return after sin | Return to baptism rather than another washing | LC IV.77–79, p. 750 [verified: scan] |

### Baptism remains; repentance returns to it

> “Darum bleibt die Taufe immerdar stehen”.

— LC IV.77, German, Triglot p. 750 [verified: scan].

> «Por eso el bautismo permanece para siempre».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

LC IV.78 says another pouring with water is not required; IV.79 calls repentance a return to baptism (p. 750) [verified: scan]. The catechism's own connection to daily repentance is reported, without converting it into a claim about the frequency of a present parish's ceremonies.

No description of sponsors, parish scheduling or denominational baptism certificates is inferred from these passages. Those matters require their own mapped practice witness if the owner wants them on the screen.
