## Findings

### 1 · Qué enseña has no statement on what baptism does to the Church: incorporation, common priesthood, the character's ordering to worship — **must-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-1 to QE-10 as a set; QE-8 site draft («consagrado para el culto»); §2.
**Problem:** The CCC sets out the grace of baptism under five heads (1262–1274): remission of sins, new creature, **incorporation into the Church**, sacramental bond of unity, indelible seal. D has four as statements; the third appears only in the definition and, read ecumenically, in QE-10. Yet for the Church it is the heart of baptism's ecclesial meaning: the baptized share the priesthood of Christ, his prophetic and royal mission, and the character orders them to worship. A Catholic theologian would not sign a column on baptism that never says "common priesthood of the faithful", which Vatican II teaches in a dogmatic constitution. It also matters for screen 02: BF&M VII makes baptism "prerequisite to the privileges of church membership" `[verified in D]`, so the columns differ on what baptism does to the Church, and the Catholic side of that difference is missing. QE-8's «consagrado para el culto» has no source under it (CCC 1273 / LG 11 are not quoted).
**Fix:** Add a statement among the default five (it is a primary effect, not "ver más"), e.g. **QE-x · Miembro de la Iglesia y partícipe del sacerdocio de Cristo**. Sources (texto oficial) `[verified]`:
- CEC 1267: «El Bautismo hace de nosotros miembros del Cuerpo de Cristo. […] El Bautismo incorpora a la Iglesia. De las fuentes bautismales nace el único pueblo de Dios de la Nueva Alianza que trasciende todos los límites naturales o humanos de las naciones, las culturas, las razas y los sexos».
- CEC 1268: «Por el Bautismo participan del sacerdocio de Cristo, de su misión profética y real […] El Bautismo hace participar en el sacerdocio común de los fieles».
- CEC 1269–1270 (duties and rights in the Church; «obligados a confesar delante de los hombres la fe», LG 11).
- CEC 1273: «Incorporados a la Iglesia por el Bautismo, los fieles han recibido el carácter sacramental que los consagra para el culto religioso cristiano (cf LG 11)».
- LG 10: «Los bautizados, en efecto, son consagrados por la regeneración y la unción del Espíritu Santo como casa espiritual y sacerdocio santo»; «El sacerdocio común de los fieles y el sacerdocio ministerial o jerárquico, aunque diferentes esencialmente y no sólo en grado, se ordenan, sin embargo, el uno al otro».
- LG 11: «Los fieles, incorporados a la Iglesia por el bautismo, quedan destinados por el carácter al culto de la religión cristiana».
Grade: «Magisterio auténtico (concilio ecuménico) — Vaticano II, *Lumen gentium* 10–11» (D's default for Vatican II). Draft: «Para la Iglesia católica, el bautismo hace a la persona miembro del Cuerpo de Cristo, que es la Iglesia, y la hace participar "del sacerdocio de Cristo, de su misión profética y real" (Catecismo 1268). El carácter bautismal la "consagra para el culto religioso cristiano" (Catecismo 1273).» Move CEC 1273 / LG 11 under QE-8 as the source of «consagrado para el culto». Add 1 P 2:5, 9 (CCC 1268; LG 10) and Ef 4:25 (CCC 1267) to §3. Optional: CEC 1246 / CIC 864 («Es capaz de recibir el Bautismo todo ser humano, aún no bautizado, y solo él») `[verified]`.

### 2 · OB-6: the 1755 *Oros* is cut so that the Orthodox objection reads as about mode only; it denies the validity of baptism given outside the Church, which Trent VII bapt. can. 4 defines — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-6: synthesis, "Their own texts" (1755 *Oros* bullet), Catholic answer, ending canon; QE-7.
**Problem:** D quotes the *Oros* only where it speaks of three immersions and of receiving converts "as being unbaptized". The same text gives a second, ecclesial ground in words: «We, who … were raised in the Orthodox Church … recognize only one Church … It is her Mysteries [i.e. sacraments], and consequently her baptism, that we accept»; «we reject and abhor baptisms belonging to heretics. For they disagree with and are alien to the divine Apostolic dictate. They are useless waters … They give no sanctification to such as receive them, nor avail at all to the washing away of sins» (Metallinos' English, oodegr page in `o/oros1.txt`) `[verified]`. That is a direct denial of a defined Catholic teaching, that baptism given «ab haereticis in nomine Patris, et Filii, et Spiritus Sancti, cum intentione faciendi, quod facit Ecclesia» is true baptism (Trent VII bapt. can. 4, `[verified in D]`; Florence: «imo etiam Paganus & haereticus baptizare potest» `[verified in D]`). As drafted, the synthesis, the answer and the ending canon (can. 3) all address the mode and miss the stronger objection; a Catholic reader would see the Church's actual answer (can. 4) absent from the one page where an official text denies it. D's own rule ("an official text that rejects or denies a proposition the Catholic Church teaches counts") counts it.
**Fix:** Either split OB-6 into OB-6a (mode: 1895 §IV, §VI, §VIII; 1848 §5.xii, §22; Philaret Q.290) and OB-6b (validity of baptism outside the Orthodox Church: 1755 *Oros*), or keep one page with both grounds in the synthesis. Quote the *Oros* sentences above (© English, short quotes; Greek still to read in Mansi 38, D's map gap 4). Catholic answer for 6b, all from texts already in D: Trent VII bapt. can. 4; Florence (*Exsultate Deo*, minister sentence); Lateran IV «a quocumque rite collatum»; CEC 1256 (reason: «la voluntad salvífica universal de Dios … y la necesidad del Bautismo para la salvación»); CIC 869 §2; UR 3 and 22; end on can. 4. The "who raises it" line keeps the Orthodox diversity, and should quote Dositheus XV on the other side: heretics who join the Church «received their valid Baptism with weakness of faith. Wherefore, when they afterwards become possessed of the perfect faith, they are not again baptised» (Robertson p. 139) `[verified: OCR]`. Dositheus XVI's «provided he be Orthodox» for the minister in necessity `[verified in D]` belongs to the same objection (6b), not to "agreements". This also changes Question 8 (see views).

### 3 · QE-3: the hope for infants who die without baptism is reduced to "entrust to mercy"; limbo and the medieval texts are not mentioned — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-3 site draft (last sentence) and grade line; §5 CV-12; Map gaps.
**Problem:** (a) The draft says the Church «los confía a la misericordia de Dios». CEC 1261 goes further, and that further step is the Church's present teaching: «nos permiten confiar en que haya un camino de salvación para los niños que mueren sin Bautismo. Por esto es más apremiante aún la llamada de la Iglesia a no impedir que los niños pequeños vengan a Cristo por el don del santo Bautismo» `[verified]`. The draft is weaker than the Catechism and drops the pastoral consequence the Catechism draws. (b) Readers will ask about limbo. D is silent on it and on the medieval texts that say those who die «sólo con el pecado original» receive «penas diferentes» (Lyon II, Florence *Laetentur caeli*, as the ITC reports them `[verified via ITC n. 22 and note 48]`). A hostile critic will call the silence selective. The Church's own institutions have dealt with exactly this: the ITC study of 2007 says limbo «nunca ha entrado en las definiciones dogmáticas del Magisterio … Sigue siendo por tanto una hipótesis teológica posible» (preliminary note) and that the privation of the vision of God «ha sido durante mucho tiempo doctrina común de la Iglesia, que es algo distinto de la fe de la Iglesia» (n. 40); its conclusion: «serias razones teológicas y litúrgicas para esperar que los niños que mueren sin bautismo serán salvados […] Subrayamos que se trata de motivos de esperanza en la oración, más que de conocimiento cierto» (n. 102); «Ninguna de las consideraciones arriba expuestas puede ser aducida para minimizar la necesidad del Bautismo ni para retrasar su administración» (n. 103) `[verified, es, vatican.va]`. Status per its own note: «aprobado en forma específica por la Comisión … el Cardenal William J. Levada, … una vez recibido el consenso del Santo Padre en la audiencia concedida el 19 de enero de 2007, ha autorizado su publicación» `[verified]`.
**Fix:** (a) Redraft the last sentence of QE-3: «A los niños que mueren sin bautismo la Iglesia los confía a la misericordia de Dios, y el Catecismo dice que la misericordia de Dios y la ternura de Jesús con los niños "nos permiten confiar en que haya un camino de salvación" para ellos; por eso, añade, es "más apremiante aún" no retrasar su bautismo (Catecismo 1261).» Same in CV-12. (b) The ITC is not in the map's Baptism row (map §7.7 describes ITC documents as studies published with the Holy See's non-objection). Per the playbook, propose it as a map gap; if accepted, use it in a "ver más" note only, labelled with its own approval formula and never as a grade: «La Comisión Teológica Internacional (2007) explica que el limbo "nunca ha entrado en las definiciones dogmáticas del Magisterio" y "sigue siendo … una hipótesis teológica posible".» Do not quote the medieval texts from the ITC's footnotes; if Pedro wants them shown, read Florence *Laetentur caeli* in Mansi 31 first.

### 4 · QE-3: the draft omits CCC 1260 (those who do not know the Gospel) — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-3 site draft; OB-1 answer, first paragraph.
**Problem:** The draft lists baptism of blood, the catechumen's desire and infants. It leaves out the case the CCC puts between them: «Todo hombre que, ignorando el Evangelio de Cristo y su Iglesia, busca la verdad y hace la voluntad de Dios según él la conoce, puede ser salvado. Se puede suponer que semejantes personas habrían deseado explícitamente el Bautismo si hubiesen conocido su necesidad» (CEC 1260, quoting GS 22) `[verified]`. Without it, a reader takes the column as saying that outside the rite only martyrs and catechumens can be saved, which is narrower than the Church teaches (LG 16: «Pues quienes, ignorando sin culpa el Evangelio de Cristo y su Iglesia, buscan, no obstante, a Dios con un corazón sincero y se esfuerzan, bajo el influjo de la gracia, en cumplir con obras su voluntad, conocida mediante el juicio de la conciencia, pueden conseguir la salvación eterna» `[verified]`). OB-1's answer cites «Catecismo 1258–1260» but quotes only 1258.
**Fix:** Add to the QE-3 draft after the catechumen: «y de quien, sin conocer el Evangelio, busca la verdad y hace la voluntad de Dios según la conoce, el Catecismo dice que "puede ser salvado" y que "se puede suponer" que habría deseado el bautismo (Catecismo 1260).» Add LG 16 to the sources (magisterio auténtico). Grade line: «Catecismo y Vaticano II; la Iglesia no lo ha clasificado por separado».

### 5 · Christian initiation: baptism's link with Confirmation and the Eucharist is left out of Qué enseña — **should-fix**

### 6 · QE-2's grade presents the infused virtues as only "the more probable opinion" — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-2 "Grado"; §6 row Vienne.
**Problem:** The grade line says the infusion of grace and virtues in infants is taught «by Vienne as "the more probable" opinion». That was the state of the question in 1312. Trent then taught, of justification itself, that with the remission of sins man receives «haec omnia simul infusa … fidem, spem, et caritatem» (Session VI ch. 7, Ayala OCR; Spanish: «se infunden à un mismo tiempo al hombre por Jesu-Cristo, con quien se une, la fe, la esperanza, y la caridad»; Waterworth: "receives, in the said Justification, together with the remission of sins, all these (gifts) infused at once, faith, hope, and charity") `[verified: OCR; page to be fixed by the verifier, ch. 7 is Ayala pp. 71–73 per justification/catholic.md]`, and condemned denying the grace and charity «quae in cordibus eorum per Spiritum Sanctum diffundatur, atque illis inhaereat» (VI can. 11) `[verified in justification/catholic.md QE-2]`; ch. 7 names baptism the instrumental cause. CEC 1266 teaches the infused virtues for every baptized person without qualification. Graded as "more probable", the site understates what the Church now teaches. Applying ch. 7 to infants is `[inference]`, but the CCC does it in words.
**Fix:** Grade line: «Que la justificación infunde la gracia y la caridad: dogma, Trento ses. VI, can. 11; que con ella se infunden la fe, la esperanza y la caridad: enseñado por Trento (cap. 7); que esto vale también para los niños: el Concilio de Vienne (1312) lo eligió "como más probable" y el Catecismo lo enseña sin restricción (1266); la Iglesia no lo ha clasificado por separado.» Keep the Vienne note; it is accurate history.

### 7 · OB-1: the title and synthesis say more than Westminster — **should-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-1 title and first sentence of the answer.
**Problem:** Title: «El bautismo no es necesario para salvarse». No change to the answer's ending if the uniform rule stands, but the honest note should move into a reviewer comment visible to Pedro on the 04 page.

## Views on D's open questions (not decisions)

- **Q2 (does a definition that excludes a Catholic practice count?).** From the Catholic side the answer is plain: the position BF&M VII states is the one Trent defined against (V can. 4; VII bapt. cann. 12–13), so a Catholic reader sees "Christian baptism is the immersion of a believer" as a denial, whether or not it names the Church. Leaving it off the page would read as the Church having no answer to the disagreement readers know best. My view: (b), with a narrower wording than D's: "a text that rejects, denies, or **defines in exclusive terms** ('X is …')". Two cautions. First, apply it by the grammar of each text: BF&M VII and NH XIV are exclusive on both subject and mode; AG 6a ("All who repent and believe … are to be baptized") states a sufficient condition and is exclusive only on mode («commanded … by immersion»), so AG counts for mode only. Second, the synthesis must not go past the text: no "rebaptize", no "infant baptism is invalid"; D's honest note already says this and should stay. The same rule then applies to WCF XXVIII.3 ("Dipping … is not necessary") as an objection to the Orthodox, which is consistent.
- **Q3 (asymmetry):** accept; with finding 2 the Orthodox have two grounds, not one.

## Verdict

A Catholic theologian would sign QE-1, QE-2 (with finding 6), QE-4 to QE-10 and the answers to OB-2 to OB-5 and OB-7 as the Church's position, with the additions above. He would not sign two things as they stand. First, the column never says that baptism incorporates into the Church and makes the baptized share Christ's priesthood, one of the CCC's five heads of baptismal grace and the teaching of *Lumen gentium* 10–11 (finding 1). Second, OB-6 presents the Orthodox objection as about mode only by cutting the 1755 *Oros* before its denial that heretics' baptism gives any grace, so the Catholic answer misses its own defined canon, Trent VII bapt. can. 4 (finding 2). Several drafts are weaker than the Church's documents: the hope for unbaptized infants (CEC 1261), those who do not know the Gospel (CEC 1260), the infused virtues (Trent VI ch. 7 and can. 11), the unity of initiation (CEC 1212, 1275, 1306). One objection title overstates its source (OB-1). The objections otherwise match the Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist, AG and Orthodox official texts read. No neutrality breach beyond one agent-voice argument (finding 11).
