### L-02 — Small Catechism definition and all four answers: CONFIRMED

Visually read German original, *Concordia Triglotta* 1921 pp. 550,552 (`third-order/triglot-original-550.jpg`, `-552.jpg`); [p. 550](https://archive.org/details/ConcordiaTriglotta/page/n830/mode/1up). Matched definition “Sie wirkt Vergebung der Sünden”, first answer “nicht allein schlecht Wasser”, third answer “Wasser tut’s freilich nicht” through its faith clause, and entire fourth answer beginning “Es bedeutet, daß der alte Adam in uns”. Draft quotes and locators agree after long-s normalization and word joining. The householder heading matches. Spanish directly conveys the German; “común” correctly avoids a moral reading of “schlecht”. “Contrición y arrepentimiento” retains an owner-review question, not a certified human translation. Third answer's continuation is essential context: water without the Word is not baptism; with the Word it is a gracious water of life and washing of new birth. Do not let the shortened excerpt imply plain-water insignificance.
