## B. Westminster chapter XXXI: the two numberings

- B1 · CONFIRMED · The 1880 Spanish chapter XXXI has four articles and no magistrates clause. OCR (`doctrinasyreglam00west`, «CAPITULO XXXI Los Sínodos y Concílios»): I «Para mejor gobernar … es deber de los pastores y de otros oficiales de las iglesias particulares … convocar estas asambleas»; II «Pertenece á los sínodos … el decidir ministerialmente cuestiones con respecto de la fé …»; III «Todos los sínodos ó concílios despues de los tiempos de los apostoles … están expuestos á errar, y muchos de ellos han errado …» (p. 155); IV «no deben ocuparse sino en lo que sea puramente eclesiástico». No «magistrado» in connection with synods.
- B2 · CONFIRMED · The Edinburgh 1894 English (`confessionoffaith00chur`, pp. 96–97) prints I «there ought to be such assemblies as are commonly called Synods or Councils» (the convening clause is not in its I), II «As magistrates may lawfully call a synod of ministers, and other fit persons…», III «It belongeth to synods and councils ministerially to determine controversies of faith…», IV «All synods or councils since the apostles' times … may err, and many have erred …».
- B3 · WRONG (incomplete) · The spec (§0.2) lists only 31.1–31.4 for the Edinburgh print; the print has a **fifth** article, V «Synods and councils are to handle or conclude nothing but that which is ecclesiastical…». So the correspondence is: Spanish I = English I + the convening part that the English attaches elsewhere (the Spanish I contains both «debe haber asambleas» and «convocar»), Spanish II = 31.3, III = 31.4, IV = 31.5; the magistrates clause 31.2 has no Spanish counterpart.
- B4 · CONFIRMED · A3's note under the Papacy practice table («the 1880 Spanish ch. XXXI has no magistrates clause: its I says that pastors and other officers must convoke synods, whereas the Edinburgh 1894 English WCF 31.2 says magistrates may…») is true as stated.
