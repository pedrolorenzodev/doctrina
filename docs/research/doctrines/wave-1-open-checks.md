## Task 2. Westminster XXV.6

### 2(a) Church of Scotland

**Answer.** Yes: the General Assembly's Declaratory Act V of 21 May 1986 says the Church "no longer affirms" the antichrist sentence of WCF 25.6 and "does not require its office-bearers to believe" it. It dissociates; it does not amend the Confession's text. Confidence: high.

- Source: Church of Scotland, General Assembly 1986, Act V, "Declaratory Act anent the Westminster Confession of Faith", Edinburgh, 21 May 1986, Session 6. https://churchofscotland.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0014/1823/1986_act_05.pdf (fetched with curl, text extracted 2026-10-19) `[verified]`.
  - "This Church no longer affirms the following contents of the Westminster Confession of Faith:" then lists Chap. 22 s. 7, 24 s. 3, 25 s. 6 and 29 s. 2.
  - Chap. 25, Section 6 as quoted by the Act: "He (the Pope of Rome) is Antichrist, that Man of Sin and Son of Perdition, that exalteth himself in the Church against Christ, and all that is called God."
  - "This Church therefore dissociates itself from the above statements and does not require its office-bearers to believe them."
- Note for the site: the Act's own quotation is not the 1647 wording verbatim (it prints "Antichrist", not "that antichrist"; it bracket-glosses "the Pope of Rome"). Quote the Act's text as the Act prints it.
- Secondary: Church of Scotland, "Statements of the Church's faith", https://churchofscotland.org.uk/about-us/our-faith/statements-of-the-churchs-faith `[snippet]` (search result only; the page text did not return the 1986 Act when fetched, so not relied on).
