**Progress:** DONE 2026-10-17 (unattended run). Parts A-H written. Totals at verdict level (one verdict per numbered item below): 57 CONFIRMED, 18 WRONG, 8 UNCONFIRMABLE (83 items; most items group several quotes, all checked individually). Priority 1 (claims tagged [verified] that carry a quote, number, date or locator) all have a verdict. Ayala scans read by eye: 16 pages (pp. 50, 51, 63, 64, 68, 71, 72, 73, 74, 75, 76, 79, 89, 90, 93, 94), which resolves every OCR-open word listed in the Verifier notes. WebSearch not used; direct fetches only. Only this file was written in the repo; scratch in `just-ver/`.

Legend: CONFIRMED / WRONG (with the correct reading and the tag it should get) / UNCONFIRMABLE (with why). References are to `catholic.md` (D) by its own labels (QE-n, PQ-n, OB-n, CV-n) and line numbers.

Method: texts re-read from the scratch copies (`../just-cat/`, `../dl/`, `../v/`) and from fresh fetches (vatican.va, Wayback availability API, bfm.sbc.net, archive.org full texts, the Latin CIC). Every quote was normalised (case, accents, long s, ligatures, punctuation, hyphenation) and matched by exact string; where the Ayala or Waterworth OCR breaks a word, a word-level alignment was used, the differing words were listed and then read on the page scan. Printed pages come from the running heads of each OCR leaf (Ayala leaf - 21; Waterworth leaf - 273; WCF 1894 leaf - 3), re-checked on scans. Three independent public-domain Latin editions of Trent (1859, 1887, 1890 prints on archive.org) were used to cross-check the Latin wording; where Ayala's own print differs from them, the scan decides. No unlicensed copy was used.

Doubts recorded for the apply step (nobody to ask in an unattended run):

- E6: the section 3 table has Bible words in quotation marks that match no named edition; it depends on Question 1.
- C12 and D12 could not be checked from allowed sources (PCPCU signature and approval chain; AG article).
- Limits of this verification: Latin of Ayala pp. 91-92 (can. 1 end, cann. 3-5), p. 94 beyond can. 11-15 and pp. 83-88, 96-99 were checked on OCR with the word-level alignment, not on the scan; the apply step may keep a claim with evidence.
