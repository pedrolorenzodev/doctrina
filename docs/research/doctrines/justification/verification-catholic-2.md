1. Augustine's date range (PQ-5, OB-6 «Sources» and answer text «c. 400-420»): the repository source says «c. 400-420s» for the anti-Donatist and anti-Pelagian groups together. Either quote it exactly and say it covers both groups, or drop the range. Do not keep «400-420» beside a `[memory]` date of 426-427.
2. OB-5 English title: the BSB has no contiguous "the promise may be guaranteed". Either reword the title to a BSB phrase ("may be guaranteed") or label it as a splice, and correct the claim that it is in BSB wording.
3. OB-6 «Pasajes»: Lamentaciones 5:21 is not a Trent citation (Trent's margin names Zachar. 1 and Joel 2). Tag it `[inference]` or remove it.
4. OB-2: replace «Trento dice que su única causa formal es la justicia de Dios» by a form that does not change Ayala's «santidad» (for example introduce the bracketed quote only, or say it is the Latin *justitia Dei*).
