## Findings

### 1 · OB-3: the Annex is quoted so that the Church appears to accept the *simul* it called "not acceptable" — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4, OB-3, "Respuesta católica", third paragraph; same clause in QE-9 "Sources".
**Problem:** The answer quotes the 1998 Response ("is not acceptable") and then the Annex: «juntos pueden comprender al cristiano como *simul justus et peccator*, a pesar de sus diferentes aproximaciones». Cut this way, a Catholic reader sees the Church contradict herself within one paragraph, on the very point she named as "the major difficulties" (Response n. 1). The Annex says the opposite of a concession: it first states, in the Catholic sense, that the justified **do not remain sinners**, and only then, in a second and different sense (we all sin and pray as the publican), allows the formula. Both "en este sentido" clauses must be shown, or neither. The answer also stops short of the Response's own strongest sentence, which is the Church's actual judgment on the formula as presented in 1998.
**Fix:** Replace the Annex sentence with its two halves, in order. Annex 2A (texto oficial en español, vatican.va 2001) `[verified: Wayback]`: «La justificación, por la que Dios "confiere el don de una nueva vida en Cristo" (DJ 22), es perdón de los pecados y hace justos. […] Somos verdadera e internamente renovados por la acción del Espíritu Santo, permaneciendo siempre dependientes de su acción en nosotros. […] En este sentido, los justificados no siguen siendo pecadores. Aun así nos engañamos si decimos que no tenemos pecado (1Jn 1:8-10) […] En este sentido, Católicos y Luteranos juntos pueden comprender al cristiano como *simul justus et peccator*, a pesar de sus diferentes aproximaciones a este argumento tal como es expresado en DJ 29-30.» Before it, add the Response's conclusion on the formula, n. 1 (English; AI Spanish needed, D's Question 8) `[verified: Wayback]`: "it remains difficult to see how, in the current state of the presentation, given in the Joint Declaration, we can say that this doctrine on 'simul iustus et peccator' is not touched by the anathemas of the Tridentine decree on original sin and justification." Then the Official Common Statement 2 (es) `[verified: Wayback]` closes the sequence honestly: the Annex «acredita ulteriormente el consenso alcanzado en la Declaración conjunta» with regard to «los interrogantes planteados» by the 1998 Response. Order on the 04 page: Trent V can. 5 → CCC 1264 → 1998 Response → Annex 2A both senses → can. 25.

### 2 · Qué enseña never says what justification gives: adoption as sons, participation in the divine life, conversion — **must-fix**

**Where:** §1, "Definición breve" and the site draft of the definition; QE-1 to QE-9 as a set; §2.
**Problem:** Every statement mirrors a Tridentine canon against a Reformation thesis. That is what Trent's canons do, but the Church's own positive account of justification is missing from the column: the CCC's summary puts at the centre that grace makes us **adoptive children** and **partakers of the divine nature**, introducing us «en la intimidad de la vida trinitaria», and that justification begins as **conversion**. The 1998 Response itself names this as the gap in the JDDJ's presentation and asks for it: "As for the various ways in which Paul describes man's new condition, as mentioned in the Joint Declaration, we could add the categories of sonship and of heirs. (Gal 4:4-7; Rom 8:14-17)" (n. 7) and "the gift of the Holy Spirit which makes us capable of living according to our condition as children of God" (n. 8) `[verified: Wayback]`. D reproduces the gap the Church criticized. D also chooses ch. 7 as the definition and never quotes ch. 4, which is the one Trent itself titles "descriptio Justificationis impii", and it is there that adoption appears. A Catholic theologian would not sign the definition as "the Church's" without it; the Athanasius quote in PQ-5 is the only trace.
**Fix:** (a) Definition: put Trent VI ch. 4 beside ch. 7. Latin (Ayala p. 67) `[verified: OCR]`: «Justificationis impii descriptio insinuatur, ut sit translatio ab eo statu, in quo homo nascitur filius primi Adae, in statum gratiae, et adoptionis filiorum Dei, per secundum Adam Jesum Christum, Salvatorem nostrum». Español (Ayala p. 67; OCR «eri» read «en») `[verified: OCR]`: «viene à ser la traslacion ó tránsito de aquel estado en que nace el hombre hijo del primer Adan, al estado de gracia y de adopcion de los hijos de Dios por el segundo Adan Jesu-Cristo nuestro Salvador». English (Waterworth p. 32) `[verified: OCR]`: "a translation, from that state wherein man is born a child of the first Adam, to the state of grace, and of the adoption of the sons of God, through the second Adam, Jesus Christ, our Saviour." Redraft: «Para la Iglesia católica, la justificación es el paso del pecado "al estado de gracia y de adopción de los hijos de Dios" por Jesucristo (Trento, ses. VI, cap. 4). Comienza con la conversión que obra la gracia del Espíritu Santo; no es solo el perdón de los pecados, sino también "santificación y renovación del interior del hombre" (Catecismo 1989)…» (b) Add a statement, by default among QE-1–QE-5 (it is the positive core, not "ver más"): «La gracia: hijos adoptivos, partícipes de la vida de Dios», sourced on CCC (texto oficial) `[verified]`: 1996 «La gracia es el favor, el auxilio gratuito que Dios nos da para responder a su llamada: llegar a ser hijos de Dios (cf Jn 1, 12-18), hijos adoptivos (cf Rm 8, 14-17), partícipes de la naturaleza divina (cf 2 P 1, 3-4), de la vida eterna (cf Jn 17, 3)»; 1997 «La gracia es una participación en la vida de Dios. Nos introduce en la intimidad de la vida trinitaria […] Como "hijo adoptivo" puede ahora llamar "Padre" a Dios, en unión con el Hijo único»; 2000 (gracia santificante o habitual / gracias actuales); 1989 «La primera obra de la gracia del Espíritu Santo es la conversión, que obra la justificación»; summary 2017, 2021. Grade: CCC as catechesis; the adoption clause is Trent ch. 4 (chapter, «enseñado por Trento; la Iglesia no lo ha clasificado por separado»). (c) Athanasius in PQ-5 then reads as the CCC's illustration of a stated teaching, not as the only place the idea appears.

### 3 · The role of baptism is kept out of Qué enseña and is thin in Cómo se vive — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 "Considered and left out" (baptism as instrumental cause); QE-2, QE-3 site drafts; §5 CV-1.
**Problem:** D leaves baptism to the baptism doctrine. For the Church it is constitutive of justification itself: Trent names it the instrumental cause (ch. 7, `[verified in D]`), ch. 4 says the translation "cannot be effected without the laver of regeneration, or the desire thereof", and the CCC says «La justificación es concedida por el Bautismo, sacramento de la fe» (1992; 2020: «Nos es concedida mediante el Bautismo») `[verified]`. This is also the point where the Catholic column differs most visibly from the Baptist and Reformed columns on screen 02; leaving it to a sentence in the definition under-presents it. CV-1 speaks only of adult preparation, though most Catholics are justified as infants, and says nothing of the desire of baptism, which ch. 4 itself states.
**Fix:** (a) One sentence in the QE-3 draft (faith and baptism go together in Trent's "sacramento de fe"): «La causa instrumental de la justificación es el bautismo, "sacramento de fe, sin la qual ninguno jamas ha logrado la justificacion" (Trento, ses. VI, cap. 7); desde la promulgación del Evangelio, el paso a la gracia no se da "sin el Bautismo, ó sin el deseo de [OCR: «cl»; read «él»]" (cap. 4, Ayala p. 67 `[verified: OCR]`).» (b) CV-1: add infants and desire. CCC 1250 (es) `[verified]`: «los niños necesitan también el nuevo nacimiento en el Bautismo […] La pura gratuidad de la gracia de la salvación se manifiesta particularmente en el bautismo de niños.» CCC 1258–1259 `[verified]`: the baptism of blood and «el deseo del Bautismo, produce los frutos del Bautismo sin ser sacramento»; catechumens who die before baptism: «el deseo explícito de recibir el Bautismo, unido al arrepentimiento de sus pecados y a la caridad, les asegura la salvación». Keep the full sacramental doctrine in the baptism dossier; the "pura gratuidad" line belongs here, because it answers the reader who thinks Catholic justification starts from human effort.

### 4 · "Justified by faith": the column never says what the Church means by faith — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-3 (site draft and sources); §4 OB-1, "Respuesta católica".
**Problem:** OB-1 and can. 12 turn on the meaning of "faith". D quotes can. 12 (faith is not "only confidence") but never the Church's positive account, so the reader is left with the Reformation caricature that Catholic faith is bare intellectual assent. Trent ch. 6 defines the faith of the preparation, and it includes hope and confidence ("confiding that God will be propitious to them for Christ's sake"); the CCC says faith is «ante todo una adhesión personal del hombre a Dios». DJ 36, which D already quotes in OB-5, gives the Catholic side with Vatican II's «Tener fe es encomendarse plenamente a Dios».
**Fix:** Add to QE-3's sources and one clause to its draft and to OB-1's second paragraph. Trent VI ch. 6 (Ayala p. 69) `[verified: OCR]`: «conciben la fe por el oido, y se inclinan libremente à Dios, creyendo ser verdad lo que sobrenaturalmente ha revelado y prometido; y en primer lugar, que Dios justifica al pecador por su gracia adquirida en la redencion por [OCR: «Fesu»]-Christo»; English (Waterworth p. 33): "they are raised unto hope, confiding that God will be propitious to them for Christ's sake" `[verified: OCR]`. CCC 150 (es, https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p1s1c3a1_sp.html) `[verified]`: «La fe es ante todo una adhesión personal del hombre a Dios; es al mismo tiempo e inseparablemente el asentimiento libre a toda la verdad que Dios ha revelado.» CCC 1814 (es, …/p3s1c1a7_sp.html) `[verified]`: «Por la fe "el hombre se entrega entera y libremente a Dios" (DV 5) […] "El justo […] vivirá por la fe" (Rm 1, 17). La fe viva "actúa por la caridad" (Ga 5, 6).» CCC 1815 `[verified]` restates Trent ch. 7 in modern words: «privada de la esperanza y de la caridad, la fe no une plenamente el fiel a Cristo ni hace de él un miembro vivo de su Cuerpo».

### 7 · Merit is presented without its ground in adoption and charity — **should-fix**

### 9 · QE-6: the grade covers more than the canons define — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1, QE-6, "Grado" and title.
**Problem:** The title and draft state positively that «nadie puede saber con certeza de fe que ha recibido la gracia», graded Dogma on cann. 13–16. That sentence is ch. 9. The canons condemn narrower propositions: that certainty of forgiveness is *necessary* for all (can. 13), that one is absolved *by this alone*, believing it for certain (can. 14), that the reborn is *bound of faith* to believe himself predestined (can. 15), and that one has *absolute and infallible certainty* of perseverance without special revelation (can. 16) `[verified in D; Waterworth p. 46]`. ### 12 · QE-7 and CV-2: recovery of grace is stated without "at least in desire" and perfect contrition — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-7 site draft; §5 intro paragraph 2 and CV-2.
**Problem:** The drafts say grace lost by mortal sin is recovered «en el sacramento de la Penitencia». Trent VI ch. 14, which D quotes, says more: the penitence of the fallen includes «la confesion sacramental de ellos, à lo menos en deseo para hacerles à su tiempo» (Ayala p. 84) `[verified: OCR]`; "at least in desire, and to be made in its season" (Waterworth p. 41) `[verified: OCR]`; and the eternal punishment is remitted «either by the sacrament, or by the desire of the sacrament» (ibid.). **Fix:** Add to QE-7's draft and CV-2: «Para la Iglesia, la contrición perfecta, nacida del amor a Dios, "obtiene también el perdón de los pecados mortales, si comprende la firme resolución de recurrir tan pronto sea posible a la confesión sacramental" (Catecismo 1452).» CCC 1452 (es, https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p2s2c2a4_sp.html) `[verified]`. Source line: Trent VI ch. 14 «à lo menos en deseo».

**Where:** §5.

Esto es propio del sacramento de la Reconciliación.»

### 18 · Supererogation (D's Question 13) — **minor, view only**

**Where:** §4 OB-4, note on WCF XVI.4.
**View:** D is right that Trent VI does not use the word and that the treasury of merits belongs to indulgences. But the Church's own frame for "more than God requires" is the evangelical counsels, not indulgences: CCC 1973 (es, https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p3s1c3a1_sp.html) `[verified]`: «Más allá de sus preceptos, la Ley nueva contiene los consejos evangélicos. La distinción tradicional entre mandamientos de Dios y consejos evangélicos se establece por relación a la caridad»; 1974: they «manifiestan la plenitud viva de una caridad que nunca se ve contenta por no poder darse más». That CCC 1973 is the Church's answer to WCF XVI.4 is `[inference]`: the CCC does not name the Westminster text or use "supererogación". ## Verdict

A Catholic theologian would sign QE-1, QE-2, QE-4, QE-5, QE-7 and QE-8 as the Church's teaching, and the answers to OB-2, OB-4, OB-6 and OB-7, with the additions above. He would not sign two things as they stand. First, OB-3 quotes the 1999 Annex so that the Church seems to accept the *simul* she called "not acceptable", by dropping the Annex's own «en este sentido, los justificados no siguen siendo pecadores» (finding 1). Second, the column presents justification almost wholly as Trent's answers to the Reformers and leaves out what the Church's Catechism and her 1998 Response put at its centre: adoption as children, participation in the divine life, conversion (finding 2). The other omissions are the role of baptism, the meaning of faith, merit's ground in adoption and charity, perfect contrition, and the criterion question. Several agreement quotes are cut just before the clause that carries the Catholic sense (findings 1, 5, 10). The grades are right except QE-6, which covers a chapter sentence. The objections match the Lutheran, Reformed and Baptist official texts; the one miss is New Hampshire XI, which D's own rule counts. No neutrality breach beyond one sentence in the agent's voice (finding 14).
