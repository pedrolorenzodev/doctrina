## Conventions and source boundary

- **Column boundary:** the four families in the Baptist map, not a confession binding every Baptist. A = Particular/Reformed; B = General/Free Will; C = Southern Baptist/convention; D = bodies organized around principles. The owner's work order requires these strands to remain visible; it does not settle which the eventual screen presents.
- **A documents:** First London 1644, with the separately printed 1646 revision; Somerset 1656; Second London 1677/1689 (2LCF); Philadelphia 1742, whose additions are printed by McGlothlin; Baptist Catechism c. 1693/1695.
- **B documents:** Standard Confession 1660; Orthodox Creed 1678/9 (OC); northern Freewill Treatise 1834 with explicitly distinguished 1869 variants; NAFWB Treatise 2026. The northern historical text is not substituted for the present NAFWB text.
- **C documents:** New Hampshire in Brown's **1853** eighteen-article form (NH); Abstract of Principles 1858 (AP), an institutional standard; BF&M 1925, 1963 and 2000, the last with the 2023 amendment to VI. McGlothlin's NH presentation combines a base text and variant notes: quotations below explicitly reconstruct 1853 from those notes. It is not a scan of an 1833 original.
- **D documents:** BUGB Declaration of Principle, current official web text; BWA Beliefs Statement, supplementary federation testimony, whose member-body subscription status the map does not establish. Neither is silently treated as an SBC confession.
- **Regional controls:** the mapped UEBE Confession and Principles, CEBA 1987, CNBM doctrines/principles, CNBV distinctive doctrines, Paraguay's BF&M text, and the other mapped subscription/principles evidence are kept distinct from their English-language relatives. Similarity does not prove adoption. Coverage records acquisition limits rather than declaring a body silent from its relative's text.
- **Edition:** historical English quotations use W. J. McGlothlin, *Baptist Confessions of Faith* (Philadelphia: American Baptist Publication Society, 1911), with page and article. His historical introductions are editorial evidence, not confessional teaching. Page images: [Archive.org scan](https://archive.org/details/baptistconfessio00mcgl).
- **Spanish:** the map identifies no public-domain Spanish edition for these historical confessions. UEBE and circulating 1689 translations have no established reusable public-domain licence. Spanish renderings below are made from the original English and labelled **[traducción propia asistida por IA]**, unreviewed by Pedro. Official modern Spanish excerpts, when used, are labelled separately.
- **Tags:** `[verified: scan]` means that particular English wording was read on the printed page during drafting; it does not certify the dossier. `[unverified]` marks web transcriptions, OCR-only material, edition/licence details not independently checked, and translations. There is no verification, critic or apply pass.
- **Copyright:** historical English print is treated as public-domain material under the mapped edition boundary; current BF&M, NAFWB and contemporary declarations receive only short excerpts and official links. The 1963 US nonrenewal claim is not permission for worldwide full-text reuse.
- **Scripture:** only references supplied by the Baptist documents are listed. Adjacent references in a combined article are not automatically assigned to one clause. No independent biblical argument is added.

### A — Second London: the sufficient rule

> “The Holy Scripture is the only sufficient, certain, and infallible rule of all saving Knowledge, Faith and Obedience”
>
> «La Sagrada Escritura es la única regla suficiente, cierta e infalible de todo conocimiento salvífico, fe y obediencia». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.1, McGlothlin p. 227. English [verified: scan]; the scanned page is less sharp than the adjacent pages, and the tag applies to this opening clause only. The affirmation of Scripture's role comes first.

> “The whole Councel of God concerning all things necessary for his own Glory, Mans Salvation, Faith and Life, is either expressely set down or necessarily contained in the Holy Scripture”
>
> «Todo el consejo de Dios acerca de cuanto es necesario para su propia gloria, la salvación del ser humano, la fe y la vida está expresamente expuesto o necesariamente contenido en la Sagrada Escritura». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.6, p. 229. English [verified: scan]. The wording is **“necessarily contained”**, not the differently worded Westminster formulation imported by memory.

### A — First London: written canonical Scripture

> “but onely the word of God contained in the Canonicall Scriptures”
>
> «sino sólo la palabra de Dios contenida en las Escrituras canónicas». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

First London **1644** VII, McGlothlin p. 176, closing clause of its rule of knowledge, faith, obedience, worship and Christian duties. English [verified: scan]. The sentence's preceding list rejects human inventions and unwritten traditions; this is the historic confession's language, not a description of another tradition's position.

> “In this written Word God hath plainly revealed whatsoever he hath thought needfull for us to know, beleeve, and acknowledge, touching the Nature and Office of Christ”
>
> «En esta Palabra escrita Dios ha revelado claramente cuanto ha considerado necesario que conozcamos, creamos y reconozcamos acerca de la naturaleza y el oficio de Cristo». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

First London 1644 VIII, p. 176. English [verified: scan]. No later version is silently substituted for its wording.

### B — Orthodox Creed: Scripture and its own reception of creeds

> «contiene todas las cosas necesarias para la salvación; por eso no debe exigirse a nadie como artículo de la fe cristiana aquello que no se lea en ella ni pueda probarse por ella». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXVII, McGlothlin p. 152. English [verified: scan]. The grammatical subject is Holy Scripture in the preceding part of the article.

> «Los tres credos, a saber, el niceno, el de Atanasio y el de los apóstoles, como se los llama habitualmente, deben ser plenamente recibidos y creídos». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXVIII, p. 153. English [verified: scan]. The article's own explanation follows in Por qué; receiving these creeds is not erased by a generic account of Baptist noncreedal identity.

## Por qué

### Its author, rather than an external grant of authority

> “dependeth not upon the testimony of any man, or Church; but wholly upon God (who is truth it self) the Author thereof; therefore it is to be received, because it is the Word of God”
>
> «no depende del testimonio de ningún ser humano ni Iglesia, sino enteramente de Dios, que es la verdad misma, su autor; por eso ha de recibirse, porque es la Palabra de Dios». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.4, McGlothlin p. 229. English [verified: scan]. This is the confession's stated reason for reception, not the site's judgment about the canon's historical identification.

### Its own account of assurance

> “our full perswasion, and assurance of the infallible truth, and divine authority thereof, is from the inward work of the Holy Spirit, bearing witness by and with the Word in our Hearts”
>
> «nuestra plena persuasión y seguridad de su verdad infalible y autoridad divina procede de la obra interior del Espíritu Santo, que da testimonio por la Palabra y con ella en nuestros corazones». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.5, p. 229. English [verified: scan]. Its preceding sentences acknowledge the church's testimony and the text's excellencies; the excerpt must not be made to deny that acknowledgement.

### Interpretation and the supreme judge

> “The infallible rule of interpretation of Scripture is the Scripture it self”
>
> «La regla infalible de interpretación de la Escritura es la Escritura misma». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.9, pp. 230–231. English [verified: scan]. Its continuation directs difficult places to other passages speaking more clearly.

> “can be no other but the Holy Scripture delivered by the Spirit”
>
> «no puede ser otro que la Sagrada Escritura entregada por el Espíritu». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.10, p. 231, subject: supreme judge of religious controversies, councils, ancient writers, human doctrines and private spirits. English [verified: scan]. No Father is thereby assigned to a modern Baptist confession.

### OC's reason for receiving the three creeds

> “they may be proved, by most undoubted authority of holy scripture”
>
> «pueden probarse por la autoridad más indudable de la Sagrada Escritura». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXVIII, p. 153, excerpt from its stated explanation. English [verified: scan]. Its own structure receives the creeds on this basis and directs their catechetical teaching; the dossier supplies no argument reconciling it with another Baptist body's formulations.

### Sparse historic formulations

Standard 1660's title cites Acts 24:14 and its XIII requires continuance in apostolic doctrine; Somerset's articles repeatedly give scriptural proofs. Neither is expanded here into an unprinted chapter on scriptural sufficiency. Philadelphia's additional chapters do not add a new Scripture chapter; its relation to London is McGlothlin's explicitly identified editorial testimony (p. 297). ## Cómo se vive

### Reading, translation and ordinary means

> “those things which are necessary to be known, believed, and observed for Salvation, are so clearly propounded, and opened in some place of Scripture or other, that not only the learned, but the unlearned, in a due use of ordinary means, may attain to a sufficient understanding of them”
>
> «las cosas necesarias para conocer, creer y observar para la salvación están tan claramente propuestas y expuestas en algún lugar de la Escritura que tanto los instruidos como los no instruidos, usando debidamente los medios ordinarios, pueden alcanzar una comprensión suficiente de ellas». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.7, McGlothlin p. 230. English [verified: scan]. Its opening expressly says not all things are equally plain or clear to everyone; clarity is quoted with its own scope.

> “they are to be translated into the vulgar language of every Nation, unto which they come”
>
> «han de traducirse a la lengua común de cada nación a la que lleguen». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

2LCF 1.8, p. 230. English [verified: scan]. The paragraph gives the people's right and obligation to read and search the Scriptures as the reason.

> “all people ought to have them in their mother tongue, and diligently, and constantly to read them in their particular places and families”
>
> «todos deben tenerlas en su lengua materna y leerlas diligente y constantemente en sus lugares y familias». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXVII, p. 152. English [verified: scan]. Its XXXVIII additionally directs catechetical exposition of the creeds in Christian families (p. 153).

### Assemblies are not silently removed

> “it being of divine authority”
>
> «siendo de autoridad divina». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

OC XXXIX, p. 154, referring to legally convened general councils/assemblies of bishops, elders and brethren through church representatives. English [verified: scan]. The same article gives them lawful authority to hear, determine and excommunicate. Its language stands beside XXXVII's scriptural supremacy; the dossier does not reason out a harmonization or erase one clause.

### Catechesis and confessional accountability

> “Confessions are only guides in interpretation”
>
> «Las confesiones son sólo guías para la interpretación». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** [unverified]

1925 confessional statement, point 4, quoted in the official [BF&M 2000 preamble](https://bfm.sbc.net/preamble/). English [unverified], short excerpt. The rest of the point restricts authority over conscience; the convention's adoption/accountability framework remains body-specific.
