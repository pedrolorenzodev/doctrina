## Qué enseña

### Scripture as the standard for conduct, creeds and opinions

> “the supreme standard by which all human conduct, creeds, and opinions should be tried”
>
> «la norma suprema por la que deben examinarse toda conducta humana, los credos y las opiniones». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** — unreviewed by Pedro.

NH 1853 I, McGlothlin p. 302, unchanged clause in the base/variant presentation. [Page image](https://archive.org/download/baptistconfessio00mcgl/page/n315.jpg). English [verified: scan], B-02. Its subject is the Bible introduced on p. 301; no wider Baptist-family composite definition is supplied.

### Current BF&M: revelation, truth and Christ

> “supreme standard”
>
> «norma suprema». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** — unreviewed by Pedro.

BF&M 2000 I, current official publisher print, p. 1 / PDF p. 3. [Official article](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/#i), [print](https://bfm.sbc.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BFM2000.pdf#page=3). English [verified: scan], B-04; short copyrighted excerpt.

Site draft (attributed paraphrase): **Para Fe y Mensaje Bautistas, la Biblia fue escrita por personas inspiradas por Dios y es su revelación al ser humano. Dios es su autor, la salvación su fin y la verdad sin mezcla de error su contenido. La Escritura es la norma que examina la conducta, los credos y las opiniones religiosas; toda ella testimonia a Cristo, centro de la revelación divina.** Source: current I, print p. 1, B-04. This is a paraphrase, not a quotation or a full-text substitute.

## Por qué

### Divine inspiration, author and purpose

> “the Holy Bible was written by men divinely inspired, and is a perfect treasure of heavenly instruction”
>
> «la Santa Biblia fue escrita por personas divinamente inspiradas y es un tesoro perfecto de instrucción celestial». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** — unreviewed by Pedro.

NH I, McGlothlin p. 301; [image](https://archive.org/details/baptistconfessio00mcgl/page/301/mode/1up). English [verified: scan], B-02. The excerpt starts after the bracketed introductory “that”; p. 302 continues with God as author, salvation as end, truth, judgment, Christian union and normative testing. The source's rationale is retained without importing 2LCF's detailed illumination/clarity/interpretation account.

Current BF&M I gives its own divine-revelation and Christ-focused rationale (B-04, attributed paraphrase above). **Sparse:** NH I and these BF&M articles/preamble do not offer the excluded families' extended accounts of inward assurance, original languages, ordinary means or assemblies. Their omission here is a bounded source limit, not a statement that Baptists never discuss them.

## Cómo se vive

### Confessions as interpretive guides and doctrinal accountability

> “Confessions are only guides in interpretation”
>
> «Las confesiones son sólo guías para la interpretación». **[traducción propia asistida por IA]** — unreviewed by Pedro.

BF&M current preamble, reproducing the 1925 point 4; printed p. 21 / PDF p. 23. [Official preamble](https://bfm.sbc.net/preamble/), [print](https://bfm.sbc.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BFM2000.pdf#page=23). English [verified: scan], B-05; short copyrighted excerpt.

For the preamble, Scripture has sole authority for faith and practice; confessions guide interpretation without authority over conscience. The same named convention account calls confessions instruments of doctrinal accountability and allows their revision; freedom does not erase the positive confessional role. Attributed paraphrase of p. 21, B-05.
