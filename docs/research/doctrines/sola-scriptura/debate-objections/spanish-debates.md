## Transcript evidence ledger

### Scripture is the only infallible or final norm; teachers and tradition are fallible supports

- Edgar Pacheco defines Sola Scriptura as Scripture being “la única norma que se autovalida [...] es infalible” and says there is no comparable reference to bishops, councils or popes ([9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J5DGTTVy8Gs&t=585s)). At 5:21–8:53 he distinguishes this from solo biblicism and names Scripture, tradition, reason and experience in Methodist theology.
- Carlos Veloz: “el principio [...] determina que la única palabra infalible es la palabra escrita de Dios” ([18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C215NvY2XRo&t=1098s)); historical tradition is acknowledged but “pasamos todo por el filtro de la Escritura” at 20:38–21:12.
- José says Scripture is the “máxima autoridad” and “la única autoridad infalible”, while magisterium and tradition are fallible secondary supports ([8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=518s)).
- Independent count: 3. Catholic answers are heard in all three programs. Official anchors pending final write.

### Scripture contains what is necessary for salvation and every good work

- José says Scripture, with the Spirit, reason and other supports, is sufficient “para salvación, para glorificar a Dios por medio de buenas obras y para alcanzar el gozo” ([14:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=894s)). At 1:41:05–1:43:33 he builds the case from 2 Timothy 3:14–17 and 1 John 1:4.
- Edgar uses 2 Timothy 3:16 to establish inspiration and Scripture's unique norm, but the debate passage located does not separately make the full sufficiency inference.
- Carlos's exchange concerns whether an unstated moral conclusion can be drawn from Scripture; it does not state the positive sufficiency thesis.
- Eduardo argues that 2 Timothy 3 makes the man of God complete and therefore in need of no infallible addition ([1:32:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x27TxdlN10M&t=5569s)); he restates the sufficiency claim at 1:37:15–1:39:27.
- Independent count: 2 clear, 1 related. Below threshold as a separate objection.

### No present claimant can demonstrate possession of the apostles' oral teaching; filter tradition through Scripture

- José accepts that apostolic oral teaching was infallible, then asks: “¿cómo me demostrás vos [...] que la religión papista tiene la doctrina que los apóstoles enseñaron oralmente?” ([24:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=1456s)). Since he did not hear the apostles, he says he adheres to the written teaching at 35:41–35:57 and 43:08–43:31.
- Edgar challenges how alleged oral tradition leading to papal infallibility can be traced at 38:40–43:39 in the 229K debate.
- Carlos accepts historical tradition but filters it through Scripture and denies an infallible magisterium (20:38–21:12).
- Independent count: 3 when phrased as the fallibility/non-verifiability of later tradition rather than a denial that apostolic oral preaching ever existed.

### Canon: fallible historical evidence can yield warranted recognition

- José answers the Catholic canon challenge with historical method, textual criticism, testimony and internal evidence ([1:14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=4474s)); he expressly admits these supports and his reasoning are fallible.
- Edgar says the agreed canon need not be reopened in that debate and argues that the Church recognizes rather than grants the books' authority (15:04–18:59).
- Eduardo appeals to harmony, fulfilled prophecy, historical testimony and the Church's recognition, while denying that the Church is itself the canon ([29:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x27TxdlN10M&t=1775s)–44:56).
- Independent count: 3.

## Objections heard

### PASS 1 · “La Escritura es la única norma infalible; tradición, magisterio y maestros son ayudas falibles”

**Popular wording heard.** Edgar: «la Escritura es la única norma que se autovalida […] es infalible»; Carlos: «la única palabra infalible es la palabra escrita de Dios»; José: «la máxima autoridad» y «la única autoridad infalible». None presents *sola Scriptura* as refusing all teachers or tradition.

**Independent people.** Three: Edgar Pacheco, Methodist ([9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J5DGTTVy8Gs&t=585s)); Carlos Veloz, evangelical Christian ([18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C215NvY2XRo&t=1098s)); José, denomination unstated ([8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=518s)). Carlos appears inside a Catholic reaction program, but the counted words are his recorded direct statement, not the commentator's paraphrase.

**Catholic answer heard.** Santiago Alarcón answers Edgar and José that the apostolic deposit was transmitted orally and in writing, and that Scripture does not state that only the written form is infallible. Byron answers Carlos that the dispute concerns who can decide authoritatively between incompatible readings. Byron's stronger claim that only an infallible external interpreter could reach the moral conclusion in his example is an apologetic claim, not an official Catholic text, and is not used as the answer below.

**Catholic primary-source answer.** *Dei Verbum* 9 says that Scripture and Tradition flow from the same divine source and that «la Iglesia no deriva solamente de la Sagrada Escritura su certeza acerca de todas las verdades reveladas»; DV 10 entrusts authentic interpretation to the living Magisterium and immediately says it «no está sobre la palabra de Dios, sino que la sirve»; DV 21 calls Scripture, together with Tradition, «la regla suprema de su fe». [Official Spanish text](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_sp.html), nn. 9–10, 21.

**Objecting-tradition official anchor.** Formula of Concord, Epitome, “Rule and Norm” ¶1: «la única regla y norma según la cual deben apreciarse y juzgarse todos los dogmas y todos los doctores […] [son] los escritos proféticos y apostólicos»; ¶2 subjects patristic and modern writings to Scripture as witnesses; ¶7 calls Scripture alone judge, norm and rule. Translation from the Latin in the dossier; Triglot 1921, pp. 776–779; [Book of Concord text](https://bookofconcord.org/epitome/rule-and-norm/). The same debate-layer position is stated by the Baptist Faith and Message preamble—“the sole authority for faith and practice […] Confessions are only guides”—and the Assemblies of God paper—“the final authority”; those official excerpts and editions are recorded in the dossier §4.

**Overlap.** Exact overlap with OB-1 and part of OB-5; Baptist and Assemblies of God exclusive definitions belong to the dossier's debate layer.

**Verdict.** PASS: three independent speakers; direct Catholic replies; Lutheran, Baptist and Pentecostal official anchors; target is the present Catholic relation among Scripture, Tradition and Magisterium.

### PASS 2 · “Demostrá que esa tradición actual conserva la enseñanza oral de los apóstoles”

**Popular wording heard.** José asks: «¿cómo me demostrás vos […] que la religión papista tiene la doctrina que los apóstoles enseñaron oralmente?» ([24:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=1456s)). Edgar asks how the alleged oral tradition that leads to papal infallibility can be traced (38:40–43:39). Carlos accepts historical tradition but says «pasamos todo por el filtro de la Escritura» (20:38–21:12). This is not a denial that the apostles preached orally; it disputes the infallibility or identifiable continuity of later claims.

**Independent people.** Three: José; Edgar Pacheco; Carlos Veloz. Affiliations and the caveat on the reaction format are in the table above.

**Catholic answer heard.** Santiago answers José from 2 Thessalonians 2:15 and appeals to early ecclesial transmission and succession; he answers Edgar that the Catholic claim is a public, continuing apostolic tradition rather than a privately recovered oral sentence. Carlos's Catholic commentator argues that an acknowledged but always fallible tradition cannot finally settle contested readings.

**Catholic primary-source answer.** DV 7 says the apostles transmitted what they had received «tanto […] en la predicación oral […] como» in inspired writing, and left bishops as successors so the Gospel would remain whole and living. DV 8 says apostolic Tradition progresses in the Church and gives knowledge of the complete canon; DV 9–10 joins it to Scripture in one deposit. The Catechism 76 distinguishes oral and written transmission, and 83 limits the relevant Tradition to what comes from the apostles. [Dei Verbum 7–10](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_sp.html); [Catechism 74–100](https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p1s1c2a2_sp.html).

**Objecting-tradition official anchor.** Westminster Confession 1.6 says the whole counsel necessary for God's glory, salvation, faith and life is in Scripture expressly or by good and necessary consequence, «y á esta revelacion de su voluntad, nada se puede, ni se debe, añadir nunca, ni por nuevas revelaciones del Espíritu, ni por las tradiciones de los hombres»; it also affirms the Spirit's inward illumination and Christian prudence. 1647 text in the 1894 edition, p. 19; Spanish Monterrey 1880, p. 9; [1894 edition record](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffaith00chur). Formula of Concord, Epitome, “Rule and Norm” ¶2 receives patristic and modern writings as witnesses subordinate to Scripture, not judges.

**Overlap.** OB-1 and OB-2. The debate adds the epistemic challenge—how a present claimant identifies apostolic oral transmission—without changing the confessional rule.

**Verdict.** PASS under the grouped formulation above: three independent speakers; Catholic answers heard; Reformed and Lutheran anchors; target is the present Catholic claim that apostolic Tradition is preserved in the Church. Agent grouping requires Pedro's confirmation.

### PASS 3 · “La Iglesia reconoce el canon; no le da autoridad a la Escritura”

**Popular wording heard.** Edgar distinguishes recognizing the canon from conferring authority (15:04–18:59). José says a fallible case can be made from historical testimony, textual criticism and internal evidence ([1:14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6T3r_UGW0rQ&t=4474s)). Eduardo appeals to harmony, prophecy and historical Church testimony while denying that the Church is its own canon ([29:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x27TxdlN10M&t=1775s)–44:56).

**Independent people.** Three: Edgar Pacheco; José; Eduardo Gutiérrez, Protestant/evangelical pastor.

**Catholic answer heard.** Santiago answers Edgar and Eduardo that appealing to the Church's historical discernment while denying its authoritative judgment leaves the canon dependent on fallible criteria. He asks José how a fallible list can bind as the sole infallible rule; José accepts that his historical reasoning is fallible.

**Catholic primary-source answer.** The Catholic answer partly agrees with the objection: Vatican I says the books are sacred and canonical not because the Church later approved merely human compositions, but because, inspired by the Holy Spirit, they have God as author and were handed to the Church (*Dei Filius*, ch. 2). The disputed second claim is that «por esta Tradición conoce la Iglesia el Canon íntegro» (DV 8) and that apostolic Tradition made the Church discern the list (Catechism 120). [Dei Filius, official Latin](https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-ix/la/documents/constitutio-dogmatica-dei-filius-24-aprilis-1870.html); [Dei Verbum 8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_sp.html); [Catechism 120](https://www.vatican.va/archive/catechism_sp/p1s1c2a3_sp.html).

**Objecting-tradition official anchor.** Westminster Confession 1.4: Scripture's authority «no depende del testimonio de ningun hombre ó iglesia […] sino enteramente del de Dios»; 1.5 says the Church's testimony may move high esteem, but full persuasion of Scripture's infallible truth and divine authority comes from the Spirit witnessing by and with the Word. 1647 text, 1894 edition p. 19; Spanish Monterrey 1880, pp. 8–9; [edition record](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffaith00chur). Westminster Larger Catechism 4 adds the internal marks and the Spirit's witness.

**Overlap.** Exact overlap with OB-4. It also intersects OB-6 only when the list of Old Testament books is disputed; that deuterocanonical form did not recur in this sample.

**Verdict.** PASS: three independent speakers, direct Catholic answers, an exact Reformed anchor and a present Catholic target. The page should preserve the common ground that God—not the Church—is the source of Scripture's authority.
