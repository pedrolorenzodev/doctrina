It continues with eternal righteousness, Spirit, eternal life. Direct Latin Spanish accurate; no synthetic German/Latin quote. All seven AC references in the Scripture table match printed Latin ¶¶6–17 (John 20:21 sqq.; Mark 16:15; Romans 1:16; John 18:36; Luke 12:14; Philippians 3:20; 2 Corinthians 10:4). Expanded John 20:21–23 reflects the quoted contents, not a false printed range.
