### 1 · OB-2 answer takes a side the Church left open: "sin concluir de ese texto que todo lo revelado esté escrito" — **must-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-2, Respuesta católica, third paragraph (D l. 338); echoed in OB-1's answer, second paragraph («Lo que la Iglesia no acepta es que la Escritura sola sea la fuente de toda certeza», l. 307).
**Problem:** The sentence implies the Church holds that some revealed truth is *not* written: the "material insufficiency" of Scripture. The Church's documents do not say that. DV 9 and CCC 82 speak of where the Church draws its **certainty** («certitudinem suam … non per solam Sacram Scripturam hauriat»), not of a content missing from Scripture; Trent IV says the truth and discipline are contained «in libris scriptis, et sine scripto traditionibus» without dividing the content between them. Whether every revealed truth is also contained in Scripture in some way is a question Catholic theologians still debate, and the documents read do not settle it `[memory: Trent's draft "partim … partim" was replaced by "et"; at Vatican II the "certainty" sentence of DV 9 was a late addition worded so as not to decide the question; neither checked here, and neither should go on the site]`. A Catholic theologian on either side of that debate would refuse to sign a line that settles it in the Church's name. The OB-1 phrase is close to DV 9 but turns "certainty" into "fuente", which reintroduces the two-sources picture DV 9 avoids (finding 3).
**Fix:**
- OB-2, replace the third paragraph with the Church's own words only: «La Iglesia lee 2 Timoteo 3:16-17 como testimonio de que "toda la Escritura es divinamente inspirada y útil para enseñar […] a fin de que el hombre de Dios sea perfecto" (Dei Verbum 11). Lo que no acepta es que la Iglesia saque de la Escritura sola "su certeza acerca de todas las verdades reveladas" (Dei Verbum 9).» DV 9 (es) `[verified in D]`; DV 11 Latin «ut perfectus sit homo Dei, ad omne opus bonum instructus» `[verified]`.
- OB-1, second paragraph: «Lo que la Iglesia no acepta es sacar solamente de la Escritura "su certeza acerca de todas las verdades reveladas" (Dei Verbum 9)», instead of «que la Escritura sola sea la fuente de toda certeza».
- Add a non-site note under QE-2 (for Pedro and the critic): «The documents speak of the Church's certainty, not of revealed content absent from Scripture; whether all revealed truth is also in Scripture is not decided by the texts read. The site states neither side.» This also answers in advance any objection that "Rome says the Bible is incomplete".
- Optional strengthening for OB-2 (the Church's counterpart to WCF 1.6's "all things necessary"), DV 8 (es) `[verified in D]`: «lo que enseñaron los Apóstoles encierra todo lo necesario para que el Pueblo de Dios viva santamente y aumente su fe». It shows where the Church places "todo lo necesario": in the apostolic preaching, written and oral.

### 2 · Por qué, introduction: «Cristo no mandó escribir, sino predicar» is not in any Catholic document — **must-fix**

**Where:** §2, Texto para el sitio, introducción (D l. 189).
**Problem:** The draft presents as "las razones" of the Church a sentence that is apologetic rhetoric, not the Church's text. IV); los apóstoles lo transmitieron de palabra y por escrito, y dejaron sucesores (Dei Verbum 7); …» Trent IV (Ayala pp. 40–41) and DV 7 `[verified in D]`. Same check for PQ-1's heading («Cristo mandó predicar»): keep it, it is the documents' claim.

### 3 · QE-2: the Dogma grade covers Vatican II's wording; the 1564 profession is placed where it blurs CCC 83 — **should-fix**

**Where:** §1 QE-2, Grado (D l. 95) and Sources, 1564 profession bullet (l. 102); §6 table, Dogma row («el valor de las tradiciones apostólicas»).
**Problem:**
- *Grade.* The site draft is built on DV 9–10 («brotan "de la misma divina fuente"», «"un solo depósito sagrado de la palabra de Dios"»), but the grade line labels the whole statement «Dogma — definido por Trento». What Trent IV defines (decree with anathema) and *Dei Filius* ch. 2 restates is narrower: revelation «continetur in libris scriptis et sine scripto traditionibus», both received «pari pietatis affectu ac reverentia», and contempt of those traditions is anathematized. The "one wellspring / one deposit" articulation is Vatican II's (DV 9–10), magisterio auténtico by D's own default rule. QE-3 already does this correctly in two layers; QE-2 should too.
- *"Two sources".* D never says "dos fuentes", which is right for today's teaching (DV 9: «ex eadem divina scaturigine»). The critic and the apply step should know that the phrase does exist in the Magisterium before the Council, so that it is neither introduced as current teaching nor denied as never used: Pius XII, *Humani generis* (12 Aug 1950), Latin «uterque doctrinae divinitus revelatae fons tot tantosque continet thesauros veritatis», Spanish (vatican.va, n. 15) «las dos fuentes de la doctrina revelada contienen tantos y tan sublimes tesoros de verdad, que nunca realmente se agotan» `[verified]`. DV 10's note cites *Humani generis* (Denzinger 2314 (3886)) `[verified in D]`.
- *1564 profession.* Clause II embraces «Apostolicas **et ecclesiasticas** traditiones, reliquasque ejusdem Ecclesiae observationes et constitutiones». Placed under a Dogma statement about apostolic Tradition, it suggests ecclesiastical observances share that grade, which is exactly what CCC 83 (quoted three bullets below) distinguishes, and what OB-3's objectors attack.
**Fix:**
- QE-2 Grado: «Dos capas. Que la revelación "se contiene en los libros escritos y en las tradiciones no escritas" y que ambas se reciben "con igual afecto de piedad y reverencia": Dogma (de fe divina y católica), definido por Trento (ses. IV, decreto con anatema) y declarado por el Vaticano I (*Dei Filius*, cap. 2); la Iglesia no lo ha clasificado por separado [inference]. Que Escritura y Tradición brotan "de la misma divina fuente" y forman "un solo depósito": magisterio auténtico (concilio ecuménico), Dei Verbum 9–10.» CIC 750 §1 can stay as the canonical definition of the object of faith, not as a grade of DV's wording.
- 1564 profession: keep it, with a note in Sources: «Historical profession; clause II also embraces ecclesiastical traditions and observances, which the Church distinguishes from apostolic Tradition (CEC 83).» Better still, move it next to clause III (finding 12), where it bears on interpretation.
- §6 Dogma example: «que la revelación se contiene en la Escritura y en las tradiciones apostólicas, recibidas con igual reverencia (Trento ses. IV)», not «el valor de las tradiciones apostólicas», which is vaguer than the decree.

### 8 · OB-5: "councils can err" is left to the Papacy page; LG 25 answers it here, and grants part of the Augsburg quote — **should-fix**

**Where:** §4 OB-5, Respuesta católica, third paragraph (D l. 418): «Cuándo enseñan infaliblemente el Papa y los concilios se trata en la página del papado».
**Problem:** WCF 31.4 ("All synods or councils … may err, and many have erred") and AC XXVIII's Augustine ("Neither must we submit to Catholic bishops if they chance to err") are the objection's two sharpest texts, and the answer refers both away. The Catholic answer is short and belongs on this page because the objection is about the rule of faith, not about the pope: (a) individual bishops are not infallible, which concedes part of the Augsburg quote in the Church's own words; (b) bishops in an ecumenical council, when they define, are «maestros y jueces de la fe»; (c) that infallibility reaches only as far as the deposit of revelation, which links back to DV 10 («no está sobre la palabra de Dios»). Without it the page shows the Church answering "the faithful cannot err" (LG 12) to a text about councils, which reads as evasion.
**Fix:** replace the last sentence of that paragraph with LG 25 (es, vatican.va) `[verified]`: «"Aunque cada uno de los Prelados no goce por si de la prerrogativa de la infalibilidad", cuando "reunidos en concilio ecuménico, son para la Iglesia universal los maestros y jueces de la fe y costumbres", a sus definiciones "hay que adherirse con la sumisión de la fe"; y esa infalibilidad "se extiende tanto cuanto abarca el depósito de la Revelación, que debe ser custodiado santamente y expresado con fidelidad" (Lumen gentium 25). Lo que corresponde al Papa se trata en la página del papado.» Optional, for the grade line or a Sources note: CIC 749 §3, «Ninguna doctrina se considera definida infaliblemente si no consta así de modo manifiesto» `[verified in D, Conventions]`, which shows that not every conciliar sentence is a definition (the Catholic counterpart of "many have erred"). Grade: LG 25 restates Vatican I on the pope; on councils, magisterio auténtico (concilio ecuménico) as worded; the papal definition is graded on the Papacy page.

### 17 · OB-7: *Divino afflante Spiritu* quoted without the two clauses that give the Church's reason — **optional**
