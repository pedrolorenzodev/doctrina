## Conventions and source boundary

- **Column:** the Book of Concord's own distinction between Scripture as rule and the confessions as witnesses, with the document-by-document boundary inherited from `../eucharist/lutheran.md`.
- **Map categories:** Tier 1 comprises the preface, ecumenical creeds, unaltered AC, Ap, SA with Tr, SC, LC, FC Ep/SD. The Catalog is an appendix. Luther's other works are explanations, not an independent norm; body-level statements and ecumenical agreements apply only to their named bodies.
- **Subscription strands:** LCMS's whole-collection subscription, ELCA's graded language, LWF's especially AC/SC basis, and CELC's “because” basis remain distinct. No subscription is silently imposed on all mapped churches. These mapped adoption descriptions are [unverified] in this drafting pass.
- **Original witness:** *Concordia Triglotta*, St. Louis: Concordia Publishing House, 1921, ed. F. Bente, English F. Bente/W. H. T. Dau; archive `ConcordiaTriglotta`.
- **Original languages:** German for FC, SA and catechisms; Latin for Ap and Tr; both German and Latin official for AC. An English translation is never the intermediary for Spanish.
- **Spanish:** every new quotation is [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from the named original language; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The map identifies no usable public-domain Spanish witness; modern copyrighted/unclear-licence versions are not copied.
- **Locators:** FC Ep RN = Epitome, Rule and Norm; FC SD RN = Solid Declaration, Rule and Norm. The German original's section order is not silently replaced by a Latin translator's paragraph segmentation.
- **Tags:** [verified: scan] records a page-image reading during drafting; it does not constitute the prohibited independent verification pass. Other claims and all Spanish translations remain [unverified]. Long s is transcribed as s; divided words joined.
- **Scratch:** `~/doctrina-scratch/third-order/triglot.txt`, metadata, page XML and `triglot-original-<printed-page>.jpg`. Earlier offset locator image names are not reliable printed-page names.
- **Scope:** Scripture's doctrinal role as stated by these documents. No modern inerrancy controversy, canon reconstruction, debate layer, critic or apply pass.

## Qué enseña

### Definition: the prophetic and apostolic Scriptures as rule

> “Wir glauben, lehren und bekennen, daß die einige Regel und Richtschnur, nach welcher zugleich alle Lehren und Lehrer gerichtet und geurteilt werden sollen, sind allein die prophetischen und apostolischen Schriften Alten und Neuen Testaments”.

— FC Ep RN 1, German original, Triglot p. 776 [verified: scan]; opening statement before its proof citations.

> «Creemos, enseñamos y confesamos que la única regla y norma según la cual deben juzgarse y evaluarse todas las doctrinas y todos los maestros son solamente los escritos proféticos y apostólicos del Antiguo y del Nuevo Testamento».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The first person belongs solely to the quoted Formula, never to the site.

### QE-1 · Every doctrine and teacher is judged

The opening quotation includes “alle Lehren und Lehrer”, not merely selected controversies. Its proof citations are Psalm 119 and Galatians 1; the adjacent Latin column identifies Psalm 119:105 and Galatians 1:8 (p. 776) [verified: scan].

The later summary calls Scripture:

> “der einige Richter, Regel und Richtschnur”.

— FC Ep RN 7, German, Triglot p. 778 [verified: scan].

> «el único juez, regla y norma».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### QE-2 · Other writings remain subject to Scripture

> “Andere Schriften aber der alten oder neuen Lehrer, wie sie Namen haben, sollen der Heiligen Schrift nicht gleichgehalten, sondern alle zumal miteinander derselben unterworfen”.

— FC Ep RN 2, German original, p. 776 [verified: scan]; excerpt ending before the paragraph's continuation.

> «Los demás escritos de maestros antiguos o nuevos, cualquiera sea su nombre, no deben equipararse con la Sagrada Escritura, sino quedar todos juntos sujetos a ella».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

The continuation receives them as witnesses to where prophetic and apostolic teaching has been preserved after the apostles' time. It does not discard them as useless (same page) [verified: scan].

### QE-3 · Confessions testify and explain

> “Die andern Symbola aber und angezogenen Schriften sind nicht Richter wie die Heilige Schrift, sondern allein Zeugnis und Erklärung des Glaubens”.

— FC Ep RN 8, German original, p. 778 [verified: scan]; opening excerpt.

> «Los demás símbolos y escritos citados no son jueces como la Sagrada Escritura, sino solamente testimonio y explicación de la fe».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

The same paragraph identifies what their testimony concerns: how Scripture was understood and explained in disputed articles within the church. It supplies its own distinction between source and witness.

### QE-4 · Creeds and catechisms are retained

FC Ep RN 3 receives the Apostles', Nicene and Athanasian creeds. RN 4 receives the unaltered AC, its Apology and the Smalcald Articles; RN 5 receives the Small and Large Catechisms as the laity's Bible (p. 776) [verified: scan]. Their presence beside the rule statement is part of the document's own order, not a later qualification added by the dossier.

### QE-5 · God's Word establishes articles of faith

> “Gottes Wort soll Artikel des Glaubens stellen und sonst niemand, auch kein Engel.”

— SA II.2.15, German original, Triglot p. 466 [verified: scan].

> «La palabra de Dios debe establecer los artículos de fe, y nadie más, ni siquiera un ángel».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The sentence is excerpted from SA's treatment of the appeal to Fathers; it is not made into a new reply to an objection here.

### QE-6 · Useful explanations remain useful

> “andere gute, nützliche, reine Bücher, Auslegungen der Heiligen Schrift, Widerlegungen der Irrtümer, Erklärungen der Lehrartikel”.

— FC SD RN 10, German original, Triglot p. 854 [verified: scan]; list excerpt from the paragraph which says these are not rejected.

> «otros libros buenos, útiles y puros, exposiciones de la Sagrada Escritura, refutaciones de errores y explicaciones de los artículos doctrinales».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The same paragraph qualifies their use by agreement with the stated doctrinal form.

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · The rule supplied by the text

FC Ep RN 1 itself joins its rule statement to “Dein Wort ist meines Fußes Leuchte und ein Licht auf meinem Wege” and the warning concerning an angel preaching differently (German, Triglot p. 776) [verified: scan]. Its adjacent Latin prints Psalm 119:105 and Galatians 1:8. No further independent deduction is supplied here.

Spanish [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from that German psalm wording, unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]:

> «Tu palabra es lámpara de mi pie y luz en mi camino».

This is the Formula's embedded biblical wording; it is not a newly imported Spanish Bible edition.

### PQ-2 · Scripture as the test stone

FC Ep RN 7 calls its standard:

> “dem einigen Probierstein”.

— German original, Triglot p. 778 [verified: scan]; excerpt from the clause by which all teaching is tested and judged.

> «la única piedra de toque».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### PQ-3 · Explanation measured by its source

FC SD RN 10 explains the role of the common doctrinal form with the clause:

> “weil sie aus Gottes Wort genommen”.

— German original, Triglot p. 854 [verified: scan]; excerpt within its reason for judging other writings by the stated doctrinal form.

> «porque ha sido tomada de la palabra de Dios».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. It is the document's own rationale for that form's authority; it is not a claim that every contemporary body subscribes identically.

### PQ-4 · Witnesses rather than another judge

FC Ep RN 8 explains the symbols as testimony to how Scripture was understood in the church's disputed articles (QE-3). SA II.2.15 says articles cannot be made from the Fathers' words or works and concludes with the God's-Word sentence in QE-5 (Triglot p. 466) [verified: scan]. The Fathers appear as the document's quoted witnesses, never as members of a modern denomination.

## Cómo se vive

### Testing teaching

FC Ep RN 1 directs its rule to doctrines and teachers; RN 7 calls it the test stone. FC SD RN 10 explicitly retains useful explanations and interpretation under the stated qualification (printed pp. 776,778,854) [verified: scan]. These are the documents' prescriptions for teaching, not a survey of current Bible-reading habits.

### Word and sacramental ministry

AC XXVIII.8 describes ecclesial power:

> “Haec potestas tantum exercetur docendo seu praedicando Verbum et porrigendo sacramenta vel multis vel singulis iuxta vocationem”.

— Latin original, Triglot p. 84 [verified: scan]; excerpt before the explanation of the eternal things conferred.

> «Esta potestad se ejerce solamente enseñando o predicando la Palabra y administrando los sacramentos, a muchos o a cada uno, según el llamamiento».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from Latin; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. AC's German original on that page likewise speaks of teaching/preaching God's Word and administering sacraments, rather than a civil coercive power [verified: scan].

### Churches, schools and homes

FC SD RN 8, in its Latin column, reports the catechisms proposed in churches, schools and private houses; the German original on p. 852 states:

> “öffentlich in Kirchen, Schulen und Häusern gebraucht worden”.

— German original, Triglot p. 852 [verified: scan]; excerpt from the Formula's report about the catechisms' reception and use.

> «usados públicamente en iglesias, escuelas y casas».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from German; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The historical report is attributed to the Formula; it is not rewritten as a claim about every current Lutheran parish.

The SC's own household-teaching heading and baptism's Word-and-water explanation are located in p. 550 (read during this tradition's baptism draft). Their actual text is retained in `../baptism/lutheran.md`; no extra contemporary custom is inferred from them.

### Limits of this account

No statistics, prescribed modern reading schedule or approval of a particular modern Bible translation is derived from the rule-and-norm clauses. Present-body statements in map Tier 4 would have to retain that body label if later used.

## Grado de autoridad

### The Formula's own categories

| Category | What the source actually says | Locus |
|---|---|---|
| Scripture | Judge, rule and norm; only test stone | FC Ep RN 1,7 [verified: scan] |
| Other ancient and modern teachers | Subordinate witnesses | FC Ep RN 2 [verified: scan] |
| Creeds and confessions | Testimony and explanation of faith | FC Ep RN 3–5,8 [verified: scan] |
| Useful books and interpretations | Retained as useful explanations under the document's stated conformity condition | FC SD RN 10 [verified: scan] |
| Luther's additional writings | Explanation under the Word's sole norm, not an equal norm | FC SD RN 9, pp. 852–854 [verified: scan] |

“Norma normans / norma normata” is not inserted into a quotation as though these Latin expressions appeared here. The map and applied verification distinguish later explanatory vocabulary from the Book of Concord's own wording.

### Reception across bodies

The map's constitutions distinguish LCMS, ELCA, LWF and CELC wording. Those documents explain who subscribes and how; they are not additional universal confessions. No fresh constitution comparison was undertaken here [unverified]. This is why every Formula claim remains labelled Formula rather than generalized to every Lutheran body.
