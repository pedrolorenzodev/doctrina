## Qué enseña

### Definition: God's written Word as the rule

WCF 1.2, after listing the books of both Testaments, says:

> «Todos estos fueron dados por la inspiracion de Dios para ser la regla de la fé y de la práctica.»

— Monterrey 1880, p. 7 [verified: scan].

### QE-1 · Divine authorship and authority

> «La autoridad de las Sagradas Escrituras, la que hace que deben creerse y obedecerse, no depende del testimonio de ningun hombre ó iglesia cualquiera que fuera, sino enteramente del de Dios, quien es su autor; porque El es la misma verdad; por esto deben ser recibidas porque son la palabra de Dios.»

— WCF 1.4, Monterrey 1880, p. 8 [verified: scan].

### QE-2 · Sufficiency, including necessary consequence

> «Todo el consejo de Dios, tocante á todas las cosas necesarias para su propia gloria y para la salvacion, fé y vida del hombre; es ó expresamente expuesto en las Escrituras ó se puede deducir de ellas por buena y necesaria consecuencia; y á esta revelacion de su voluntad, nada se puede, ni se debe, añadir nunca, ni por nuevas revelaciones del Espíritu, ni por las tradiciones de los hombres.»

— WCF 1.6, Monterrey 1880, p. 9 [verified: scan]; terminal proof marker omitted.

The same paragraph retains internal illumination and practical judgment:

> «la iluminacion interna del Espíritu de Dios, es necesaria á fin de que se entiendan de una manera salvadora, las cosas reveladas en la palabra».

> «hay algunas circunstancias tocante al culto de Dios y al gobierno de la iglesia, comunes á las acciones y sociedades humanas, que deben arreglarse conforme á la luz de la naturaleza y la prudencia cristiana, guardándose siempre las reglas generales de la palabra».

— WCF 1.6, Monterrey 1880, p. 9 [verified: scan]; excerpts, not the complete paragraph.

### QE-3 · Clarity qualified by the confession itself

> «Las cosas reveladas en las Escrituras, considerándolas en su naturaleza, no son todas igualmente claras, ni se entienden igualmente por todos».

— WCF 1.7, Monterrey 1880, p. 10 [verified: scan].

Its affirmative qualification follows:

> «las cosas que necesariamente tienen que saberse, creerse y guardarse para conseguir la salvacion, son propuestas y declaradas en uno ú otro lugar de las Escrituras, tan claramente, que no solo los eruditos, sino aun los que no lo son, pueden alcanzar un conocimiento suficiente de ellas, por el debido uso de los medios ordinarios».

— WCF 1.7, same page [verified: scan].

### QE-4 · Scripture interprets Scripture

> «La misma Escritura es la regla infalible para interpretarse á sí misma; y por tanto, cuando hay una cuestion respecto del sentido verdadero y pleno de cualquier pasaje; cuyo significado no es multíplice, sino uno solo, se puede buscar y establecer segun otros textos que hablan con mas claridad.»

— WCF 1.9, Monterrey 1880, p. 12 [verified: scan].

### QE-5 · The supreme judge

> «El Espíritu Santo, que habla en las Escrituras, el Juez Supremo, por quien toda controversia religiosa tiene que decidirse y por quien tambien todos los decretos de los concilios, las opiniones de los escritores antiguos, y las doctrinas de los hombres y de los espíritus particulares tienen que determinarse, y en su fallo ó sentencia, podemos descansar confiadamente.»

— WCF 1.10, Monterrey 1880, p. 12 [verified: scan]. The printed Spanish sentence is retained, including its syntax; it is not silently repaired.

### QE-6 · Canon and original languages

WCF 1.2 lists the Old and New Testament books (Monterrey pp. 6–7) [verified: scan]. WCF 1.3 calls the books commonly titled Apocrypha human writings rather than part of the canon (p. 7) [verified: scan]. This is its canon boundary, not an assignment of these books' status to another tradition.

> «la iglesia tiene que apelar finalmente á los originales en esos idiomas en toda controversia».

— WCF 1.8, Monterrey 1880, p. 11 [verified: scan], referring in the same paragraph to Hebrew for the Old Testament and Greek for the New.

### QE-7 · Translation and access

> «la Biblia debe traducirse en la lengua vulgar de todas las naciones á que pueda venir».

— WCF 1.8, Monterrey 1880, p. 11 [verified: scan]. Its own stated ends are acceptable worship and hope through Scripture's patience and comfort.

## Por qué

### PQ-1 · Preservation, propagation and comfort

WCF 1.1 gives its own reason for committing revelation to writing:

> «para mejor conservar y propagar la verdad, y para el mejor consuelo y establecimiento de la iglesia contra la corrupcion de la carne, la malicia de Satanás y del mundo, le plugo ponerlo todo en escrito».

— Monterrey 1880, p. 5 [verified: scan]; excerpt before the proof marker.

### PQ-2 · God's authorship

The ground supplied in WCF 1.4 is “Dios, quien es su autor” (QE-1). The paragraph does not ground Scripture's authority in the confession itself. Its proof apparatus cites 2 Timothy 3:16; 1 John 5:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:13 (Monterrey p. 8) [verified: scan].

### PQ-3 · The Spirit's inward witness

WCF 1.5 recognizes the church's testimony:

> «El testimonio de la iglesia puede mover é inducirnos á guardar para las Sagradas Escrituras una estimacion alta y reverencial».

— Monterrey 1880, p. 8 [verified: scan].

The paragraph's final clause locates persuasion and conviction in:

> «la obra interior del Espíritu Santo, quien dá testimonio juntamente con la palabra divina, y por ella á nuestros corazones».

— WCF 1.5, Monterrey 1880, p. 9 [verified: scan]. The quotation begins after the printed “provienen de”.

Between these excerpts WCF itself lists the heavenly character of Scripture's matter, efficacy of doctrine, majesty of style, agreement of its parts, scope of God's glory, and the way of salvation. These are the confession's own listed considerations (p. 8), not independent arguments added by the dossier [verified: scan].

### PQ-4 · The end served by the rule

> «La palabra de Dios que se contiene en las Escrituras del Antiguo y del Nuevo Testamento, es la única regla que ha dado Dios para enseñarnos como hemos de glorificarle y gozar de El.»

— WSC 2, Monterrey 1880, opening catechism page immediately before printed p. 164 (archive image n164) [verified: scan].

### PQ-5 · Councils as help

WCF 31.4, historical numbering, says:

> “All synods or councils since the apostles' times, whether general or particular, may err, and many have erred; therefore they are not to be made the rule of faith or practice, but to be used as an help in both.”

— Edinburgh 1894, p. 97 [verified: scan]. A Spanish rendering is not generated here because a public-domain Spanish WCF witness is available; the original English is retained to keep the historical paragraph numbering explicit.

The immediately preceding WCF 31.3 says determinations consonant with God's Word are to be received “with reverence and submission” (same page) [verified: scan]. The document's qualification accompanies its criticism of conciliar fallibility.

## Cómo se vive

### Reading, worship and translation

WCF 1.8 specifies the people's right and interest in Scripture and says they ought to read and search it in the fear of God (Monterrey p. 11) [verified: scan]. Its translation requirement appears in QE-7.

WLC 156 distinguishes public congregational reading from everyone's reading privately and with their families; the continuation on Edinburgh p. 196 says:

> “by themselves, and with their families: to which end, the holy scriptures are to be translated out of the original into vulgar languages.”

— WLC 156, Edinburgh 1894, p. 196 [verified: scan]; printed proof markers omitted.

> «por sí mismos y con sus familias; para este fin, las Sagradas Escrituras han de traducirse del original a las lenguas comunes».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], directly from the preceding original; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]. The subject of this continuation is the people named on the preceding page.

### How WLC says to read

> “The holy scriptures are to be read with an high and reverent esteem of them”.

— WLC 157, Edinburgh 1894, p. 196 [verified: scan].

> “with desire to know, believe, and obey the will of God revealed in them; with diligence, and attention to the matter and scope of them; with meditation, application, self-denial, and prayer.”

— WLC 157, Edinburgh 1894, p. 197 [verified: scan]; proof markers omitted.

Spanish [traducción propia asistida por IA], from those English excerpts; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified]:

> «Las Sagradas Escrituras deben leerse con alta y reverente estima de ellas».

> «con deseo de conocer, creer y obedecer la voluntad de Dios revelada en ellas; con diligencia y atención a su contenido y propósito; con meditación, aplicación, negación de uno mismo y oración».

### Preaching remains a called office

> “The word of God is to be preached only by such as are sufficiently gifted, and also duly approved and called to that office.”

— WLC 158, Edinburgh 1894, p. 197 [verified: scan]; proof markers omitted.

> «La palabra de Dios debe ser predicada solamente por quienes están suficientemente dotados y también debidamente aprobados y llamados a ese oficio».

— [traducción propia asistida por IA], from English; unreviewed by Pedro [unverified].

### Reception and application

WLC 160 prescribes diligent, prepared, prayerful hearing, examination of what is heard by Scripture, reception with faith and love, meditation and fruit in life (Edinburgh 1894, p. 199; OCR located, not quoted as a checked print passage here) [unverified]. WSC 89–90 treats the efficacy of the Word and preparation, attention, meditation and practice; those answers were located in the same volume's catechism, not used to invent additional customs [unverified].

These are the standards' prescriptions. No claim is made about how often a present congregation follows them.

## Grado de autoridad

### Scripture and confessions

WCF 1.10 states its own supreme judge in QE-5; WSC 2 calls Scripture the “única regla” in PQ-4. The dossier's grading follows those statements rather than presenting Westminster as another infallible source [verified: scan].

### Councils

Historical WCF 31.3 makes their function ministerial and requires reception of determinations consonant with the Word; 31.4 makes them fallible helps. Both paragraphs were read in Edinburgh 1894, p. 97 [verified: scan].

This does not erase church authority: the same 31.3 requires reverence and submission. It does not give every private reader an authority the confession never names.
