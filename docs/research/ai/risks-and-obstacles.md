## Objections to the plan itself (for the record)
- **The answer step on Haiku may be too weak for the neutrality rules**; the plan bets on code checks plus the evaluation to show it, with Sonnet one env change away (15× the cost: ~100 answers per 5 USD).
- **The typed-question routing keeps scripted answers when the regex matches**: cheap and reviewed, but a nuanced question that happens to match «Padres» gets the canned John 6 answer. Accepted for v1 because the scripted answers are the reviewed ones and the existing specs depend on them; revisit when real answers prove reliable.
- **No proposal is planned for the live states** (pending, errors): the plan treats them as states of approved components (kind b). If Pedro sees them as new UI, they move into the S-ENTRY proposal.
