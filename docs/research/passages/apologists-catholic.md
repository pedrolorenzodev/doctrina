## 1. Eucharist · John 6:51–58, 6:60–69 (page entry 3 of `eucharist-catholic.md`)

**Argument A · The flesh «of no avail» (John 6:63) cannot be Jesus's own flesh.**
- **Joe Heschmeyer**, «Does This Verse Disprove the Eucharist?», *Shameless Popery* (Catholic Answers), YouTube, 20 Aug 2024, 14:42, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7yY0zSUm20&t=273s, minutes 4:33 to 4:44 `[verified: captions]`: «if you take this to mean Jesus can't be talking about his own flesh because he says the flesh is of no avail you have to ignore the entire preceding teaching in the bread of life discourse where he speaks of his flesh explicitly as being of great avail».
- **Joe Heschmeyer**, same video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7yY0zSUm20&t=352s, minutes 5:52 to 6:07 `[verified: captions]`: «you cannot say Jesus's flesh profits nothing when his flesh saves us that strips the cross of its meaning John 6:63 cannot be denying the efficacy or importance of Jesus's flesh» (the caption prints «663»; written «6:63» here).
- *Not counted:* Dios Te Salve, `epr44J8X8ys` (Spanish, 4:05–4:34), reads «el espíritu» as the Holy Spirit giving life; the creator is unnamed and not in the criterion (see `videos/eucharist-catholic.md`).
- *Not checkable:* **Jimmy Akin**, «Answering a Protestant Objection to the Eucharist (John 6:63)», Catholic Answers Live, 27 Sep 2024, `PucLuJsfekA`: no captions could be read, so no quote is given.

**Argument B · The disciples took Jesus literally, and he did not call them back.**
- **Karlo Broussard**, «Is Receiving the Eucharist Cannibalism?», Catholic Answers Live, YouTube, 27 Mar 2021, 6:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xS29MKkkxpU&t=185s, minutes 3:05 to 3:22 `[verified: captions]`: «If Jesus were only speaking figuratively—that is to say, eating his flesh and drinking his blood only means that we must believe in him—if that's all Jesus meant, well then his disciples […] would not have had a hard time embracing that teaching».
- **Karlo Broussard**, same video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xS29MKkkxpU&t=220s, minutes 3:40 to 3:51 `[verified: captions]`: «it is precisely because of this teaching to eat his flesh and drink his blood that these disciples get up and walk away, and Jesus does not call them back».

**Argument C · It is not cannibalism: Christ gives his substance under the appearance of bread and wine.**
- **Karlo Broussard**, same video, minutes 5:08 to 5:19 `[verified: captions]` (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xS29MKkkxpU&t=308s): «Jesus reveals at the Last Supper that he's going to give us the substance or the reality of his flesh and blood, but under the appearance of bread and wine».

**Count and independence.** Two persons, both Catholic Answers staff and both in the Catholic Answers Magazine: **not independent as an organisation**. ## Method (repeatable)
1. English captions: `yt-dlp --skip-download --write-auto-subs --sub-langs en --sub-format vtt --sleep-subtitles 5 -o "%(id)s" <url>`; Spanish ones and any that yt-dlp refuses: `node ~/.doctrina-youtube/transcript.js <outdir> <id>…` (about 45 s per video). Rate limit: about 20 yt-dlp subtitle requests in 5 minutes.
2. Convert to text with cue times, grep the passage's words, copy the sentence and its first and last cue.
3. For articles: `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0" <url>`, strip tags, match the sentence in the paragraph.
4. Candidates for the next pass: search `<creator> <passage>` with `yt-dlp "ytsearch8:…" --print "%(id)s | %(view_count)s | %(duration_string)s | %(channel)s | %(title)s"`, keep the criterion creators, read the captions before quoting.
