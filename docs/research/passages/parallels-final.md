## Decision (agent's call, for Pedro to confirm)
- **Source: A. T. Robertson's harmony, section level**, cross-checked against **Stevens and Burton**. A parallel is the same event or saying in another book as the harmony groups it in one section; thematic cross-references (OpenBible, TSK, STEPBible) are not used for «Pasajes paralelos».
- **Why not OpenBible/TSK:** the predecessor measured that its high-vote pairs are often thematic (`parallels.md`: for Baptism 3, Acts 2:38, the top pairs are Mark 16:16, Luke 24:47, 1 Pet 3:21, Rom 6:3, Matt 3:2; none is the same saying). It stays valid only for a future «ver también», where it is CC BY 4.0 (`parallels.md`, «Source and licence», verified there).
- **STEPBible:** its cross-reference data comes from TSKe (a TSK enhancement), the same thematic kind. Licences found are mixed (the repository says CC BY 4.0; STEPBible's wiki pages say CC BY-NC 3.0 or CC BY-NC-ND 3.0; a fork says CC BY-NC 4.0) `[snippet: WebSearch 2026-11-01; github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data, stepweb.atlassian.net «Copyrights & Licences»]`; TSKe's own licence not found. Not used.

## The source, edition and licence
- **Robertson:** *A Harmony of the Gospels for Students of the Life of Christ, Based on the Broadus Harmony in the Revised Version* (New York and London: Harper & Brothers, 1922; «COPYRIGHT, 1922, BY HARPER & BROTHERS», read in the e-text). E-text: Project Gutenberg eBook #36264, https://gutenberg.org/ebooks/36264 (release 2011-05-29) `[verified: e-text downloaded and read 2026-11-01]`. Princeton Theological Seminary's scan is catalogued «No Copyright – United States» (https://commons.ptsem.edu/id/harmonyofgospels00robe) `[snippet]`. The data used is the book's «Analytical Outline of the Harmony»: 185 section entries (1–184, with 128a and 128b) giving the references in each Gospel (plus Acts and 1 Cor where Robertson places them).
- **Author's dates:** Robertson 1863–1934 (died 24 September 1934) `[snippet: Wikipedia and SBTS archive, WebSearch 2026-11-01]`.
- **Licence by territory (the repo's rule is «free in the US, Spain and all of Latin America»):**
  - US: published 1922, public domain `[snippet: Princeton Commons and Gutenberg]`.
  - Mexico: the term is life + 100 years (Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor, art. 29) `[snippet: Wikimedia Commons «Copyright rules by territory/Mexico»]`, so Robertson's work (death 1934) is protected there until the end of 2034. **Robertson's book is therefore not free everywhere under the repo's rule.**
  - Spain and the rest of Latin America: not established here (the search found conflicting statements about Spain's term for authors who died before 1987) `[unverified]`.
- **What the site would ship:** only reference numbers (verse ranges), no text of the book. The correspondences «Mark 1:9–11 = Matt 3:13–17 = Luke 3:21–22» are facts about the Gospels that every harmony states; but the choice of sections is Robertson's arrangement. To keep a source that is free everywhere underneath every pair, the data is cross-checked against **Stevens and Burton**, *A Harmony of the Gospels for Historical Study* (1893; 1902 printing: https://archive.org/details/cu31924029339474; Princeton copy https://archive.org/details/harmonyofgosp00stev): the Internet Archive record gives the authors as William Arnold Stevens 1839–1910 and Ernest De Witt Burton 1856–1925 `[verified: archive.org metadata API 2026-11-01]`, so the work is public domain in Mexico too (life + 100: 1925 + 100 → free from 1 January 2026) `[inference from the Mexico term above]`. Each pair in the data carries `confirmedBy` after the verifier's cross-check (below).
- **Attribution line (proposal; where it goes is open item K3):** «Paralelos según las armonías de los Evangelios de A. T. Robertson (1922) y de W. A. Stevens y E. D. Burton (1893).»

## What the data says (`parallels-data.json`)
- 231 passages in 20 pages; **65** have at least one parallel; **164** pairs (179 computed, 15 removed after verification), of which **82** are marked `coarse` (same event in the harmony, but the section ranges in the two books differ in size by more than a factor of two, or a section exceeds 40 verses, or the verifier listed the pair as «same event, saying not established»: the alignment verse by verse is not established) and **30** are cross-book. **166** passages have none (see below).
- Per passage: `refs` (the page's own), `parallels[]` with `ref` (OSIS range, the section's whole range in that book), `kind` (`gospel`, or `cross-book` for Acts and 1 Corinthians placed in the section by Robertson), `coarse`, `source`, `confirmedBy`, `sections` (Robertson's section numbers) and `sectionTitles`.
- **Rules applied (by script):** (1) only refs in the Gospels, Acts or 1 Cor can match; (2) a section matches a ref only if the section's range in the ref's book covers at least half of the ref's verses (so a one-verse sliver of a long ref, e.g. Mark 14:26 for the Last Supper, does not pull in Gethsemane); (3) the parallels are the section's ranges in **other** books; (4) a parallel that overlaps one of the passage's own refs is dropped (the Last Supper passage that already cites Matt, Mark, Luke and 1 Cor has none to add); (5) a chapter-only ref (`John.6`) yields none, because it cannot say which verses.
- **Passages with none (166):** the Epistle, Old Testament, Psalm and John-only passages, which no harmony of the Gospels covers; John-only sayings that Robertson sets alone in a section (John 3:5, the Bread of Life discourse 6:51–69, 17:21 and so on).

## Known limits (read before using)
- **Section granularity.** Robertson sets whole sections side by side. Where one Gospel's range is much longer than the other's (Matthew 23 against Mark 12:38–40; Matt 5–7 against Luke 6:17–49; the Pilate section against John 18:36), the sections are parallel as events but not verse for verse. The `coarse` flag marks them; the page should show the non-coarse ones by default or label the rest «mismo episodio».
- **Cross-book entries are Robertson's identifications**, not statements of the text (1 Cor 15:6 set with the Galilee commission in Matt 28:16–20; 1 Cor 15:5 with Luke 24:33–35; Acts 1:3–8 with Luke 24:44–49). They are all `coarse` or `kind: cross-book`; the site never concludes, so they should be hidden by default or shown with Robertson named. **Agent's call:** kept in the data, flagged.
- **Known doublets are not parallels.** The harmony's appendix «Similar Incidents and Chief Repeated Sayings» lists, for example, «Baptism of Death: §§ 108 and 125» (Luke 12:50 and Mark 10:38); the data does not include them.
- **The first pass of the data is Robertson alone;** pairs the Stevens–Burton cross-check cannot read (OCR) are marked `[unconfirmed by S–B]` in the verification.

## Removed after verification (15 pairs; the verifier's W1–W6 and one more)

- Matt 25 as parallel of the Luke 21:33 passages (sola scriptura·baptist 1, eucharist·lutheran 18): the parables are not a counterpart; Matt 24 stays. **Agent's call**, from the verifier's suggestion.
