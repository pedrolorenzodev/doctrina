## The test used (agent's call, written down so it can be rechecked)
There is no neutral list of «most faithful to the documents». Three **observable proxies** are used, each one a fact that can be checked, and none of them is the agent's opinion of orthodoxy:
1. **Standing in the tradition's own institutions:** ordained or employed by a body of that tradition (a priest, a pastor, a staff apologist of its main lay apostolate, a seminary post).
2. **Depth:** published books or academic degrees on doctrine, not only video commentary.
3. **Audience:** subscribers of the main channel at the date of reading.

A person who passes 1 and 2 and has an audience is listed as «candidate». The site would still quote only what he says against a document of his tradition, so fidelity is checked per quote, not once per person (that check belongs to C1, not to this file).
