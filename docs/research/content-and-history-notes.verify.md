### Ignatius, *Smyrnaeans* 6.2–7.2 and Greek

Status: **CONFIRMED WITH CONTEXT LIMIT**.

- The v2 witness is `ew-flesh`, `data/raw/eucharist.content.json` 5426–5450: it quotes 7.1 and says the opponents are docetists.

- [verified] Lake 1912, *Smyrnaeans* 6.2 describes the same opponents before the Eucharistic sentence: they have no concern for love, widow, orphan, oppressed, prisoner or freed person, hungry or thirsty. - [verified] The Greek behind the central line is `Εὐχαριστίας καὶ προσευχῆς ἀπέχονται, διὰ τὸ μὴ ὁμολογεῖν τὴν εὐχαριστίαν σάρκα εἶναι τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ` (“They abstain from Eucharist and prayer because they do not confess the Eucharist to be the flesh of our Saviour Jesus Christ”). - [inference] The note “Los adversarios son docetas” is a defensible modern classification because chapters 1–5 repeatedly oppose the claim that Christ only seemed to suffer and insist on real flesh; the letter itself does not use the noun “docetas.” Keep the note as editorial context, not as a quotation from Ignatius.
