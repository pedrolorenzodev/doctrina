## 2. What users say

- **Auto-scroll is the most repeated UI complaint.** OpenAI community, many threads: «ChatGPT won't let me scroll down while it's writing» ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-wont-let-me-scroll-down-while-its-writing/1130778)), «Auto scrolling error consistently impedes straightforward usage» ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/auto-scrolling-error-consistently-impedes-straightforward-usage/1229852)), a Safari user whose page jumps up several messages while it generates, and a user who cancelled their subscription over a frozen scroll ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/after-submitting-query-to-chatgpt-cant-scroll-at-all/696051)) `[search]`.
- **Two opposite wishes, same root.** One request asks the opposite of following: stop auto-scrolling once the question and the start of the answer are visible, add a «Jump to latest» button: «Don't automatically scroll the user through the entire response» (posted 8 Aug 2026) `[verified]` https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-smarter-auto-scrolling-for-long-chatgpt-responses/1389613. Other threads and tools ask to follow while the reader is at the bottom and stop the moment they scroll up, with «jump to latest» to resume ([21st.dev](https://21st.dev/blog/ai-chat-ui-components-react), [Telerik](https://feedback.telerik.com/blazor/1712929-implement-correct-auto-scroll-behavior-in-chat-control), [1min.ai board](https://feedback-1minai.userjot.com/board/p/option-to-disable-auto-scroll-during-ai-message-generation)) `[search]`. Pitfall they all name: a handler that reads its own layout growth as the user's scroll and freezes mid-answer; only real wheel/touch/key input should count `[search]` ([Medium](https://medium.com/@disgcfrguy/the-scroll-problem-nobody-talks-about-when-building-ai-chat-interface-987c223cafc0)).
- **Ask about one part of an answer without leaving it.** «Inline mini-chat pop-up»: asking about a passage of a long answer «breaks the reading flow» because the input is at the bottom; branching into a new chat «loses the context»; OpenAI support gave no timeline (May–June 2026) `[verified]` https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-inline-mini-chat-pop-up-for-quick-clarifications/1381173. Related requests: «Pop-Out Threads for Quoted Text», «Ask About This» sidebar, native message quoting, reply to an earlier message, pin/highlight/exclude messages `[search]`. ChatGPT already has «Ask ChatGPT» on selected text, which creates a reference chip above the composer ([bug report](https://community.openai.com/t/unrendered-markdown-in-the-ask-chatgpt-reply-feature/1388930)) `[search]`.
- **Find things again:** pin chats, search inside one chat, folders ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/pin-chats-and-search-chats/1001420), [thread](https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-pinning-and-organizing-chats-in-chatgpt/1129948)); branches as nested chats ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/feature-request-for-ui-threaded-chats/1325087)) `[search]`.
- **Long chats get slow:** typing lag because the whole thread is in the DOM; request for virtual scrolling ([thread](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-typing-lag-in-long-chats-needs-virtual-scroll-like-yesterday/1273495)) `[search]`.
- **Too long:** users and vendors keep fighting verbosity with prompts («concise»); an Intercom user asked for a short first answer with the option to expand ([Intercom](https://community.intercom.com/questions/5907)); no mainstream product exposes a plain length control `[search]`; a general «yapping» guide is a vendor blog (low weight).
- **Suggested follow-ups divide users.** Perplexity: a Reddit user never used them, another calls them «sort of pointless» and says they push the real answer down and wants to hide or fold them; when you click one, the others disappear ([r/perplexity_ai Jan 2025](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/perplexity_ai/comments/1i9drta/do_yall_actually_use_the_followup_questions), [Mar 2025](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/perplexity_ai/comments/1jhciwr/perplexity_replies_to_why_they_switched_follow_up)); a G2 reviewer likes them as a way to dig further; Perplexity tested labelled sponsored follow-ups (reported click rate not confirmed) `[search]`. NN/g's diary study (425 conversations, six types) found suggested follow-ups useful for most conversation types and recommends different ones by type: depth («define the term») for exploring, breadth («related facets») for chiseling, broader ones when it cannot answer `[verified]` https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-conversation-types/ ; short keyword prompts mark newcomers who need inline guidance (Simon Willison's summary, `[search]`).
- **Citations raise confidence only when you can see the words** (round 1 §2: HN NotebookLM threads; Tow Center; Stanford; BBC). Perplexity users value inline numbered sources but reviews are SEO-grade `[search]`; the hover card above is the most direct pattern seen.
- **Streaming speed:** no study of preferred typewriter speed was found. The closest is «Streaming, Fast and Slow» (arXiv 2504.17999, preprint): streaming faster than people read adds nothing; it proposes slowing down on dense passages (simulation, not a preference test) `[search]`; a tool's help page offers a user speed setting; a developer reports adding a small random delay per token `[search]`. Reading speed is a known quantity (~4 words/s for adults, `[inference: from general knowledge, not checked today]`), so a stream far above it gives no comfort: Pedro's note (the mock is too fast) is consistent with this.
- **Transparency of context:** research dashboards that show a chatbot's internal «user model» beside the chat raised users' sense of control (arXiv 2406.07882) `[search]`; UX guides say named, domain-scoped examples tell users what the bot can do before typing, a generic «How can I help?» does not `[search]`.
- **Bible and religious chats:** no real user reviews were reachable (Magisterium AI and Bible Chat searches returned store listings and directories only; Reddit and App Store are not indexed by the search tool). Facts only: Magisterium AI's free tier is rate-limited (90 queries/week) and its founder says it is «not a substitute for the authorities of the Church» `[search]`.

**Not reached:** Reddit and App Store reviews of Bible chats; Copilot and Grok in conversation; the logged-in «Ask ChatGPT» selection popup (my triple-click did not trigger it); mobile layouts of the products; NN/g articles other than the conversation types.

## 3. What it means for the drawer (the agent's reading, to discuss with Pedro) `[inference]`

1. **The composer is the product.** It is always visible, pinned, and the context controls live in or on it, not above the thread. Matches Pedro's note 4.
2. **Context is a status, not a form.** Seen once (full), afterwards one quiet line («Juan 6 · Reina-Valera 1909 · todo el corpus») that opens on demand. Matches Pedro's note 3; no product shows a settings block above the thread.
3. **Prefabricated questions are a starting aid, not a permanent list.** They belong to the empty state, change with how the drawer was opened (header button vs a verse selection), disappear once the chat has started, and come back as short follow-ups after an answer only if they are useful and foldable. NN/g and the Perplexity threads both point to «offer fewer, different by context».
4. **Scroll: do what the reader asked for and let them take over.** Follow only while at the bottom; the first wheel, touch or key scroll upward turns following off; a round ↓ button («Ir al final») resumes it; sending a new question resumes it. Alternative seen in ChatGPT and Claude: pin the question at the top and never follow. Pedro chose follow-with-release; the proposal also shows the pinned-question mode because one of the two main requests is for it.
5. **Streaming at reading speed, not model speed.** A calm default plus a speed choice; the current mock (55 ms per word, ~18 words/s) is about four times faster than reading speed.
6. **Show what it is doing while it works** (Claude's status line with a timer): for a corpus search it can say what is being consulted: «Buscando en los Padres», «Comprobando la cita en la edición».
7. **Features the products miss or users request, and fit Doctrina:** ask about one passage of an answer without leaving it (mini-thread / reply chip); pin an answer; find a past question; a length choice; the hover card with the quoted words for every source (already built). Each is shown as an option, none decided.
8. **Things to avoid, from the products' own noise:** upsell and notification banners above the composer, tiny disclaimers in grey (merge into one honest line), model pickers (one model), a sparkle/robot identity, typing dots, emoji reactions.
