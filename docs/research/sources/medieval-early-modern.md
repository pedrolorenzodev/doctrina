## 3. Creative alternatives and workarounds found
1. **One OCR job, two authors**: the Leonine ST volumes (open on archive.org) print Cajetan's full commentary under each article → PD Latin Aquinas + Cajetan together, OCR already decent (systematic "U"→"li" fix).
2. **Renewal-scoped reading of the Benziger 1947 Summa**: the renewals cover only "synoptical charts" / "appendices, indices & synoptical charts" → the Dominican translation itself stays free in the US (Stanford Copyright Renewal DB, R612635/R612636/R624562).
3. **HathiTrust copyright review as a free legal opinion**: HT has already opened Deferrari's Hugh of St Victor (1951, rights "pd"); the same check (catalog API `rightsCode`) can be run on any 1931–63 US translation we want (Bonaventure 1946/1963 still "Limited").
4. **BNE per-page PDFs** carry ABBYY text layers → the Spanish Summa can be harvested without OCR; ask BNE (info.repro@bne.es) for the full PDF/ALTO to avoid ~4,800 requests.
5. **EEBO-TCP CC0** covers the 16th–17th-c. English of Bossuet, Pascal, Bellarmine's catechism, the Roman Catechism, Canisius, Hosius, Sarpi, de Sales, Kempis, Erasmus' *Enchiridion*/*Paraphrases* — hand-keyed text, commercial use explicitly allowed.
6. **AI translations already published under PDM (Boyne Archives/churchlatin.ai)**: a usable *draft* for Cano 1563 and for Baronius/ASS/AAS (finding aid); never cite them as an edition.
7. **Paired dossiers available entirely from PD/CC0**: Paschasius (own) ↔ Ratramnus (TCP) ↔ Floyd's 1624 *Plea for the Real Presence* (TCP, Catholic reply to the Bertram edition); Bossuet *Exposition* (TCP 1672) ↔ Claude (TCP 1687); Pascal ↔ Nouet's Jesuit reply (TCP 1659); Bellarmine ↔ Whitaker/Perkins (overlooked-era); Erasmus *De libero arbitrio* (own) ↔ Luther (Cole 1823).
