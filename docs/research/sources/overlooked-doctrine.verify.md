## 7. SA I.4 and FC SD VIII.24 (bookofconcord.org text, Triglot-based)
- SA Part I, Art. 4 (page /smalcald-articles/i/): "IV. That the Son became man in this manner, that He was conceived, without the cooperation of man, by the Holy Ghost, and was born of the pure, holy [ and always] Virgin Mary." (the bracketed "and always" = Triglot editorial insertion for German "ewige/Latin semper"; does not appear as "ever virgin"). Previously-from-memory wording is NOT "ever virgin": correct to "pure, holy [and always] Virgin Mary".
- SD VIII.24 (/solid-declaration/, par. 24): "On account of this personal union and communion of the natures, Mary, the most blessed Virgin, bore not a mere man, but, as the angel [Gabriel] testifies, such a man as is truly the Son of the most high God, who showed His divine majesty even in His mother's womb, inasmuch as He was born of a virgin, with her virginity inviolate. Therefore she is truly the mother of God, and nevertheless remained a virgin." Also SD VII.100: "...when He was born of His mother [the most holy Virgin Mary]."
