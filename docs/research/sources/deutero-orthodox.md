### F5. OCR test 1 — Torres Amat, Barcelona 1884, Baruch 2:1–9 (AI158, page n277) (VERIFIED, hands-on)
Files: `samples/deutero-orthodox/ocr/` (bar2_gt.txt = my ground truth read from the image; bar2_left2_spa6.txt = tesseract; bar2_abbyy.txt = IA's OCR; score.py).
- Page layout is hard: 2 columns separated by an engraved pillar, right column warped by the binding curve.
- **Tesseract 5 `spa --psm 6` on a cropped clean column: CER 1.0%, WER 4.2% (240 words).** Residual errors: footnote superscript read as "1", "Ser"/"Obras" capitalised, "coImarcanos", final "." read as ",", 2 stray noise chars. All trivially fixable with a dictionary/LLM pass + human review.
- `spa --psm 4`: CER 4.7% (it dropped line ends); `spa_old`: CER 5.4%; `--psm 3`: CER 10%.
- **IA's existing ABBYY OCR: CER 2.2%, WER 10%, and it silently MODERNISES the orthography**: habia→había, traeria→traería, comeria→comería, desolacion→desolación, confusion→confusión; plus convertirnos→convertimos (rn→m), "hiio 1 v". This is the dangerous kind of error for a site that promises exact quotation: plausible, invisible, systematic. Don't use IA's text layer as-is; re-OCR with tesseract and use ABBYY only as a second witness for diffing.
- Italics in the original (Torres Amat marks words added for sense in italics: "ya", "por eso") are lost in plain-text OCR → hOCR/font info or manual pass needed if we want to keep them (they matter: they mark translator additions).

### F8. Jünemann's Spanish Septuagint — the one full Spanish LXX by an author dead > 80 years (legal reasoning; facts from es.wikipedia, VERIFIED page fetch)
- Wilhelm (Guillermo) Jünemann Beckschäfer (1855–1938), German-Chilean priest, translated the whole LXX OT "según los códices Vaticano, Sinaítico, Alejandrino" plus the NT. NT published 1928 (Concepción); **OT unpublished until 1992** (34 manuscript notebooks; ed. Gustavo Leiva Carrasco & Alfonso Naranjo Urrutia, Centro de ex alumnos del Seminario Conciliar de Concepción, Santiago; Chilean Episcopal Conference decree 70 (860/92), 8 Oct 1992). Includes "Libro III de Esdras" (= 1 Esdras) and "Libro III de los Macabeos" at the end. https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblia_de_J%C3%BCnemann
- Status by country (my reasoning, NOT legal advice): Chile and Argentina life+70 → translation PD since 1 Jan 2009; Spain life+80 (author died before 1987) → PD since 1 Jan 2019 (LPI art. 129.1 "obra inédita en dominio público" right does not apply because in 1992 it was not yet PD in Spain). **US: a work created before 1978 and first published 1978–2002 is protected until at least 31 Dec 2047 (17 U.S.C. §303(a))** → the OT is likely still © in the US, where the site is hosted. The NT (published Chile 1928) is PD in the US (pre-1931).
- Verdict: PERMISSION for the OT (rights owner = Jünemann's heirs/legatee, probably the Diocese/Archdiocese of Concepción or the Seminary alumni centre that published 1992 — to confirm). The editors' 1992 apparatus/notes are separately ©. Archive.org has a user upload of the 1992 PDF (`biblia-padre-guillermo-junemann`, uploader-supplied, no rights statement) — do NOT use it as a source.

### F13. OCR tests 2 and 3 (VERIFIED, hands-on; files in `samples/deutero-orthodox/ocr/`)
Method: crop the Spanish text column from the archive.org page image (`https://archive.org/download/<id>/page/n<N>.jpg`), run tesseract 5.x, compare with a ground truth I transcribed from the image, word/char edit distance (difflib). Footnote reference marks excluded.

### F14. CREATIVE FIND — a 16th-century PD Spanish 4 Maccabees inside the Spanish Josephus (VERIFIED in scan text)
- 4 Maccabees circulated for centuries as Josephus's "De imperio rationis" (via Erasmus's 1517 Latin recension). The Spanish Josephus of **Antwerp, Martín Nucio, 1554** — *Los veynte libros de Flauio Iosepho, de las antiguedades Iudaycas, y su vida ... con otro libro suyo del imperio de la razon, en el qual trata del martyrio de los Machabeos, todo nueuamente traduzido de Latin en Romance Castellano* — archive.org `bub_gb_T6UZsVWh8UcC` (Google scan, **Public Domain Mark**, 846 images, djvu.txt present). Its table lists "Libro muy elegante del imperio de la Razón, en que hermosamente trata el martirio de los Machabeos" (fol. ~381); the text (djvu.txt l. ~68500–70300) contains the 4 Macc material (David and the water = 4 Macc 3:6–18; Simon/Apollonius and Seleucus = 4 Macc 4:1–14). Sample: `samples/deutero-orthodox/macc4/josepho1554.txt`.
- A later translation by **Juan Martín Cordero (1520/31–1584)**: *Historia de las guerras de los judíos ... Quinta edición ... añadida la vida de Josefo y el Imperio de la razón o Martirio de los Machabeos, traducidos por el mismo Cordero*, Madrid, Benito Cano, 1791 — catalogued in BVMC with the scan hosted by the Fondo Antiguo, Universidad de Murcia (record fetched via browser: https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra/historia-de-las-guerras-de-los-judios-y-de-la-destruccion-del-templo-y-ciudad-de-jerusalen--escrita-en-griego-por-flavio-josefo-hebreo--y-traducida-por-juan-martin-cordero-tomo-i---quinta-edicion-fielmente-corregida-y-anadida-la-vida-de-josefo-y-el-imperio-de-la-razon-o-martirio-de-los-machabeos--traducidos-por-el-mismo-cordero/). Which tomo holds the *Imperio de la razón* not checked.
- **Caveat that must be shown to readers**: these are translations of Erasmus's Latin *paraphrase*, not of the Greek 4 Maccabees; no verse numbers; 16th-c. orthography. Usable as a historical Spanish witness ("cómo lo leía el mundo hispano"), not as the study text. Effort L (OCR of 1554 Gothic-free roman type, alignment to 4 Macc verses by hand).

### F15. Reina–Valera 1602 ("Biblia del Cántaro") also carries the full Protestant Apocrypha (VERIFIED in scan TOC)
- Scan `bub_gb_QgEIlTOPQhwC` (Amsterdam 1602, PD mark, 876 img; djvu.txt saved `samples/deutero-orthodox/oso/rv1602.txt`). Table of contents (l. ~1536–1552) lists, with their own foliation: "[3.] De Esdras", "[4.] De Esdras llamado ... Cap. Valera's preface (l. 547–549): "libros que han añadido son estos: El 3. y 4. que llaman de Esdras, la oracion de Manasse, el libro de Tobias, de Iudith, de la Sabiduria ..., el de Iesus hijo de Sirach ..., y el 1. y 2. de los Machabeos, y en algunas Biblias se halla el tercero."

This fits the "each tradition speaks in its own words" invariant.

### F17. BLM vs BLL quality spot check (VERIFIED, `usfm_spablm`, `usfm_spabll`)
- 2 Macc 12:43–45 (key text for the purgatory doctrine) and Wis 3:1–3: **spablm is a close, register-appropriate rendering of the WEB** ("ofrecer un sacrificio por el pecado ... orar por los muertos ... hizo el sacrificio expiatorio por los que habían muerto, para que fuesen absueltos de su pecado"). **spabll is a loose colloquial paraphrase** ("andarle rezando a los difuntos", "pagó el sacrificio", "fue un gran detalle de su parte") → unsuitable for a study site.

Using spablm on doctrine pages means quoting a Spanish rendering of an English rendering of the Greek → acceptable as a reading text with a visible label, not for word-level argument.
- Swete Odes (First1KGreek tlg028, CC BY-SA): 14 odes incl. **Ode 8 "Προσευχὴ Μαννασσή" (Prayer of Manasseh)** and Ode 14 "Ὕμνος ἑωθινός" (Gloria); Ps 151 present as chapter 151 of tlg027. OCR residue visible ("Κυρίχ·", "ΙΙροσευχὴ", "ΠΒ΄", marginal refs like "Luc. ii." inlined) → needs a cleanup pass. Samples `swete_odes.xml`, `swete_ps.xml`.
- Versification trap seen: WEB/BLM 2 Macc 12:45 contains what the Vulgate/Torres Amat number as 12:46 (LXX vs Vulgate numbering) → map with STEPBible TVTMS, never by verse number alone.
- **Data-quality flag**: eBible `eng-rv` (labelled "Revised Version with Apocrypha (1895)") 2 Esdras 7:35 reads "Recompense will follow ... wicked deeds won't sleep" — modern contractions that the 1895 RV would not use → this book may have been filled from WEB-derived text. Verify against an RV 1895 scan before using eng-rv as the "RV".
