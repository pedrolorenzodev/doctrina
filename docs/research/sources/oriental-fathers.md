## 1. Scope and method
Area: Syriac, Coptic, Armenian, Ethiopic, Church of the East, Desert Fathers, Arabic Christian. Method: (1) open corpora first — cloned Digital Syriac Corpus (632 TEI) and parsed Coptic Scriptorium meta.json (2,390 docs), checked Beta maṣāḥǝft; (2) PD English via archive.org advancedsearch/metadata (life dates from library authority strings), English Wikisource API, Roger Pearse's tertullian.org index (786 links parsed); (3) hands-on OCR of PD scans (Lamy Serto, Chabot Estrangela with tesseract syr; English OCR quality checks on 12 books); (4) Spanish: archive.org title/language search, web search, SciELO article (Google Books API unavailable: HTTP 429 quota); (5) copyright reasoning per jurisdiction (US pre-1931 / URAA; Argentina life+70; Spain life+80 for authors †<1987). Samples in samples/oriental-fathers/ (dsc/ clone, dsc-index.tsv, OCR texts, page images, OCR outputs).

- Jurisdiction note (my reasoning, not legal advice): DSC itself says base texts are PD. US: ancient text + editorial emendations of a critical edition is at most thin copyright; DSC re-keyed plain text. Spain LPI art. 129.2 (editions of unprotected works individualised by typography: 25 years) and Germany §70 UrhG (scientific editions: 25 years) — Beck CSCO 1959–63 and Charlesworth 1973 are long past 25 years; **Kitchen 2009 (Book of Steps) is inside the 25-year window until ~2034** in Spain/Germany. Mitigation: the Book of Steps base text is Kmosko 1926 (PD) — use DSC but record that it matches Kmosko, or OCR Kmosko for that one work. (Risk low: DSC re-keyed text does not reproduce Gorgias typography, which is what art. 129.2 protects.)
- Verdict: **READY** for Syriac originals of Aphrahat, Narsai, Jacob (selected), Isaac (Part I), Philoxenus, Odes, Book of Steps, Ephrem (Nisibene, Nativity, prose). Attribution "Digital Syriac Corpus, CC BY 4.0" + original edition. Effort S (TEI → our JSON).

### Copyright yardsticks used in this report (2026)
- US: published before 1931 = PD (all items below dated ≤1930 are PD in the US regardless of translator death).
- Argentina (Ley 11.723 art. 5): life + 70 → translators who died ≤1955 are PD.
- Spain: authors who died before 7-Dec-1987 get life + **80** (LPI disp. transitoria 4ª) → only authors who died **≤1945** are PD in 2026. Source: CEDRO FAQ / blog "Apuntes sobre el dominio público en España" (2026-04-14) https://www.cedro.org/blog/articulo/blog.cedro.org/2026/04/14/apuntes-sobre-el-dominio-p%C3%BAblico-en-espa%C3%B1a and https://www.cedro.org/propiedad-intelectual/preguntas-frecuentes (search-result summary; I did not open the BOE text itself — see §6).
- Consequence that matters in this area: **R. H. Connolly (1873–1948)** translations (Narsai 1909, Didascalia 1929) are PD in US and Argentina but **protected in Spain until 31-Dec-2028** (PD 1-Jan-2029). Dates from archive.org metadata of liturgicalhomili00narsuoft ("Connolly, R. Hugh (Richard Hugh), 1873-1948").

### 2.2 Ephrem the Syrian (Syriac, †373)
- Original (DSC, CC BY 4.0): Nisibene hymns (Beck CSCO 1961/63), Nativity 1–28 (Beck CSCO 186, 1959), Prose Refutations, prose "On Virginity", Discourses to Hypatius. Missing from DSC: Hymns on Faith (87), on the Church, on Virginity (madrashe), on Paradise, Unleavened Bread (Azymes), Crucifixion, Resurrection, Commentary on the Diatessaron.
- PD Syriac for the missing ones: **T. J. Lamy, Sancti Ephraem Syri Hymni et Sermones, 4 vols (Mechelen 1882–1902)**, Syriac + Latin facing, archive.org ids sanctiephraemsy00lamygoog (vol 1, 1902 print), sanctiephraemsy02lamygoog (vol 2, 1886), sanctiephraemsy01/04lamygoog (vol 3, 1882), sanctiephraemsy03lamygoog (vol 4, 1902), all flagged NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT. Vol 1 contains Epiphany (baptism) hymns and the Holy-Week cycle (Unleavened Bread / Crucifixion / Resurrection), plus Marian hymns elsewhere in the set. Also Assemani's Roman edition (1732–46, Syriac+Latin) — PD (memory; not checked on archive.org).
  - **Hands-on OCR test (done):** Lamy vol 1, scan page n120 (pp. 51–52, Epiphany hymn on baptism). `tesseract -l syr` on the Serto column gave usable Syriac, e.g. "ܢܘܪܐ ܗܝ ܓܝܪ ܕܟܡܢܐܝܬ / ܗܐ ܪܫܡܐ ܐܦ ܠܓܙܪܗ̇ / ܒܫܡܗ̈ܐ ܬܬܐ…", with ~1 error per line (e.g. ܡܥܠܡܕ for ܡܥܡܕ). Latin column OCR is near-perfect. Files: samples/oriental-fathers/lamy-n120-small.jpg, lamy-syr-ocr.txt, lamy-lat-ocr.txt. → Syriac from Lamy is feasible with OCR + correction (ideally checked by a Syriac reader). Lamy's Latin is a loose paraphrase, useful only as a crib.
- English PD:
  - NPNF2 vol 13 (1898): Nisibene Hymns (selection, 48 Wikisource pages), Hymns on the Nativity (21), Hymns for the Epiphany (17), The Pearl (= Hymns on Faith 81–87, 9 pages), Homilies (5: On Our Lord, On Admonition and Repentance, On the Sinful Woman, etc.). Full on English Wikisource (128 pages total for the Ephraim/Aphrahat part, verified via API) — **READY**.
  - **J. B. Morris, Select Works of S. Ephrem the Syrian (Oxford, Library of the Fathers 41, 1847)** — archive.org selecsep00ephr (Princeton TS), also SelectWorksOfSEphremTheSyrian, selectworksseph00morrgoog. Contents (verified from OCR): 13 Rhythms on the Nativity; Rhythm against the Jews (Palm Sunday); The Pearl (7 Rhythms on Faith); **Eighty Rhythms upon the Faith against the Disputers (= Hymns on Faith 1–80, complete)**; 3 Rhythms against the Scrutinizers. Morris †1880 → PD everywhere. OCR is readable but 2-column with footnotes interleaved → needs cleanup (WORK M). The Hymns on Faith contain Ephrem's key Eucharist texts (fire and Spirit in the Bread, "Medicine of Life") and statements on Church authority ("the Church alone could teach the faith" quoted in Morris' note).
  - H. Burgess, Select Metrical Hymns and Homilies of Ephraem Syrus (London 1853) — archive.org selectmetricalhy00ephrrich etc. (PD; contents not yet checked).
  - C. W. Mitchell, S. Ephraim's Prose Refutations (1912/1921) — PD in US; Mitchell's death date unknown (unverified) → check before Spain use.
- Spanish: none PD found (see §4).
- Doctrine value: very high for Mary (Nativity hymns: Mary's virginity, "Mary and Eve"), Eucharist (Faith 10, Azymes, Crucifixion), Baptism (Epiphany hymns), saints/departed (Nisibene 35–77 on death and Hades), Church (Faith hymns). - Verdict: READY (NPNF parts, Wikisource); WORK (Morris 1847 Faith hymns: OCR cleanup, M); WORK (Azymes/Crucifixion/Church/Virginity: Lamy Syriac OCR + own EN/ES translation, L). Spanish: own translation.
