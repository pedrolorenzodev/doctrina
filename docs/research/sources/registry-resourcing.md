## Scope and method

This report covers the 44 rows in `data/site/sources.extra.json` and the 127 citation/witness rows in `data/raw/eucharist.content.json`: 106 non-Bible rows (stable identifiers Q0–Q105 from the audit inventory) and 21 Bible rows (B0–B20). The five additional John 6 passages that `registry-audit.md` recommends withholding are not rows in the Eucharist raw file and therefore are outside Task E.

[verified: OCR] “OCR” below means the stored Internet Archive `_djvu.txt` for the cited dated scan was read and matched to the viewer leaf obtained through Internet Archive full-text search. It does **not** mean that every page image was independently proofread in this pass. The audit warns that API leaves can be ±1; rows Q20 and Q100 retain that warning. Editorial square-bracket ellipses in held excerpts are preserved and are not represented as words printed in the source.

The legal shorthand **PD-all** means: public domain in the United States because the edition was published before 1931, and the named English contributor died no later than 1945 (or an expired anonymous/corporate publication term applies), using the conservative common line in [`legal-gaps.md` §2.3](legal-gaps.md#23-what-safe-in-all-target-countries-means-translator-died-in-year-x-date-checked--2026). It does not remove the separate paying-public-domain questions in Argentina, Uruguay, or Bolivia. This is research, not legal advice.
