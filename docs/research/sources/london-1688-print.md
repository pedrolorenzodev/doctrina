## The scan

- archive.org item `bim_early-english-books-1641-1700_a-confession-of-faith-p_1688`, title «A confession of faith, put forth by the elders … 1688», 169 images, «Digitized from IA40310604-13» (Early English Books 1641–1700 microfilm; the images are black-and-white microfilm frames). `[verified: archive.org metadata]`.
- From the OCR text of the item (title page image read by verifier V7, leaf n2): «A Confession of Faith, Put forth by the Elders and Brethren of many Congregations of Christians (Baptized upon Profession of their Faith) in London and the Country. With an Appendix concerning Baptism … London: Printed for John Harris, at the Harrow against the Church in the Poultrey. 1688», and, on the leaf before the title page, «Aug. 15. 1688. Licensed, and Entered according to Order». `[verified: OCR text only]`. Chapter XXIX «Of Baptism» begins on printed p. 97 (leaf n120); the OCR contents lines are garbled and give no legible page numbers. Whether this print is the one the 1689 General Assembly adopted is **not** claimed here.
- Page numbers below are the **printed** pages; the image number is the item's leaf (`/page/n<leaf>`); in this item printed page N is leaf n(N+23) for the opening chapters, and printed p. 97 is leaf n120.

## What this changes and does not change

- **Does not change** the decided primary (1719): this is a research finding for Pedro's call. It shows that the 1719 and the 1688 print **differ** at three of the four loci collated (1.5 note b; 29.1 note «Rom.6.2.4.» against the 1719's «Rom, 6. 24.»; 29.2 note f), where the 1719 differs from the 1688 and the 1677 (which agree with each other), and agree at 1.4 (where 1677 differs) and at the other two 29.1 notes; 1.1 was not collated. So «1719 reproduces 1688» is false as a general statement, and the labelled edition in each citation matters.
- Option for Pedro (not recorded as a decision): cite the 1688 print as primary where a scan is read, 1719 as the variant, or keep 1719 as primary with the 1688 variant noted; either way the rows must not mix editions in one citation.
- **Not done:** the rest of the margin (1.6–1.10, 26.3–26.4, 28, 29.3–29.5, 30.3–30.4), any text variant (only margin notes were read), the Appendix on baptism, the page images of the front matter, a check whether the microfilm leaf order matches the print's signatures.
