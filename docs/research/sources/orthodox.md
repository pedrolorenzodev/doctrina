### O5. OCR test — Greek of the Confession of Dositheus, Decree XVII (Eucharist) (VERIFIED, hands-on; files in `samples/orthodox/ocr/`)
- **Kimmel 1850 Google scan (`monumentafideie00kimmgoog`) is physically cropped**: the left edge of every Greek column is cut off in the scan (page 457 = leaf n528: "ιος παρέδωκε", "αρεδίδου"...). Unusable for the Greek. **Use the Harvard copy of the 1843 first issue, *Libri symbolici Ecclesiae Orientalis* (`librisymbolicie00kimmgoog`, 615 images, same pagination: Dositheus XVII on p. 457 = leaf n563)**, which is complete.
- Kimmel 1843, tesseract 5 `grc --psm 6` on the Greek column (`k43_grc.txt`): ~170 words, roughly **18% word errors** (worn 1840s type with ligatures and ϑ: "ἐνεῖμο" for ἐκεῖνο, "φωῆς" for ζωῆς, "μετοψσιρῦσϑαι", "δοξάβξρυσιν", "Λουϑήρου" lost initial). IA's own OCR of the same page is similar. → Kimmel is a collation witness, not the OCR base.
- **Schaff 1877 vol. II, p. 427 (leaf n440)**, same decree, tesseract `grc --psm 6` on the Greek column (`schaff_grc.txt`): **~90% words correct**; errors are systematic and fixable (ϑ read as σ/π: "μασηταῖς", "καπσώς", "ἑνούσσαι"; dropped smooth breathings "ανωτέρω", "αφεσιν"; stray accent-only lines between text lines, trivially filtered). Schaff reprints Kimmel's text (stated in his headnote). → **Greek base = Schaff vol. II (clean modern type) OCR'd with tesseract, collated against Kimmel 1843, human-reviewed. Effort M per confession.** The IA ABBYY layer of Schaff's Greek is useless (it read Greek as Latin: "Ώιστίύομίν").
- English Robertson 1899 OCR is near-clean (see O1) → READY after normalisation.
