Agent: free-church · run date 2026-10-09 · status: COMPLETE (written incrementally)

## 1. Scope and method
Area: Baptist (1644/46, 1677/89, Orthodox Creed 1678, Philadelphia, New Hampshire, Abstract of Principles, BF&M 1925/1963/2000, catechisms), Anabaptist/Mennonite (Schleitheim, Dordrecht, Martyrs Mirror, Menno, Riedemann, Hubmaier), Methodist (Articles of Religion, sermons, Notes), Pentecostal (AG SFT 1916/current), Evangelical consensus (Lausanne, Manila, Cape Town, Chicago), Spurgeon, Spanish evangelical classics, open datasets.
Method: GitHub (cloned Creeds.json and reformed-standards; GitHub search API for 8 queries, READMEs read); archive.org advancedsearch + metadata + djvu.txt downloads (McGlothlin 1911, Böhmer 1910, Menno 1871, Martyrs Mirror, Vedder 1905, Hodge-es 1897, Disciplina 1883, Valera 1851); HathiTrust catalog/full-text search (via Chrome); en/es/de Wikisource API; Stanford Copyright Renewal DB (via Chrome); rights pages opened directly (Lausanne, SBC/BF&M, AG, Alliance/ICBI, DTS archives, GAMEO, Wesley Center, IEW/WHF, menonitas.org, Chapel Library PDFs, Peregrino PDFs); CrossWire module pages; WebSearch for leads only. Hands-on tests: tesseract `frk` on a Böhmer Fraktur page (near-perfect), OCR spot checks of 7 PD scans, OCR of the full 1978 ICBI typescript (no notice), range-fetch of the 198 MB SBC 1963 Annual (first 6 MB, title page), difflib overlap 1689 vs WCF. Google Books API was out of quota (shared key) and Wikimedia API rate-limited at times (429) — worked around via Chrome. Total samples < 60 MB.

### 2.1 Baptist confessions

**Key PD anthology found: W. J. McGlothlin, *Baptist Confessions of Faith* (Philadelphia: American Baptist Publication Society, 1911).** archive.org: https://archive.org/details/baptistconfessio00mcgl (also `bapconfe00mcgl`, `cu31924029452566`, Google scan `baptistconfessi00mcglgoog`). Published 1911 → PD in the US; McGlothlin d. 1933 → PD in Argentina (life+70) and Spain (d. before 1987 → life+80 = 2013). Preface (verified in OCR): English confessions reproduced "as nearly as possible exactly" from original editions; "The translations, except that from the Swedish, are by the author." So his own English translations of Anabaptist/German/French texts are PD too. Contents (verified from OCR TOC): Schleitheim ("Earliest Anabaptist Articles" — note: translated from **Zwingli's Latin** version, not the German), other Bern articles, **Riedemann's Rechenschaft (extracts)**, Appeal to the Lords of Lichtenstein, Mennonite earliest confession c.1580, **Waterland "Brief Confession" (de Ries/Gerrits)**, Smyth's Short Confession, Helwys 1611 (Amsterdam), Standard Confession 1660, **Orthodox Creed 1678**, Somerset, **First London 1644 (full)**, 1651 "Several Churches", **Second London 1677/1689 (full)**, **Philadelphia**, **New Hampshire 1833**, Free Will Baptist Treatise, German Baptist (Oncken) confession, French Baptist confession, Swedish. OCR (djvu.txt, 857 KB) quality: good, typical hyphenation/double-space artifacts; usable as base text after cleanup. Saved: `samples/free-church/mcglothlin1911.txt`.
Also PD: E. B. Underhill, *Confessions of Faith… of the Baptist Churches of England in the 17th Century* (Hanserd Knollys Society, 1854) — https://archive.org/details/confessionsfaith00undeuoft . Lumpkin, *Baptist Confessions of Faith* (Judson 1959; rev. 1969, 2011) is the modern standard but **not usable** (1959 → check renewal; revised eds. ©) — McGlothlin replaces it.

## 3. Creative alternatives and workarounds

1. **McGlothlin 1911 as the single PD backbone for Baptist + early Anabaptist texts in English** (incl. his own PD translations of German/French/Dutch confessions). Replaces Lumpkin (1959/1969 ©-entangled). One OCR source, one citation, many confessions.
2. **Fraktur is solved locally**: `tesseract -l frk` on archive.org page images (`/download/<id>/page/n<N>_w2000.jpg`) gave near-perfect 16th-c. German from Böhmer 1910. Same route opens Riedemann 1902, Hubmaier prints, Menno's German editions, *Ausbund*, etc. without anyone's transcription.
3. **Spanish 1689 via a PD Spanish Westminster** (Hodge/Arellano, México 1897): measured overlap means ~40% of the 1689 text can come nearly verbatim from an 1897 PD Spanish source and ~26% more by light adaptation; translate the rest. Alternative: ask Peregrino.
4. **Authentic 19th-c. Spanish Methodist Articles** (México 1883 Discipline) — no translation needed; same trick likely works for other Spanish mission-era church manuals on Emory's "americanmethodism" collection (e.g. *El ritual de la Iglesia metodista episcopal* 1917).
5. **US non-renewal / no-notice route**: BF&M 1963 (confirmed by Wikisource's PD-US-not-renewed tag + my own Stanford search + no notice on the Annual's title page); Lumpkin 1959 (probably not renewed); ICBI 1978 typescript bears no notice (not relied upon).
6. **Rights holders who already grant reuse**: Alliance of Confessing Evangelicals (Chicago Statement articles: "may be reproduced without permission… credit"), Lausanne for the English Cape Town Commitment ("may be reproduced… without permission"). Use those exactly as granted.
7. **Spanish Protestant originals instead of translations**: Valera's *Dos tratados del Papa y de la Misa* (1588/1599/1851) and the *Reformistas Antiguos Españoles* corpus give Doctrina Spanish-language Protestant primary sources on papacy, Mass, indulgences/purgatory, justification — all PD, no translation needed.
8. **CC0/Apache datasets** save the parsing work: reformed-standards (1689 with OSIS proofs, Apache-2.0), BaptistSymbolics (CC0), Creeds.json (Unlicense), lyteword Spurgeon (CC0), CrossWire PD modules (Wesley's Notes, 1646 confession, Spurgeon M&E).
