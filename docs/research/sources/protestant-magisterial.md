### Hands-on OCR test 1 — Schaff, Creeds of Christendom III (1877), Heidelberg Catechism p. 307 (parallel German Fraktur / English)
- Source: archive.org creedschristendo03scha (1877, UC scan), leaf n322 image (https://archive.org/download/creedschristendo03scha/page/n322_w2000.jpg).
- archive.org's own OCR (djvu.txt) mixes the two columns and destroys the Fraktur ("2öa§ tft bein einiger Sroft im Se'6eit unb"); English has errors ("son]", "bnt").
- Re-OCR with simple column cropping (PIL) + tesseract: `-l frk` on the German column → "Was iſt dein einiger Troſt im Leben und im Sterben? ... Daß ich mit Leib und Seele, beides im Leben und im Sterben, nicht mein, ſondern meines getreuen Heilandes Jeſu Chriſti eigen bin" (0 errors in the sample; long-s kept, trivially normalisable); `-l eng` on the English column → 0 errors in the sample. Samples: samples/protestant-magisterial/heid-de.png, heid-en.png.
- Schaff's note on that page: German = 3rd ed. 1563 in modern spelling; English = the Tercentenary translation by the German Reformed Church committee (Gerhart, Nevin, Harbaugh et al., New York 1863) "inserted by permission of the translators" → 1863 translation, PD.
- Conclusion: Schaff vol. III (1877–1919 printings, all PD) is a practical master source for the original + PD English of Heidelberg, Belgic (French+English), Canons of Dort (Latin+English), Scots Confession, Second Helvetic (Latin + English in 3rd ed. appendix), 39 Articles (Latin+English 1571), Westminster Standards, Zwingli's 67 Articles, Ten Theses of Berne, First Helvetic, Gallican, Irish and Lambeth Articles, Augsburg Confession (Latin/German+English), Small Catechism, Formula of Concord Epitome. Pipeline = column crop + tesseract (frk/lat/fra/eng) + human proofreading. Effort M per volume. en.wikisource has started this volume (Gallican, Irish, Lambeth, SHC appendix done; rest not).

## 3. Creative alternatives / workarounds found
1. **BNE Digital = CC BY 4.0 for PD scans** (attribution "Biblioteca Nacional de España", no prior permission) — verified on Valera 1597 and Spanish BCP 1864 cards. Use as image source and citation edition for Spanish Reformation texts.
2. **Mexican Presbyterian mission imprints (1880, 1897)** held by Princeton Theological Seminary and scanned to archive.org/HathiTrust: Spanish WCF + Shorter Catechism (Thomson 1880) and Hodge commentary (Arellano 1897). Search pattern that worked: HathiTrust full-text phrase search in Spanish, full-view limit.
3. **Column-crop + tesseract `frk`/`spa_old`** turns unusable archive.org OCR of parallel-column/Fraktur books into near-perfect text (Schaff test: 0 errors in sample). One pipeline unlocks Schaff III (≈ 15 of our confessions, original + PD English).
4. **Stanford renewal DB** clears Philadelphia Luther vols 4 and 6 (no renewal) and confirms vol 5 becomes PD on 2027-01-01.
5. **Fingerprinting translations** (a distinctive phrase → web search) exposed mislabeled "Public Domain" texts in Creeds.json/christian-standards/CCEL creeds pages.
6. **Permission partners with Spanish texts**: Misión Luterana de Puerto Rico (full BoC, claims "dominio público"/CC BY icons — ask for written CC BY on the confession texts), Editorial ATR (WCF 2024, WLC+WSC 2025), CPH (Meléndez Libro de Concordia), Faith Alive/CRCNA (Belgic/Heidelberg/Dort Spanish), Church Pension Fund (LOC 1989), CUP (BCP UK).
7. **Own translation** from PD originals (Triglot Latin/German, Schaff Latin/French) labelled as Doctrina's, where no PD Spanish exists (Book of Concord, Belgic, Dort, SHC, Scots, Zwingli, WLC).

## 4. Blockers that remain
- No PD Spanish translation of any Lutheran confession (searched archive.org, HathiTrust, Google Books full view, BNE, es.wikisource). Spanish Lutheran texts all date from 1973+ and are ©, except the PR volunteer translation of unclear licence.
- No PD Spanish Belgic, Dort, Second Helvetic, Scots Confession, Westminster Larger Catechism found.
- BCP 1662 in the UK: Crown prerogative (perpetual); CUP's permissions page is currently 404, so terms are known only from a search snippet.
- Luther's Confession Concerning Christ's Supper (1528): English only in LW 37 (©, renewed).
- Spanish translator death dates (Arellano, Thomson) unverified → AR/ES status inferred, not proven (US PD is certain).
