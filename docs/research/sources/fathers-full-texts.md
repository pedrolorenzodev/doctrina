## Method and acceptance rule

The controlling universe is the 77 verified entries in `docs/research/neutral/fathers-verification.md`: 75 Tier-1 entries plus Andrew of Crete and Germanus I, because both are already displayed in `data/raw/history.json`. “Complete” means every work listed for the entry in the corresponding neutral-universe file, not merely the passages quoted by doctrine dossiers. A surviving fragmentary corpus may be complete as extant, but the record must say so.

A reader route is accepted only when all of the following are recorded:

1. exact printed edition and, for translations, translator;
2. publication year and a dated page-image scan;
3. a digital text usable only as OCR/collation aid unless it is itself scan-backed and legally reusable;
4. copyright status in the United States, Spain and every Latin-American country under the common 2026 launch line: author/translator died in 1945 or earlier;
5. any separate licence or contractual restriction on the digital transcription;
6. a `FULL`, `PARTIAL`, `LINK ONLY` or `NONE VERIFIED` verdict for each language.

Doctrina rebuilds text from the dated scan with its own OCR and paragraph-level scan/page provenance. Wikisource, Gutenberg, CCEL, New Advent and similar sites are witnesses, never the edition of record unless their text is demonstrably scan-backed and reusable. A modern translation cannot be copied merely because the ancient original is public domain. When no safe full translation exists, Doctrina may translate from a public-domain original and label it under the three-level Spanish policy; it does not translate from a CC BY-SA transcription.
