My reasoning (not verified legal advice): with life+50 in force before 1996, Pius XI (d. 1939) texts were PD in the source country before 1996 so no URAA restoration; Pius XII (d. 1958) texts were still protected on 1 Jan 1996, so a URAA restoration argument exists for any AAS Pius XII text first published 1939-1958 -> US term 95 years from publication -> e.g. 2034-2053. Treat Pius XII as risky; Pius XI and earlier as safe-ish.
