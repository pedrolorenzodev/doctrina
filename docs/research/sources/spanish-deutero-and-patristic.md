## Copyright term and edition boundary

Argentina's current Ley 11.723, article 5, gives life plus seventy years counted from the following 1 January; article 24 treats a translator's version as distinct. Spain's TRLPI articles 11, 26 and 30 distinguish translations and apply the ordinary seventy-year term, while transitional provision four preserves the earlier term for deaths before 7 December 1987. The 1879 law's article 6 supplies eighty years. Thus, for the historical translators below, the working 2026 death-year cutoffs are 1955 for Argentina and 1945 for Spain. These are term calculations, not permissions for protected modern revisions. `[CONFIRMED: primary statutes, checked 2026-10-28]` [Argentina, updated law](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/42755/actualizacion); [Spain, current TRLPI](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930); [Spain, 1879 law](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-1879-40001&lang=es).

The already completed [country audit](legal-gaps.md) and [Catholic Bible report](catholic-spanish-bibles.md) retain a separate unresolved product/legal question about paying-public-domain regimes. Copyright term expiry does not itself settle that question. This acquisition report does not change the owner's launch rule, resolve foreign-hosting applicability, or reproduce newly copyrighted notes and typography.
