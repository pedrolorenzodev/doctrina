Many of these sit on archive.org/Scribd as user uploads with fake dates (e.g. `la-biblia-comentada-por-los-padres-de-la-iglesia-*` "1888/1898/1900" = Ciudad Nueva's 2000s Spanish ACCS; `GrandesMisticos1/CrisostomoSanJuan-HomilasI.pdf` = Ramírez Torres; `padres-apostolicos-ed.-daniel-ruiz-bueno`). **Never use these.**

**Conclusion for Spanish:** for ~95 % of the Greek Fathers there is no usable PD Spanish. The realistic path is **our own translation**: from the PD/CC Greek (F1K/PTA/OGC), with the PD English (ANF/NPNF/Lightfoot/Lake) as a cross-check, AI-drafted, human-reviewed, published as "Traducción de Doctrina (borrador revisado)" with the Greek source edition named. This is legally clean: translating a PD original creates our own copyright. The only things to avoid: translating *from* a © translation (that would be a derivative) and translating from a © critical edition where the edition itself is protected (see §6 Melito / Palamas / Symeon). Translating from a CC BY-SA source (F1K Greek) makes our translation an "adaptation" → it must be CC BY-SA too. Translating from CC BY sources (PTA BY files, OGC OCR, CGPG) or PD Migne → we may license as we wish. **Decision point for Pedro**: if our Spanish translations must be proprietary (paid product), translate from the PD edition underlying the F1K file (Migne / pre-1931 GCS / Lightfoot), not from the CC BY-SA TEI itself — or accept CC BY-SA for the translations (compatible with selling access to the site, but anyone could copy the translations).

## 6. Creative alternatives and workarounds found

1. **Open Greek Corpus (open-greek, 2026)** already solved most of the "Greek Fathers aren't digitised openly" problem: it serves 3,909 Greek works, including ~350 patristic works OCR'd from PD Migne PG (Qwen3.6-27B OCR + measured correction) under CC BY 4.0 and the calfa-co CGPG OCR (CC BY 4.0). It explicitly excludes NC texts and uses TLG only as an offline inventory. This is exactly our policy, done at scale. Per-work JSONL: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-greek/open-greek-corpus/main/data/corpus/<slug>.jsonl`; catalogue: `data/corpus_catalog.tsv` (saved locally: samples/greek-fathers/ogc_catalog.tsv). Caveat: OCR text needs a proofreading pass before we show it as "the original" — or show it labelled "texto griego: OCR de Migne PG, en revisión" (draft, per our trust invariant).
2. **Our own Migne OCR for the gaps** (Maximus, John Damascene, Germanus, Cabasilas, Sophronius, Andrew of Crete, Photius Mystagogy): tested hands-on. archive.org PG 94 page image (`https://archive.org/download/patrologicursus53migngoog/page/n690_w1800.jpg`), cropped Greek column, `tesseract -l grc --psm 6`: ~93–95 % of words right; the dominant error (κ read as χ) is systematic and fixable with a lexicon pass (OGC's public lemma frequency table `data/public_lemma_frequency.tsv` is a free lexicon for that) + LLM correction with a stronger-model spot check. Roger Pearse maintains the volume→scan map: https://www.roger-pearse.com/weblog/patrologia-graeca-pg-pdfs/ .
3. **PTA critical editions** (BBAW): for Athanasius *De incarnatione* we get a modern critical text under CC BY-SA — better than Migne — and Hippolytus *Comm. Daniel* with a CC BY German translation (a second pivot for our Spanish/English).
4. **Project Gutenberg / Distributed Proofreaders** is currently working through the *Ante-Nicene Christian Library* (T&T Clark) — double-proofread English texts with no CCEL strings attached. Watch for more volumes (Justin & Athenagoras, Irenaeus, Tatian/Theophilus, Methodius).
5. **Tertullian Project "Additional texts"** (Roger Pearse): https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/ — states verbatim "These English translations are all out of copyright … Please take copies and place online elsewhere … These [introductions] are all placed in the public domain also -- copy freely." Useful items for us: Robinson's Irenaeus *Demonstration*, Ferrar's Eusebius *Demonstratio*, Pusey's Cyril (*That Christ is One*, *Against Nestorius*, *Scholia*), Basil *Address to Young Men*, Nyssa *Life of Macrina*, Chrysostom extras. Caveat: "out of copyright" is Pearse's (UK-based) judgement; check each translator's date before relying on it (some items, e.g. the *Adversus Judaeos* translation, are from 20th-c. sources — verify).
6. **Buy-and-scan** of undigitised PD Spanish books (Caminero 1878–79, 5 vols; Segalá–Parpal 1916): legitimate, cheap, and gives us Spanish Fathers nobody else has online. Needs translator death-date research for the 1916 volume (Spain: died before 1946).
7. **Legacy Google Books feed** `https://www.google.com/books/feeds/volumes?q=…&min-viewability=full` still answers when the v1 API daily quota (shared IP) is exhausted — useful for the mechanical verification pass. Script: samples/greek-fathers/gbfeed.py.
8. **Stanford Copyright Renewal DB** via the browser (plain fetch hits a bot check; the CDP Chrome passes after ~10 s) — used to settle Melito/Bonner (renewed) and Easton (no renewal found).
9. **Quotation rights as a bridge** for blocked items (Melito Peri Pascha, Didymus Comm. Zech., Palamas Triads, Symeon NT): short quotations with citation inside a doctrine page (Argentina Ley 11.723 art. 10: up to 1,000 words per quote for teaching/criticism; Spain LPI art. 32) + "read in full at publisher" link. Not a full-reader solution.
10. **Pivot through PD Latin**: where no PD Greek exists (Symeon NT → Pontanus' 1603 Latin in PG 120; Didymus *De Spiritu Sancto* → Jerome's Latin in PG 39), translate from the PD Latin and label it as such.

## 9. The AI-translation path (Greek → ES/EN), concretely

- Source text: F1K/PTA TEI (clean) or OGC/own Migne OCR after correction. Even "clean" F1K has typos: Ignatius *Smyrn.* 7.1 in F1K reads "πρυσευχῆς" for προσευχῆς (checked in `tlg1443.tlg001.1st1K-grc1.xml`), and apparatus notes in Beta Code sit inline in `<note>` elements — strip notes, then spell-check against a lexicon.
- Draft: model translation paragraph by paragraph, aligned to the citation units (chapter.section), with the PD English (ANF/NPNF/Lake) given as a reference so terminology stays standard. Mechanical bulk drafting can go to a cheaper model; a stronger model (or a human) reviews doctrinally loaded passages (Eucharist, justification, Mary, papacy) — those are exactly the ones the site quotes.
- Label: "Traducción: Doctrina, a partir del texto griego de [edición], revisión [fecha/estado]". Until reviewed → marked draft (trust invariant).
- Licence consequence: see §4 (translating from CC BY-SA TEI → CC BY-SA translation; from PD Migne / CC BY OCR → free to license as we want).
- Hands-on sample (produced in this run, unreviewed), Ignatius *Smyrn.* 7.1 from F1K/Lake Greek: «Se abstienen de la eucaristía y de la oración, porque no confiesan que la eucaristía es la carne de nuestro Salvador Jesucristo, la que padeció por nuestros pecados y a la que el Padre, en su bondad, resucitó. Así, los que contradicen el don de Dios mueren en sus disputas; más les valdría amar, para resucitar también ellos.» — matches Lake/ANF sense; shows the route works for short doctrinal passages immediately.
