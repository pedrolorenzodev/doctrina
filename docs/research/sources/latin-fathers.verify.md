## 1. Zeballos Confesiones — ucm.532026603x and ucm.5327134242
- API: `ucm.532026603x` rightsCode `pd`, usRightsString "Full view", record 009348808, 1793; `ucm.5327134242` rightsCode `pd`, record 009314593, 1781. Item page: "Public Domain, Google-digitized."
- HT definition (https://www.hathitrust.org/the-collection/preservation/rights-database/): attribute 1 `pd` = "public domain"; attribute 9 `pdus` = "public domain only when viewed in the US". Spanish works pre-1899 get `pd` by bibliographic rule ("published outside of the US before 1899").
- Contents: **both are "tomo segundo"**. 1793 3rd ed. (Madrid, viuda e hijo de Marín, [16]+491 p., title page "TERCERA EDICION. TOMO SEGUNDO"); Tabla = Libro VII (p.1), VIII (p.97), IX (p.194), X (p.300); last page "FIN DEL TOMO II", running head "LIB. X. CAP. XLII". The 1781 volume (Madrid, Pedro Marín, [16]+491 p.) has the same Tabla. Tomo I (Books I–VI) is a different item (catalog search "Zeballos confesiones Augustin" → 6 full-view results, incl. "tomo I" 1786). I did NOT verify whether Zeballos translated Books XI–XIII (tomo II ends in Book X).
- Text: HT text-only (Google OCR) of sampled pages reads clean (accents and "i/y" spelling kept; some page-header noise). Eyeball estimate <3 % word errors, not measured. Samples: `z93_*.txt`, `zz_*.txt`.
- Terms warning (applies to every "Public Domain, Google-digitized" item): HT Access & Use Policy (https://www.hathitrust.org/the-collection/search-access/access-use-policy/): "Google requests that the images and OCR not be re-hosted, redistributed or used commercially. The images are provided for educational, scholarly, non-commercial purposes. Note: There are no restrictions on use of text transcribed from the images, or paraphrased or translated using the images." → do our own OCR/transcription from the images; do not ship Google's OCR text or images.
