# Latin Fathers — source research (agent: latin-fathers)

Status: complete (see end of file).

## 1. Scope and method

Scope: Latin Fathers and early medieval Latin authors (Tertullian, Cyprian, Novatian, Lactantius, Hilary, Ambrose, Jerome, Augustine, Rufinus, Cassian, Vincent of Lérins, Leo the Great, Prosper, Peter Chrysologus, Caesarius, Gregory the Great, Isidore, Bede, Gelasius, Optatus). Latin originals, English and Spanish translations.

Method: license/terms pages fetched directly (curl / real Chrome over CDP); GitHub API + sparse clones of Open Greek and Latin (OGL) repos to catalogue coverage; archive.org / Google Books / HathiTrust / BNE / BVMC searches for Spanish PD translations; Stanford renewal DB / HathiTrust rights for 1940s–60s English translations; hands-on OCR (tesseract spa / spa_old) on PD Spanish scans. Samples in `samples/latin-fathers/`.

### OCR quality evaluation (done hands-on)
- **Zeballos Confesiones 1793** (ucm.532026603x, p. 60 = Conf. VII.6): tesseract 5 vs. hand-made ground truth (`gt_z1793_60.txt`). Best: `spa+lat`, raw image, `--psm 4` → word error ~11 %, character similarity 0.972; `spa` alone ~16 % WER; `spa_old` worse (20–25 %). Most errors: running head, line-end hyphenation, long-s / "i" conjunction, accents on "à". Script: `samples/latin-fathers/ocr_eval.py`. → Usable with LLM-assisted post-correction + one human proofreading pass (M effort per volume).
- **Díaz de Beyral Ciudad de Dios 1893** (uc1.$b246568, image 41): `spa --psm 4` → visually near-perfect (1 error "gu"→"su" in ~350 words). → S/M effort for the whole City of God (4 vols).
- 16th-c. gothic (Moralia 1527, Diálogos 1532): not tested; expect poor tesseract results; would need Transkribus-type HTR or re-translation → treat as L.

## 5. Creative alternatives / workarounds
1. **Latin first, own translation second.** OGL CSEL/PL (CC BY-SA 4.0, verified) covers nearly everything; AI-assisted, human-reviewed Spanish (and English-gap) translations made directly from these Latin texts, labelled "Traducción Doctrina", are fully ours. BY-SA obligation applies only to the Latin text file we redistribute (attribute OGL/Leipzig + share-alike that file); our translation is a separate work — but note: a translation *of* a BY-SA text could be argued to be a derivative of the edition if it relies on its editorial choices; for PD editions (CSEL 1866–1925, Migne) the underlying edition is PD, so translate "from CSEL 33 (Knöll 1896, PD)" and cite OGL only as the digital source (legal reasoning, not lawyer-verified).
2. **18th-c. Spanish translations via OCR + modernization layer.** Keep the scan-faithful text (PD) and publish a modernized-orthography layer as our edit. OCR pipeline proven on Zeballos 1793 (≈11 % WER raw, mostly fixable mechanically) and Díaz de Beyral 1893 (near-perfect).
3. **Reuse volunteer work:** es.wikisource City of God (complete); en.wikisource Outler Confessions; Tertullian Project ("copy freely"); SermonIndex alignment. Contribute corrections back (goodwill, provenance trail).
4. **Scan-backed English:** archive.org NPNF/ANF/LF/Thompson scans + djvu OCR → align to SermonIndex or Wikisource text to get a CCEL-independent provenance chain; only ask CCEL if we want their markup.
5. **US non-renewal (LCC 1953–57):** Outler 1955 is the cleanest find; the British LCC translators (Burleigh, Burnaby, Greenslade) need a URAA/foreign-term legal check before use outside the US.
6. **Permission targets (if a modern translation is wanted):** BAC — Biblioteca de Autores Cristianos (Obras completas de San Agustín, Spanish); Editorial Ciudad Nueva (Fuentes Patrísticas, Biblioteca de Patrística); Città Nuova / NBA (augustinus.it); CUA Press (FOTC); Paulist Press (ACW); New City Press / Augustinian Heritage Institute (WSA). Draft request (to adapt): "Doctrina is a bilingual study site on Christian doctrine (validation stage, small paid tier planned). Could you tell us whether a licence is possible and on what terms (scope: web display, territories AR/ES/LatAm/US, duration)?" — Pedro sends, not the agent.
7. **Quotation right** (AR Ley 11.723 art. 10, ≤1,000 words; ES LPI art. 32) only for short cited passages of modern translations inside doctrine pages — never for the full reader.
