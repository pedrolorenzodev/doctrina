## 3. Spanish Westminster (Monterrey 1880)

- Title page (OCR, archive.org `doctrinasyreglam00west`): «DOCTRINAS Y REGLAMENTOS de la Iglesia Presbiteriana, formulados por la Asamblea [two words garbled] … conteniendo la confesión de fe, Forma de Gobierno, Disciplina, Directorio de culto y Reglas para los tribunales; traducidos del inglés por H. C. Thomson, y el Catecismo menor. MONTEREY. Tipografía del Comercio. A. Lagrange y Hno. 1880» `[verified: title page image read by verifier V7, leaf n2]`; the registry's «Monterrey» is a modernised spelling of the printed «Monterey». The registry's `wcf.es` row (l. 135) says «Monterrey, Tipografía del Comercio, 1880».
- **Registry error to fix:** `wcat.en` (editions.ts l. 1036) says «El volumen de Monterrey 1880 solo trae la Confesión». The title page announces «y el Catecismo menor» and the OCR has «Catecismo menor» headings from line 7705 on; session A verified RWSC97's Spanish on a page image of this volume. So the volume holds the Confession **and the Shorter Catechism** (the Larger Catechism is not in it; the Larger Catechism's Spanish stays AI-assisted). `site-translation.wsc.es` (AI Spanish for the WSC) therefore has a printed Spanish edition available.
- **Which Westminster the Spanish is.** The volume's own opening note on the Assembly says the documents of the Assembly (confession, catechisms, directory, form of government), «con algunas alteraciones pertenecientes al gobierno civil», form the constitution (the chapter XXXI and 25.6 findings below were not re-checked by V7) of the Presbyterian Church in the United States (OCR of the page that follows the title page). That matches what A3 found in chapter XXXI: the Spanish has no clause about magistrates calling synods (Edinburgh 1894 WCF 31.2). So the Spanish is the **American-revised** text and its numbering of ch. XXXI differs from the Edinburgh 1894 English the registry's English row cites (`02-import-spec.md` §0.2). Edition lines should say so; whether other chapters (20.4, 23.3, 25.6) differ was not checked here. 25.6 of this Spanish still contains the Pope clause (read: «Ni puede el papa de Roma ser la cabeza de ella en ningun sentido…»).
