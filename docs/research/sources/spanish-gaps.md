## 3. Creative alternatives found
1. **ULB Halle / VD17 route** for 16th–17th c. Spanish Protestant prints printed in Germany (PDM licence, IIIF, ALTO): found Reina 1601. VD16/VD17 + STCN are the right catalogues for exile Spanish imprints (Frankfurt, Kassel, Leiden, Amsterdam).
2. **NUC pre-1956 (full view on HathiTrust) as a finder** of forgotten Spanish mission translations (Wesley 1891, Cobián 1930, Stump/Caban 1935, Gutiérrez-Marín 1945): in-volume search "Spanish" / "Sermones" / "Catecismo".
3. **Methodist *Disciplines* and *Literatura escogida* (1916/1928)** reveal which Spanish evangelical books existed (course-of-study lists) → targets for scanning.
4. **Search-only HathiTrust volumes still answer "is X inside?"**: the in-volume search payload returns hit counts even when access is denied (used on Izrastzoff 1904).
5. **Public-domain scan requests**: SMU Bridwell, Emory Pitts, PTS, LoC, Leiden UB digitise PD holdings on request; a US pre-1931 imprint scanned by a US library is free to use in the US. This is the lever for Wesley 1891, Swensson/Cobián catechisms, Aventrot 1628, the PTS 1896 *Constitución*.
6. **Cheap clarification beats translation**: the Misión Luterana PR already shows CC BY logos; one written sentence from them would give a complete Spanish Book of Concord.
