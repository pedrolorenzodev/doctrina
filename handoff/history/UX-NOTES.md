# UX notes — owner's thoughts on the UI/UX proposals

_Raw, unordered notes from the owner while reviewing the proposals canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/1emZWrzgH7utqFHpri1R3C). Record first, order later. Not decisions unless marked **Decided**._

## 2026-10-05

**Timeline (T1, horizontal)**
- **Decided:** keep T1, the horizontal timeline. The owner loved it.
- Drop the zoom tabs ("Dos mil años", "Primeros siglos", "Siglos IV y V"). Use ONE density, close to the "Siglos IV y V" view: readable names, items laid out as they are there.
- To see the whole timeline, the reader **scrolls the timeline component horizontally** (left/right). The page itself does not scroll sideways.
- Consequence: the dedicated timeline page needs its header, footer and surrounding layout adjusted to fit a horizontally scrolling component (e.g. full-bleed, full-height layout).

**Search (⌘K with "Recientes" and "Seguir donde dejaste")**
- **Decided:** try it. The concept and the UI are liked.

**Doctrine landing page**
- Of the four, **B (portada y fichas)** is the least disliked, but it is not yet the best answer for what information we offer and what we prioritize. Needs discussion.
- "Las objeciones más discutidas" is confusing: it mixes objections to different traditions, so it's unclear whose objection it is, and the reader may not care about, say, objections to the Lutheran position on that screen. Rethink how objections are surfaced on the landing page.
- "Los primeros testigos" feels too basic and thin in its current form; it would add value if framed differently.

**Section page ("ficha") and overall structure**
- Not convinced yet by the B section page, nor by the overall structure.
